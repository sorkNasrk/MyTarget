            RESULTS OF THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
                     HELD ON AUGUST 7, 2026
 The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of Alibaba Health Information
 Technology Limited (the “Company”) is pleased to announce that, on August 7, 2026,
 the Company held its annual general meeting (the “AGM”) at which all the resolutions
 set out in the notice of annual general meeting dated July 6, 2026 (the “Notice”) were
 duly passed by the shareholders of the Company (the “Shareholders”) by way of poll.


References are made to (i) the Notice; and (ii) the circular of the Company dated July 6,
2026 (the “Circular”). Capitalized terms used herein shall have the same meanings as
defined in the Circular unless the context requires otherwise.
