The basis of determination of the vesting period
The vesting of the Proposed Grant spans over 3 years from the
date of the grant, with the first vesting on the first anniversary
of the date of grant, the Board and the Remuneration Committee
are of the view that such vesting period is relatively extensive,
and in line with the grants made by comparable companies in
the same industry to their employees (including directors), the
vesting periods of which range from 1 to 4 years. Further, in
determining the vesting period, the Board and the Remuneration
Committee aim to (i) encourage retention beyond the initial
vesting as Mr. Yu is incentivised to stay with the Company for
an extended period to fully benefit from his equity, aligning his
interests with the Company’s long-term success; (ii) provide
sufficient incentive to retain and motivate Mr. Yu to participate
in the formulation of strategy and long-term development of the
Company; and (iii) ensure that Mr. Yu is committed to the
Company’s performance over time as the vesting period
reinforces the notion that he should focus on the Company’s
long-term growth rather than short-term gains. Based on the
above, the Board and the Remuneration Committee are of the
view that the vesting period for the Proposed Grant is fair and
reasonable and in the interest of the Company and its
Shareholders as a whole.
Other key
restrictions:
Performance targets
With respect to Mr. Yu, upon each vesting date, the portion of
Share Awards eligible to vest will actually vest if Mr. Yu meets
the specified threshold in his performance evaluations during
the one-year period prior to the vesting date. The Board may
determine whether any eligible but not actually vested Share
Awards may be rolled over and vest on a subsequent date during
the vesting period, based on factors including the overall
financial position and operating conditions of the Group on each
vesting date, with a focus on the growth and reputation of the
Group and its industry ranking, and benchmarking of the
Company’s key performance metrics against comparable
companies in the same industry or with a listing on the Hong
Kong or a comparable stock exchange. These key performance
metrics include but are not limited to, the growth rate of the
gross merchandise value and the revenue of the Company for the
relevant periods, the quality control of the private label products
as the Company launched more diversified products with
premium quality and good value for money, the new business
opportunities and resources that Mr. Yu could bring to the
Group, and the performance and variety of Mr. Yu’s live￾streaming sessions.

period of 3 years. Such grant is akin to payment of a deferred bonus and hence an effective
incentive. In addition, the Proposed Grant will further align the interests of Mr. Yu and the
long-term interests of the Shareholders, ensuring a better linkage between the Company’s
long-term share price performance and Mr. Yu’s executive compensation. Therefore, the Board
proposed to remunerate Mr. Yu with the Proposed Grant after considering the benefits of
granting the Share Awards.

encourage and retain Mr. Yu to make contributions to the long-term growth and profits of the
Group, which aligns with the purpose of the 2023 Share Scheme and effectively promotes the
long-term growth and success of the Group.
Having considered the foregoing, the Board (including independent non-executive
Directors, but excluding Mr. Yu) and the Remuneration Committee are of the view that the
Proposed Grant is in the best interests of the Shareholders.
