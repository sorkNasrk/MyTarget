2.   CHANGE IN USE OF PROCEEDS FROM THE RMB SHARE ISSUE

An ordinary resolution will be proposed at the EGM to consider and approve the change in use of
proceeds from the RMB Share Issue and the Wuxi JV III Investment Project.

Reference is made to the Company’s announcements dated 21 March 2022, 12 May 2022, 27 June
2022, 4 November 2022, 30 January 2023, 13 April 2023, 10 May 2023, 17 May 2023, 28 May
2023, 7 June 2023, 28 June 2023, 5 July 2023, 14 July 2023, 18 July 2023, 21 July 2023, 24 July
2023, 26 July 2023, 27 July 2023, 31 July 2023 and 4 August 2023, and the Company’s circulars
dated 7 June 2022, 5 June 2023 and 29 June 2023 in relation to, among others, the RMB Share
Issue.

The Company completed the initial public offering of RMB Shares and listing on the STAR Market
of the Shanghai Stock Exchange on 7 August 2023, with total proceeds raised being RMB21,203.00
million. After deducting offering expenses of RMB282.32 million, the net proceeds from the RMB
Share Issue were RMB20,920.67 million (the “Net Proceeds”). The Net Proceeds were proposed
to be used for the “Hua Hong Manufacturing (Wuxi) Project”, the “8-Inch Factory Optimisation
and Upgrading Project”, the “Specialty Technological Innovation and Research and Development
Project” (together, the “Original Projects”) and replenishment of working capital. The total
investment amount in the Original Projects was set to be RMB18,000.00 million, resulting in
surplus proceeds of RMB2,920.68 million from the RMB Share Issue (the “Surplus”).

As at 14 August 2026, the Surplus remained unused. The Company proposes to apply approximately
RMB3,030.56 million of surplus proceeds from the RMB Share Issue (including accumulated
interest income, net of handling fees and other charges) together with approximately RMB2,525.41
million of remaining proceeds from the concluded 8-Inch Factory Optimisation and Upgrading
Project and Specialty Technological Innovation and Research and Development Project (including
accumulated interest income, net of handling fees and other charges), to invest in and construct the
Wuxi JV III Investment Project. The actual amount to be applied shall be based on the balance of
the relevant designated account(s) on the date of fund transfer.

Reference is also made to the Company’s announcement dated 1 September 2026 in respect of the
Wuxi JV III Investment Project.

b.   Necessity of the Wuxi JV III Investment Project

The Company’s Wuxi base has completed the construction of two 12-inch wafer fabrication
lines and has established a comprehensive operating system covering management talent, five
major specialty process platforms including specialty IC and power devices, basic research and
development, and manufacturing operations. To further consolidate the Company’s market position
in advanced specialty process technologies, enhance its technological capabilities, strengthen its
core competitiveness and meet domestic substitution demand and demand arising from emerging
application fields, the Company proposes to implement the Wuxi JV III Investment Project to
expand capacity and upgrade technology.

c.   Feasibility of the Wuxi JV III Investment Project

The Wuxi JV III Investment Project has strong execution feasibility. First, the project enjoys policy
support and has obtained guidance and approval from the relevant national industry authorities
together with supporting policies from local governments. Second, in terms of technology and
process platforms, supported by the listed status and industry position of the Company and its deep
accumulation in specialty processes, the Company has strong competitive advantages in relevant
technology fields. Third, in terms of funding and capital support, with the support of national and
local investment funds in the integrated circuit sector, the project has secured solid funding support,
and the relevant capital expenditure and working capital are fully supported. Fourth, in terms of
market demand and economic benefits, with the increasing demand for domestic semiconductor
production, the project is expected to enjoy favourable market demand and profitability upon
reaching full production, and will effectively promote the development of the local industry chain
and become an important component of the domestic advanced specialty process sector.

d.   Risk Analysis of the Wuxi JV III Investment Project

The Company believes that the principal risks relating to project financing, talent and technology,
product market demand and operational management remain controllable and that the project
possesses a strong ability to withstand risks. The Company will continue to strengthen internal
management, reduce costs, maintain technological leadership, develop new processes and products
and actively expand market opportunities. It will continue to grasp market development trends
through advanced technologies and, in particular, maintain the product sales plan forecast during
the calculation period, so as to secure a high level of operational safety for the project and mitigate
operational risks.

e.   Benefits of the Wuxi JV III Investment Project

The Wuxi JV III Investment Project constitutes an important strategic investment by the Company
to further expand its production capacity, enhance the advancement of its technology platforms
and product capabilities and consolidate its industry leadership. Based on the investment cycle
of the project and the financial return calculations, the project is expected to make a significant
contribution to the profitability of the Company after the major depreciation period of the project
equipment, and therefore possesses sound economic benefits.

f.   Measures for the Management of the Wuxi JV III Funds

Upon obtaining all the relevant approvals required for the Wuxi JV III Investment Project, the Wuxi
JV III will, as the case may be, set up designated bank account(s) specifically for depositing the
Wuxi JV III Funds and enter into designated account supervision agreement(s) with the Company,
the Sponsors and the commercial bank(s) with which the Wuxi JV III Funds is deposited. The
Board of Directors has authorised the Company’s management to handle any matters regarding
the opening of such designated bank accounts and the execution of such supervision agreements in
relation to the Wuxi JV III Funds.

The Company will utilise the Wuxi JV III Funds in accordance with the implementation progress of
the Wuxi JV III Investment Project and maintain separate book records accordingly. The Company
will allocate the Wuxi JV III Funds to the Wuxi JV III in batches through capital injection or other
means with due regard to the implementation progress of the Wuxi JV III Investment Project.
The specific amount, timing and other matters relating to each allocation, as well as other specific
matters relating to the Wuxi JV III Investment Project, will be determined by the Company’s
management as authorised by the Board of Directors.
