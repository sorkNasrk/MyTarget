 CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION

 The Board hereby announces that, on August 29, 2025 (after trading hours), ZA
 Global entered into the Share Purchase Agreement with ZhongAn Technology,
 Z Fin, Warrior, Opportunities Fund, Cosmos, OKG and Northstar, pursuant to
 which, (i) ZhongAn Technology conditionally agreed to subscribe for, and ZA
 Global conditionally agreed to issue and allot, a maximum of 135,423,860 ZA
 Global Ordinary Shares at a purchase price of US$0.4225 per ZA Global Ordinary
 Share; and (ii) conditional upon and concurrently with the completion of the
 Subscription, the anti-dilution mechanism under the Previous Shareholders
 Agreement is triggered given the purchase price per ZA Global Ordinary Share
 under the Subscription is lower than the adjusted purchase price under the
 Previous Shareholders Agreement and 3,739,800, 4,313,294, 199,908 and 999,541
 ZA Global Ordinary Shares will be issued to ZhongAn Technology, Z Fin, Warrior
 and Opportunities Fund at nil consideration.

Subscription price and basis of consideration

The subscription price for the Subscription is approximately US$57.22 million in
aggregate, which represents a purchase price of US$0.4225 per ZA Global Ordinary
Share and was determined after arm’s length negotiations among the parties taking
into account market conditions, the business and growth prospects of ZA Global and
the financial needs of ZA Global for the operation and expansion of its business. In
particular, part of the total subscription price is expected to contribute towards
supporting the development of digital banking business in Hong Kong, including
research and development of new products and functions in wealth management and
building Web 3.0 banking infrastructure, with the remainder to be used for general
corporate purposes of ZA Global. The subscription price payable to ZA Global by
ZhongAn Technology will be funded by its internal resources.

THE SHAREHOLDERS AGREEMENT

The Share Purchase Agreement provides that the execution and delivery of the
Shareholders Agreement is a condition precedent to the Closing, a summary of which
is set out below.

Board composition of ZA Global

The board of directors of ZA Global shall consist of no more than five directors.
ZhongAn Technology shall be entitled to nominate three directors and Z Fin shall be
entitled to nominate up to two directors.

Resolutions of a meeting of the board of directors of ZA Global shall be adopted by
the unanimous consent of those present and voting in such meeting, and each director
shall have one vote.

Pre-emptive right

If ZA Global issues any new equity interests, each of the ZA Global shareholders
shall have a pre-emptive right to purchase pro rata all or any part of such new equity
interests at the same price and upon the same non-price terms specified in the
participation notice.

Restrictions on transfer

Except as permitted under the Shareholders Agreement, none of the ZA Global
shareholders shall itself, nor shall it permit its affiliates to sell, assign, transfer,
encumber, hypothecate, pledge or otherwise alienate or dispose of, directly or
indirectly (the “Transfer”) all or any part of its ZA Global Ordinary Shares without
the prior written consent of ZA Global.

Rights of first refusal

If any ZA Global shareholder proposes to Transfer all or any part of its ZA Global
Ordinary Shares or any interest therein to one or more third parties, then all other ZA
Global shareholders shall have a right of first refusal to purchase pro rata such ZA
Global Ordinary Shares in accordance with the Shareholders Agreement.


                                        − 6 −
Co-sale right

In the event that any of ZhongAn Technology and/or Z Fin proposes to effect a
Transfer of its ZA Global Ordinary Shares (the “Selling Shareholder”) subject to the
Investors’ co-sale rights and to the extent the Investors do not exercise their
respective rights of first refusal pursuant to the Shareholders Agreement, the
Investors shall have the right to participate in the sale of equity interest in ZA Global
to a third party by the Selling Shareholder for the same price and on the same terms
and conditions as those offered by the Selling Shareholder.

Anti-dilution

In the event that at any time after the date of the Shareholders Agreement, ZA Global
issues any new securities (including any ZA Global Ordinary Shares issued in an
initial public offering of ZA Global, but not including the ZA Global Ordinary Shares
subscribed under the Share Purchase Agreement) without consideration or for a
consideration per ZA Global Ordinary Share received by ZA Global (net of any
selling concessions, discounts or commissions) that is less than the original purchase
price at which the Investors subscribed for its ZA Global Ordinary Shares (the
“Original Purchase Price”), the Original Purchase Price shall be adjusted in
accordance with the Shareholders Agreement and ZA Global shall issue additional
number of ZA Global Ordinary Shares to the Investors (without any cost on such
Investor) that the Investors should receive upon such adjustment.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE SUBSCRIPTION

The capital contribution under the Subscription will provide additional working
capital and greater financial flexibility to ZA Global which will further facilitate its
business agenda of expanding the digital banking business, including research and
development of new products and functions in wealth management and building Web
3.0 banking infrastructure.

The Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors) are of the view
that the Subscription has been entered into on normal commercial terms, and the
terms of the Subscription and the transactions contemplated thereunder are fair and
reasonable and in the interests of the Company and the Shareholders as a whole.

Mr. Yaping Ou, being a Director of the Company and a controlling shareholder of
Z Fin, Mr. Hugo Jin Yi Ou, being a Director of the Company and a director of Z Fin,
and Mr. Stanley Chiu Fai Choi, a Director of the Company who holds 100% interest
in Dynamic Figure Limited, the general partner of Cosmos, had abstained from
voting on the relevant resolution of the Board in relation to the Subscription and the
matters contemplated therein.
