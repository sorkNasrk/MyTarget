2.    PROPOSAL FOR ADOPTION OF THE NEW SHARE OPTION SCHEME

(1)   The 2015 Share Option Scheme

     The 2015 Share Option Scheme was adopted by the Company pursuant to resolutions of
the Shareholders passed on 1 September 2015 and expired on 31 August 2022. No further
Options can be granted thereunder after the expiry date.

     As at the date of this circular, the 2015 Options, the 2018 Options and the 2019 March
Options had lapsed or otherwise ceased to be exercisable. 67,000 options remain outstanding
under the 2019 December Options, which will expire on 22 December 2026.

(2)   Adoption of the New Share Option Scheme

      In view of the expiry of the 2015 Share Option Scheme and the upcoming expiry of the
2019 December Options, the Company considers that the adoption of the New Share Option
Scheme is in the interests of the Company and the Shareholders as a whole. Accordingly, the
Board proposes to seek approval from the Shareholders by way of an ordinary resolution at the
EGM for the adoption of the New Share Option Scheme in accordance with Chapter 17 of the
Listing Rules.

      A summary of the principal terms of the New Share Option Scheme is set out in Appendix
I to this circular. A copy of the New Share Option Scheme is available for inspection at the
registered office of the Company at Room 2212, Bank of America Tower, 12 Harcourt Road,
Central, Hong Kong during normal business hours from the date hereof up to and including the
date of the EGM.

(i)   Purposes

     The purpose of the New Share Option Scheme is to attract, retain and provide incentives
to the Eligible Participants, to provide them with the opportunity to obtain Shares of the
Company and to link their interests closely to the operating results and share performance of
the Company with the view to increasing the value of the Company.

     The New Share Option Scheme contains provisions relating to performance targets that
need to be achieved before an Option can be exercised and a minimum period for which an
Option must be held before the Option can be exercised. The Directors believe that such
conditions will serve to incentivize Eligible Participants to use their best endeavors in assisting
the growth and development of the Group, as well as to provide the appropriate incentives for
Eligible Participants to remain as eligible participants, thereby enabling the Group to continue
to benefit from the services and contributions of the Eligible Participants.

      The New Share Option Scheme requires Options to be held for a minimum vesting period
of two (2) years from the Grant Date before they may vest or be exercised, unless a shorter
vesting period is specified in the relevant Grant Notice and is permitted under the Listing
Rules. Subject to satisfaction of the applicable vesting conditions, Options will vest in tranches
in the proportions and on the dates specified in the relevant Grant Notice. The performance
conditions may comprise corporate and individual measures. Corporate measures include
revenue- and profit-related indicators, which will be assessed against the target levels specified
in the relevant Grant Notice and, where applicable, by reference to the Company’s historical
performance and peer-company benchmarks. Individual performance is assessed under the
Company’s performance appraisal system for the financial year preceding the relevant vesting
date, using internal grading with coefficients that may range from 100% to 0% to determine the
vesting proportion for the relevant tranche. For the 2026 Options, the minimum holding period
and the vesting schedule are described under “Grant of the 2026 Options” below and in
Appendix II.

(ii) Eligible Participants

      Eligible Participants of the New Share Option Scheme shall be limited to employees of
the Group, including (1) existing executive and non-executive Directors of any member of the
Group; (2) senior management and key managerial and technical personnel of the Group having
a direct impact on the results of operations and sustainable development of any member of the
Group; and (3) other key employees of the Group who have made, or are expected to make,
significant contributions to the business development, operational performance or long-term
growth of any member of the Group; subject always to any limits and restrictions specified in
the New Share Option Scheme, but shall not include any Independent Non-Executive
Directors;

(iii) Scheme Mandate Limit

     As of the Latest Practicable Date, there were 1,928,494,794 Shares in issue. Assuming
there is no change to the issued share capital of the Company in the period commencing from
the Latest Practicable Date to the date of the EGM, the total number of Shares that may be
issued under the New Share Option Scheme and any other schemes of the Company will be
192,849,479 Shares, representing approximately 10% of the issued share capital of the
Company as at the date of the EGM.

(iv) Duration

    The New Share Option Scheme shall be valid and effective for a period of 10 years
commencing on the Adoption Date.

(v)   Vesting Period

     No Options granted under the New Share Option Scheme shall vest until the expiry of a
two (2)-year vesting period from the date of grant. Subject to the satisfaction of the applicable
vesting conditions, the Options shall vest in tranches.

     Any vested but unexercised Options, and any Options which have not vested, shall lapse
and cease to be exercisable upon the seventh anniversary of the date of grant (or such earlier
date as may be specified in accordance with the terms of the New Share Option Scheme).

(vi) Performance Targets and Clawback Mechanism

     The grant and vesting of Options under the New Share Option Scheme shall be subject
to applicable laws and regulations and shall be conditional upon the satisfaction of the
performance targets of the Company and the individual performance targets of the relevant
Grantees. Corporate performance measures will include revenue- and profit-related indicators.
For each vesting tranche, the relevant target levels for such corporate measures will be assessed
by reference to the Company’s audited results for the financial year preceding the relevant
vesting date and, where applicable, by reference to the Company’s historical performance and
peer-company benchmarks. Each Grantee must also pass the individual performance
assessment conducted under the Company’s performance appraisal system for the financial
year preceding the Grant. Vesting proportions will be determined by reference to internal
performance grading with coefficients that may range from 100% to 0%. Any portion of a
tranche that does not vest because the applicable corporate or individual performance
conditions are not satisfied will be forfeited.

     The Board may, in its absolute discretion, determine that any unvested or unexercised
Options granted to a Grantee shall lapse, or be clawed back in whole or in part and upon such
clawback, lapse if the Grantee commits misconduct, breaches material employment, service,
confidentiality, non-competition or non-solicitation obligations, discloses trade secrets or
confidential information, competes with the Group, causes material loss or reputational
damage to the Group, is convicted of a criminal offence, breaches applicable laws or internal
policies, or ceases to be an Eligible Participant in circumstances specified in the New Share
Option Scheme or the relevant Grant Notice.

(vii) Subscription Price

      The subscription price for the Shares under the New Share Option Scheme shall be a price
solely determined by the Board and notified to a grantee and shall be at least the higher of (i)
the closing price of the Shares as stated in the Stock Exchange’s daily quotations sheet on the
date on which an Option is granted; (ii) the average closing price of the Shares as stated in the
Stock Exchange’s daily quotations sheets for the 5 trading days immediately preceding the date
on which an Option is granted; and (iii) the face value of the Share.

Rules because the Company constitutes a state-controlled overseas listed company.
Accordingly, the 2026 Options shall only take effect subject to and conditional upon the
passing of an ordinary resolution to approve the grant of the 2026 Options by the Shareholders.

      The 2026 Options are expected to be granted within 12 months after the date of the EGM.

state-controlled overseas listed company. As the identity and number of Grantees, the number
of 2026 Options to be granted to each Grantee and the Exercise Price have not yet been
finalised, the approval sought at the EGM is not intended to constitute approval of any grant
which is subject to separate Shareholders’ approval under Chapter 17 of the Listing Rules.

      As at the Latest Practicable Date, none of the proposed Grantees is a Director of the
Company and the Company does not expect any grant of the 2026 Options to any individual
Grantee to exceed any threshold requiring separate Shareholders’ approval under Chapter 17 of
the Listing Rules. A proposed Grantee may become a Director of the Company prior to the
grant of the 2026 Options. The Company confirms that such change in identity will not, by
itself, trigger any requirement for separate Shareholders’ approval under Chapter 17 of the
Listing Rules, and the Company will comply with the applicable requirements under Chapter
17 of the Listing Rules, including making further announcement(s) and disclosure as may be
required. As soon as possible upon the grant of the 2026 Options, the Company will publish
an announcement in accordance with Rule 17.06A of the Listing Rules. Such announcement
will contain the information required under Rule 17.06B in tabular form, with disclosure on an
individual basis in respect of the Grantees falling within Rule 17.06A(2). The announcement
will also disclose the number of Shares available for future grant under the Scheme Mandate
Limit in accordance with Rule 17.06C of the Listing Rules. The Company expects that none
of the proposed grants will be subject to separate Shareholders’ approval under Chapter 17 of
the Listing Rules. Accordingly, no further circular is expected to be required in connection with
the grant of the 2026 Options, and the Company will publish an announcement upon grant in
