UPDATE ON PAYMENT OF FINAL DIVIDEND
References are made to the announcement of annual results for the year ended 31
December 2025 dated 26 March 2026, the circular of 2025 Annual General Meeting dated
3 June 2026 and the announcement dated 24 June 2026 (the ‘‘Announcement’’) of Haier
Smart Home Co., Ltd. (the ‘‘Company’’) in relation to, among other things, payment of
final dividend, where the Company intended to distribute a cash final dividend for the
year ended 31 December 2025 of RMB8.867 (tax inclusive) per 10 shares. Where the total
share capital of the Company changes before the registration date for the implementation
of the equity distribution, it is expected to maintain the total distribution unchanged and
adjust the distribution ratio per share accordingly.
Due to the Company’s arrangements including the repurchase of shares and the transfer
of shares to the Employee Stock Ownership Plan of the Company, the shares entitling
dividends in the Company’s share capital would change before the registration date (7
August 2026), the Company intends to maintain the total distribution (approximately
RMB8,248 million (tax inclusive)) unchanged and adjust the distribution ratio per share
from RMB8.867 (tax inclusive) per 10 shares to RMB8.9151 (tax inclusive) per 10 shares
accordingly. H shareholders whose names appear on the H share register of members of
the Company on Friday, 7 August 2026 are entitled to receive such dividend. Such
dividend will be denominated and announced in RMB, of which A shareholders will be
paid in RMB, H shareholders will be paid in HK dollars and D shareholders will be paid
in Euros. The actual amount of HK dollars to be paid to H shareholders is calculated at
the average benchmark exchange rate of RMB against HK dollars (i.e. RMB0.8695025 =
HK$1.00) published by the People’s Bank of China for a week prior to the announcement
of dividend and payment decision (i.e. 24 June 2026). Accordingly, the cash dividend per
10 H shares is HK$10.253105 (tax inclusive). The actual amount of Euros to be paid to D
shareholders is calculated at the average benchmark exchange rate of RMB against Euros
