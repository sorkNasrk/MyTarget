                  PAYMENT DATE OF SPECIAL CASH DIVIDEND

Reference is made to (i) the joint announcement of eLong, Inc. (the “Offeror”), Tongcheng
Travel Holdings Limited (                            ) (the “Offeror Parent”) and Dida Inc.
(“Dida” or the “Company”) dated June 29, 2026 in relation to, among other things, the Dida
Offer and the proposed declaration and payment of a special cash dividend by the Company;
(ii) the announcement of the Company dated July 6, 2026 in relation to the declaration of the
Special Cash Dividend and the Record Date; (iii) the composite document jointly issued by
the Offeror, the Offeror Parent and the Company dated July 31, 2026 in relation to the Dida
Offer; and (iv) the joint announcement of the Offeror, the Offeror Parent and the Company
dated August 5, 2026 in relation to, among other things, the Dida Offer having become
unconditional in all respects (collectively, the “Announcements”). Unless otherwise defined,
capitalised terms used in this announcement shall have the same meanings as those defined in
the Announcements.

The board of directors (the “Board”) of the Company is pleased to announce that all the
Conditions have been satisfied and the Dida Offer has become unconditional in all respects.
Accordingly, the Special Cash Dividend of HK$1.1745 per Share will be paid on August
31, 2026 to the Shareholders whose names appeared on the register of members of the
Company on Monday, July 20, 2026, being the Record Date for determining the entitlement
of Shareholders to the Special Cash Dividend. All treasury shares and repurchased shares
pending cancellation, if any, will not receive the Special Cash Dividend.

Shareholders entitled to the Special Cash Dividend will receive the Special Cash Dividend in
accordance with the arrangements set out in the Announcements.

The payment of the Special Cash Dividend does not involve any reduction in the authorised
or issued share capital of the Company, any reduction in the par value of the Shares or any
change in the trading arrangements in respect of the Shares.
