III. INTERIM PROFIT DISTRIBUTION PROPOSAL OF 2026

      A special resolution will be proposed at the EGM to consider and approve the Company’s
interim profit distribution proposal of 2026, particulars of which are as follows:

      Based on the total share capital of 7,458,039,464 Shares, which is derived from the total
share capital of 7,629,418,902 Shares as of the Latest Practicable Date minus the repurchased
Shares (the balance of Shares in the Company’s A Share dedicated repurchase account was
171,279,438 Shares and the H Shares held in treasury were 100,000 shares as of the Latest
Practicable Date), a cash dividend of RMB5 (tax inclusive) per 10 Shares will be distributed
to all Shareholders, representing a total cash dividend distribution of RMB3,729,019,732. No
capital reserve will be converted into share capital. This distribution accounts for 14.10% of
the net profit attributable to the Shareholders.

     The Treasury Shares of the Company will not be entitled to the distribution of dividend.

      In the event of any changes in the total share capital entitled to profit distribution during
the implementation of this profit distribution proposal, the total dividend amount shall be
adjusted based on the total share capital entitled to profit distribution as at the shareholding
registration date, in accordance with the principle that the per-share distribution amount shall
remain unchanged. Dividends for A Shares will be paid in RMB; dividends for H Shares will
be paid in Hong Kong dollars, and the actual amount will be calculated by the average
benchmark exchange rate between RMB and Hong Kong dollars as announced by the People’s
Bank of China for the five business days prior to the date of the EGM.

     Subject to the approval of the interim profit distribution proposal of 2026 at the EGM, the
Company expects to distribute the cash dividend to the Shareholders within two months after
the convening of the EGM. The Company will make further announcement regarding the
dividend distribution arrangements, including the record date, the closure period of the register
of members, the expected payment date of the cash dividend and information relating to the
withholding of income tax.

     The resolution was considered and approved at the eighteenth meeting of the fifth session
of the Board held on 28 August 2026, and is hereby submitted to the EGM for consideration
and approval by way of a special resolution.
