Reason for the update / change
Due to the Company's arrangements including repurchase of shares and the transfer 
of shares to the Employee Stock Ownership Plan of the Company, the shares

RMB 8,248 million (tax inclusive)) unchanged and adjust the distribution ratio per share 
from RMB8.867 (tax inclusive) per 10 shares to RMB 8.9151 (tax inclusive) per 10 
shares accordingly.
