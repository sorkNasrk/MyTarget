                VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT

                    INCENTIVE SCHEME

References are made to the announcements (the “Announcements”) dated August 28, 2024,
September 12, 2024, January 19, 2025, January 24, 2025, March 31, 2025, July 26, 2026
and July 28, 2026 and the circular (the “Circular”) dated August 28, 2024 of UBTECH
ROBOTICS CORP LTD (the “Company”, collectively with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) in
relation to the H share incentive scheme (the “Scheme”). Capitalised terms used herein shall
have the same meanings as those defined in the Announcements and the Circular unless the

On October 8, 2026, the trustee of the Scheme (the “Trustee”) had purchased a total of 80,000
H shares of the Company (the “H Shares”) from the market to hold on trust for the benefit of
the Eligible Participants (as defined in the Scheme) pursuant to the terms and conditions of
the Scheme. Details of the H Shares purchased on October 8, 2026 and held by the Trustee on
trust are as follows:
