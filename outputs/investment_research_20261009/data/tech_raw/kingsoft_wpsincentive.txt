GRANT OF RESTRICTED SHARES UNDER BKOS 2026 SHARE
INCENTIVE SCHEME

The Board announces that on 1 June 2026, the BKOS Board has resolved to grant an aggregate
of 800,000 Restricted Shares to 365 Incentive Participants, comprising a number of senior
management personnel, core management personnel and technical backbones of BKOS, under
the First Grant of the BKOS 2026 Share Incentive Scheme at the grant price of RMB125.58 per
Restricted Share, to recognise the contributions made by the Incentive Participants and to retain
outstanding personnel employed by the BKOS Group.

Performance assessment requirements at the Incentive Participants’ level

BKOS conducts individual performance assessment on the Incentive Participants during the
assessment year and determines the number of Restricted Shares actually vested to the Incentive
Participants based on their assessment results. The performance assessment results of the Incentive
Participants are divided into three levels, namely “Attained”, “Partially attained” and “Not
attained”, and the actual number of Restricted Shares to be vested will be determined according to
the proportion of vesting at the individual level corresponding to the following assessment rating
table:

  Assessment results              Attained                  Partially attained                 Not attained

       Vesting ratio                100%                             80%                             0


                                                      6
The number of Restricted Shares actually vested to the Incentive Participants in the current year
= the number of Restricted Shares planned to be vested to the Incentive Participants in the current
year × Company-level Vesting ratio × Individual-level Vesting ratio.

If the Restricted Shares vested to the Incentive Participants for a vesting period cannot be vested
due to assessment reasons, the Restricted Shares shall lapse and be canceled by BKOS and shall not
be deferred to the following years.

Reserved Grant

As at the date of this announcement, the number of Restricted Shares available for the Reserved
Grant under the BKOS 2026 Share Incentive Scheme is 200,000 Restricted Shares, representing no
more than 20.00% of the total Restricted Shares available under the BKOS 2026 Share Incentive
Scheme.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE FIRST GRANT

The purpose of the First Grant to the Incentive Participants is in line with the purpose of the
BKOS 2026 Share Incentive Scheme, which is to further establish and improve BKOS’s long-term
incentive mechanism, attract and retain outstanding personnel, fully mobilise the enthusiasm of
BKOS’s core team members, effectively align the interests of the BKOS Shareholders and BKOS
with the individual interests of BKOS’s core team members, and enable all parties to jointly focus
on and contribute to the long-term development of the BKOS Group.
