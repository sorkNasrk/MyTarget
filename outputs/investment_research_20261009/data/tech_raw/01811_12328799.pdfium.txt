Announcement 
Trading Update
Power Generation For August 2026
This announcement is made by CGN New Energy Holdings Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) 
on a voluntary basis.
The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company announces 
that, according to the preliminary statistics of the Company, the power generation of 
the Company and its subsidiaries (the “Group”) on a consolidated basis amounted 
to 1,776.3GWh for the month of August 2026, representing an increase of 15.1% 
compared with the corresponding period of 2025. In particular, power generation 
of PRC wind projects increased by 22.1%, power generation of PRC solar projects 
decreased by 2.4%, power generation of PRC gas-fired projects decreased by 6.7%, 
power generation of PRC hydro projects decreased by 16.6% and power generation of 
Korea projects increased by 21.9%. For the eight months ended 31 August 2026, the 
accumulated power generation of the Group amounted to 12,719.2GWh, representing a 
decrease of 0.6% compared with the corresponding period of 2025. In particular, power 
generation of PRC wind projects decreased by 13.5%, power generation of PRC solar 
projects increased by 21.7%, power generation of PRC gas-fired projects decreased 
by 18.9%, power generation of PRC hydro projects increased by 2.1% and power 
generation of Korea projects increased by 13.5%.
