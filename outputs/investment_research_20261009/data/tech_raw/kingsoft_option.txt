                                 GRANT OF SHARE OPTIONS
This announcement is made pursuant to Rules 17.06A, 17.06B and 17.06C of the Rules Governing
the Listing of Securities (the “Listing Rules”) on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the
“Stock Exchange”).

The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of Kingsoft Corporation Limited (the
“Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) announces that on 12 June 2026,
pursuant to the 2024 Share Scheme adopted by way of a resolution passed by the shareholders
of the Company on 23 May 2024 (the “2024 Share Scheme”), the Company has granted to one
eligible participant (the “Grantee”), being an employee of the Group, share options (the “Options”)
carrying the right to subscribe for a total of 250,000 ordinary shares of US$0.0005 each in the share
capital of the Company (the “Shares”), subject to the acceptance by the Grantee.
