(excluding treasury shares) in any 12-month period up to and including the date of such grant.
None of the grant of the Awards is subject to approval by the Shareholders.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE GRANTS

The Company believes that the grant of Awards to the Grantees who are employees of the
Group can help the Company attract and retain outstanding talent and may encourage them to
contribute to the business performance and development of the Group, thereby promoting the
long-term sustainable development of the Company.

Having considered the above, the Board (including all the independent non-executive
Directors) is of the view that the grant of Awards is fair and reasonable and in the interests of
the Company and the Shareholders as a whole.

NUMBER OF H SHARES AVAILABLE FOR FUTURE GRANT

The maximum number of Shares that may be granted under the 2026 Share Award Scheme is
44,584,309 H Shares, representing the Scheme Mandate Limit. Following the grant of Awards
as described above, as of the date of this announcement, an aggregate of 43,361,375 H Shares
will remain available for future grant under the Scheme Mandate Limit, of which 4,458,430
H Shares will remain available for future grant to Service Provider Participants under the
Service Provider Sublimit of 4,458,430 H Shares.
