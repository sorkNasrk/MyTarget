                         INSIDE INFORMATION
              TAXATION MATTERS RELATING TO A SUBSIDIARY
This announcement is made by China Literature Limited (the “Company”, together with its
subsidiaries and consolidated affiliated entities from time to time, collectively the “Group”)
pursuant to Rule 13.09(2) of the Rules Governing the Listing of Securities on The Stock Exchange
of Hong Kong Limited (the “Listing Rules”) and the Inside Information Provisions (as defined in
the Listing Rules) under Part XIVA of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Chapter 571 of the
Laws of Hong Kong).

The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company hereby notifies the
shareholders of the Company (the “Shareholders”) and potential investors that a subsidiary of the
Company (the “Subsidiary”) has conducted a self-review in respect of certain tax risks associated
with its own subsidiaries in Khorgos, Xinjiang, in accordance with a notice from the tax
authorities. Following the self-review and as confirmed with the competent tax authorities, the
Subsidiary shall make a supplementary payment of enterprise income tax and late payment
surcharges for the years 2020 to 2022 (the “Taxes”), totalling approximately RMB300 million.
This matter does not involve any administrative penalties.

The Company attaches great importance to the above matter, and as at the date of this
announcement, the Subsidiary has completed the payment of the Taxes. The Taxes will be
recognised in the Company’s profit or loss for 2026 and are expected to reduce the profit
attributable to equity holders of the Company by approximately RMB300 million. The Company
will account for the above in accordance with the requirements of relevant accounting standards
and the actual circumstances. The final accounting treatment and the amount of the impact shall be
subject to the results confirmed by the Company’s independent auditor.
