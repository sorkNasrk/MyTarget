Vendors; and (iii) the benefits that would be brought to the Group by the Transaction. The Board wishes 
to supplement the following information in relation to the Valuation:
THE VALUATION 
Valuation Approach 
In order to reflect the latest financial performance and capital structure of the Target Companies, the 
Valuer has adopted the trailing enterprise value-to-sales multiple in the Valuation. Enterprise Value to
Earnings Before Interest and Taxes (“EV/EBIT”) multiple is adopted because the Target Companies is 
a mature, stable-growth business with predictable capital-expenditure needs. EV/EBIT multiple is 
suitable for capturing the profitability of companies and reflecting the capital structure of the Target 
Companies.

The Target Companies are principally engaged in the sales of pharmaceutical intermediates and active 
pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) for human drugs in China. Therefore, listed companies with similar 
business exposure in relation to the principal activity of the Target Companies are identified. As a result, 
the comparable companies were selected with reference to the criteria as follows:
1. The comparable companies are principally engaged in the sales of pharmaceutical intermediates 
and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) for human drugs (i.e. more than 70% of the total 
revenue come from the sales of pharmaceutical intermediates and APIs for human drugs in the 
latest financial year);
2. The comparable companies are based and operate in China;
3. The comparable companies are listed on Hong Kong Stock Exchange, Shanghai Stock Exchange 
or Shenzhen Stock Exchange;
4. The comparable companies have at least 3 consecutive years of publicly available audited 
financial statements; and
5. The financial information of the comparable companies is available to the public.
As a result, the Valuer identified 17 guideline public companies to be comparable to the Target Group 
with the aforesaid criteria. These Comparable Companies form an exhaustive list based on the selection 
criteria.

The adoption and basis of the Discount for Lack of Marketability (“DLOM”) is detailed as follows:
The concept of marketability deals with the liquidity of an ownership interest, that is how quickly and 
easily it can be converted to cash if the owner chooses to sell. The lack of marketability discount reflects 
the fact that there is no ready market for shares in privately held companies which are typically not 
readily marketable compared to similar interest in public companies. Therefore, a share of stock in a 
privately held company is usually worth less than an otherwise comparable share in a publicly held 
company.
