Hang Seng TECH Index 
This methodology document should be read in conjunction with the Index Methodology 
General Guide on Hang Seng Indexes Co Ltd (“HSIL”)’s website. 
Readers are reminded that there might be exceptions in the index handling to the below 
general approach in some special situations and HSIL reserves the right to determine 
the most appropriate handling.
Objective To represent the overall performance of 50 Hong 
Kong-listed companies that have high exposures to 
selected technology themes, including sizable
technology companies and technology companies that 
exhibit high sales growth
Universe Constituents of the Hang Seng Composite LargeCap & 
MidCap Index
Eligibility Criteria
Turnover Requirements Velocity Test for Tradable Indexes; and 
3-month Average Daily Turnover >= HKD 100 million 
for non-constituents
Theme Requirements2 High business exposure to at least one of the below 
Tech Themes1
:
• Digital Platforms and Solutions;
• Artificial Intelligence;
• Advanced Hardware;
• Robotics and Automation;
• Cloud; or
• Frontier Technologies
Innovation Screenings2 Meeting at least one of the below criteria:
• Technology-enabled business; or
• R&D Expense to Sales Ratio >=5%; or
• YoY Sales Growth3 >=10%

Hang Seng TECH Index
Constituent Selection
Selection Criteria 50 securities will be selected based on two streams:
Market Value Stream:
The top 40 securities with the highest Market Value
Rank will be selected as constituents
Sales Growth Stream: 
Among the eligible stocks not selected in the Market 
Value Stream, those with sales >= HKD 500 million in 
the latest two fiscal years are eligible for the Sales 
Growth Stream.
The top 10 securities in the Sales Growth Stream with 
the highest YoY Sales Growth Rank3 will be selected 
as constituents
Number of Constituents Fixed at 50
Buffer Zone Market Value Stream:
Existing constituents ranked lower than 48th will be 
removed from the index, while non-constituents ranked 
32nd or above will be included;
Securities will be added or excluded according to their 
Market Value Rank to maintain the number of 
constituents in this stream at 40
Sales Growth Stream:
Existing constituents ranked lower than 15th will be 
removed from the index, while non-constituents ranked 
5
th or above will be included;
Securities will be added or excluded according to the 
Sales Growth Rank3
to maintain the number of 
constituents in this stream at 10
Eligibility for Stock 
Connect Trading
Not Applicable
Review Frequency Quarterly (Data cut-off end-March / June / September / 
December)

Fast Entry A newly listed security that fulfils the Theme 
Requirements and Innovation Screenings will be added 
to index if its full market capitalisation ranks within the 
top 10 of the existing constituents on its first trading day
The ad hoc addition will normally be implemented after 
the close of the 10th trading day of the new issue

Appendix: Relevant Tech Sub-Themes
Tech Themes Tech Sub￾Themes Definitions
Digital 
Platforms and
Solutions
Online Business 
and E-commerce
Companies that operate digital platforms that enable third parties 
(merchants, brands, creators, and publishers) to merchandise, market, 
distribute content, and/or manage customer engagement online by 
providing integrated commerce platforms and access to different digital 
channels.
Software and 
Services
Companies that develop and offer specialised software or online 
application subscriptions and provide application stacks such as 
business software and game software.
Digital Payment Companies that provide digital payment-related services, such as 
mobile payments, payment gateways and/or digital wallets.
Digital Finance
Companies that deliver financial products and services mainly through 
digital channels, such as online-only insurance service providers. 
Exclude banks, exchanges and brokerage firms.
Blockchain
Companies that operate blockchain-based platforms and/or provide 
blockchain technology solutions to enable third parties to build 
applications, issue digital assets, and integrate business process with 
blockchain. Exclude digital asset treasury companies and companies 
that mainly operate as a virtual asset exchange.
Artificial 
Intelligence
Artificial 
Intelligence 
Infrastructure
Companies that provide hardware components and infrastructure that 
enable the development and operation of artificial intelligence platforms 
and applications.
Artificial 
Intelligence 
Platform
Companies that develop artificial intelligence-based foundation models 
and provide artificial intelligence-based platforms, enabling third parties 
to access and build models and deploy AI capabilities in business 
operations. 
Artificial 
Intelligence 
Applications
Companies that deliver AI-driven industry solutions in which AI is a core 
driver of functionality and value creation.
Advanced 
Hardware
Semiconductors
Companies that design and manufacture semiconductors across the 
value chain, such as integrated circuit design, wafer fabrication, 
assembly, packaging and testing, advanced packaging, semiconductor 
manufacturing equipment, and semiconductor materials.
Specialized 
Components and 
Hardware
Companies that design and manufacture electronic components, 
modules and enabling hardware used in advanced computing, 
communications, automotive, and industrial systems, such as optical 
and camera modules, Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) and 
sensing equipment, acoustic components, and Printed Circuit Board 
(PCB)s and Integrated Circuit (IC) substrates.

Appendix: Relevant Tech Sub-Themes
Tech Themes Tech Sub-Themes Definitions
Advanced 
Hardware
Smart Devices and 
Equipment
Companies that design and manufacture smart consumer devices, 
enterprise computing infrastructure such as servers and data storage 
systems, and telecommunications and network equipment used in 
consumer, enterprise, and industrial applications.
New Energy 
Storage and 
Materials
Companies that manufacture advanced energy storage and 
renewable energy hardware, such as Electric Vehicle (EV) battery 
cells and packs, grid-scale energy storage systems, solar 
Photovoltaic (PV) modules and key Photovoltaic (PV) components, 
and specialised battery-grade or Photovoltaic (PV)-grade functional 
materials used directly in battery or Photovoltaic (PV) manufacturing. 
Robotics and
Automation
Robotics 
Companies that design, develop, and provide integrated robotic 
solutions to address operational needs, such as packaged robots or 
configurable robotic systems, combining hardware, software, and AI 
that are capable of autonomous operation and adaptive task 
execution.
Self-Driving 
Companies that develop and commercialise advanced autonomous 
driving technology, including companies that design and manufacture 
advanced core hardware components and specialised software 
platforms to enable autonomous driving.
Automobile manufacturers are required to offer advanced driver 
assistance systems or autonomous driving capabilities across their 
Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) line-up, which accounts for the majority 
of total vehicle sales volume.
Internet of Things
Companies that design and manufacture advanced components or 
provide platform solutions for connected devices that exchange data 
to help businesses improve efficiency and monitoring through online 
connectivity.
Smart Lifestyle
Companies that manufacture smart household appliances and smart 
consumer electronics with automation capability through online 
interaction, data exchange, and device connectivity.

Appendix: Relevant Tech Sub-Themes
Tech Themes Tech Sub-Themes Definitions
Cloud
Cloud Solutions
Companies that develop and provide cloud infrastructure and 
platforms, such as Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS), Platform-as-a￾Service (PaaS), enterprise Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) delivered 
through the cloud computing infrastructure, and cloud-native network 
security services.
Big Data
Companies that develop and provide enterprise data infrastructure 
and analytics platforms, such as database management systems, 
data lakes, data warehouses, data integration and governance tools, 
and AI-driven analytics and decision-intelligence software designed to 
process large-scale data for business use.
Data Centre 
Operation
Companies that operate data-centre infrastructure and provide cloud 
hosting services, such as server colocation, managed hosting, cloud 
interconnection, and infrastructure management for cloud and high￾performance-computing workloads.
Frontier 
Technologies
Aerospace and 
Satellite 
Technology
Companies that design, manufacture, and launch rockets and 
spacecraft for space exploration and/or specialise in satellite 
communication, including companies that design and manufacture 
advanced core hardware components and specialised software 
platforms to enable space exploration.
Quantum 
Computing
Companies that develop, produce, and distribute quantum-computing 
hardware, quantum-computing solutions and/or quantum-computing 
platform services.
Brain–Computer 
Interface
Companies that provide Brain–Computer Interface (BCI) solutions 
that can translate brain signals into commands to achieve desired 
outputs.
Advanced Food 
Technology
Companies that develop and commercialise advanced technology to 
improve and transform food or agricultural production, such as smart 
farming systems, advanced agricultural solutions, synthetic food and 
plant-based meat alternatives.
Advanced 
Materials
Companies that develop and manufacture advanced materials, such 
as biological materials, inorganic materials, and nanomaterials. 
Nanomaterials include the development and application of 
technologies that enable the manipulation, engineering, or use of 
matter at the nanoscale.
