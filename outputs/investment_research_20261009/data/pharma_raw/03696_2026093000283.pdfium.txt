VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
INSILICO MEDICINE NOMINATES NEXT-GENERATION SMALL 
MOLECULE GIPR ANTAGONIST FOR POTENTIAL INTERVENTION OF 
OBESITY AND RELATED DISORDERS
This announcement is made by InSilico Medicine Cayman TopCo (the “Company”, together 
with its subsidiaries, the “Group” or “Insilico Medicine”) on a voluntary basis to inform the 
shareholders and potential investors of the Company about the latest business updates of the 
Group.
The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) is pleased to announce that, driven by the 
proprietary generative AI platform of Insilico Medicine, the Company has nominated ISM1354, 
a potential best-in-class next-generation small molecule Glucose-dependent Insulinotropic 
Polypeptide Receptor (GIPR) antagonist, as a preclinical candidate (PCC) for the potential 
intervention of obesity, as well as obesity-related disorders including type 2 diabetes, with the 
potential to expand into cardiovascular diseases.
In January of this year, the Company announced the nomination of another GIPR antagonist, 
ISM0676, which demonstrated up to a 31.3% weight loss in combination with Semaglutide in 
humanized mouse models. In comparison, the newly nominated PCC not only retains excellent 
drug-like properties but also achieves multi-dimensional optimization across pharmacokinetics 
(PK) and safety profiles.
To date, Insilico Medicine has announced the nomination of ten PCCs in the first nine months 
of 2026, breaking the Company’s historical record for annual pipeline output pushing the total 
number of PCCs nominated since 2021 to 34, thereby validating the R&D efficiency of generative 
AI through reproducible, real-world examples.
