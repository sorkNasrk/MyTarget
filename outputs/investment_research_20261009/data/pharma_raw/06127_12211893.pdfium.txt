II. Explanation Regarding the Adjustments 
1. Reasons for adjustments 
On 4 June 2026, the Company convened its 2025 Annual General Meeting, 
which reviewed and approved a differentiated profit distribution plan. Based on the 
total share capital as at the equity registration date for the 2025 profit distribution, 
excluding shares repurchased and held in the Company's dedicated repurchase 
account, the Company proposes to distribute a cash dividend of RMB0.12 per share 
(tax inclusive) to all shareholders. There will be no capitalization of capital reserves 
into share capital, nor will there be any bonus shares issued. 
Since shares held in the Company's dedicated repurchase account do not 
participate in this profit distribution, the virtual cash dividend, calculated based on the 
actual number of A shares participating in the distribution after the implementation of 
this profit distribution, will amount to RMB0.12 per share. 
In accordance with the provisions of the 2026 Restricted A Share Incentive 
Scheme (Draft), if events such as the capitalization of capital reserves into share 
capital, issuance of bonus shares, share subdivision, rights issue, share consolidation 
or dividend distribution occur between the date of announcement of this Incentive 
Scheme and the completion of restricted shares registration by the participants, the 
grant price of the restricted shares shall be adjusted accordingly. The adjustment 
method is as follows: 
Dividend distribution 
P＝P0-V 
Where: P0 is the grant price before adjustments; V is the dividend amount per 
share; and P is the adjusted grant price. After adjustments for dividend distribution, P 

[PDF PAGE 5]
shall still be greater than 1. 
Therefore, the adjusted grant price for the 2026 restricted A shares= RMB19.17 - 
0.12 = RMB19.05 per share. 
Pursuant to the authorization granted by the Company’s 2025 Annual General 
Meeting, such adjustments fall within the scope of authorization and, following 
consideration and approval by the Board of Directors, do not require further 
submission to the general meeting for review. 
III. Impact of Such Adjustments on the Company 
The adjustments made by the Company to the grant price under the 2026 
Restricted A Share Incentive Scheme will not have a material impact on the 
Company’s financial position and operating results. 
IV. Opinions of the Remuneration and Evaluation Committee under the 
Board of Directors 
The Remuneration and Evaluation Committee under the Board of Directors is of 
the opinions that, the adjustments made by the Company to the grant price under the 
2026 Restricted A Share Incentive Scheme are in compliance with relevant laws, 
regulations and normative documents such as the Administrative Measures for Equity 
Incentives of Listed Companies as well as relevant provisions of the Incentive 
Scheme. Furthermore, such adjustments have been authorized by the general meeting 
with the adjustment procedures being legal and compliant, and there is no 
circumstance that would harm the interests of the Company and all its shareholders. 
Therefore, we unanimously agree to make the adjustments to the grant price 
under the Incentive Scheme.
