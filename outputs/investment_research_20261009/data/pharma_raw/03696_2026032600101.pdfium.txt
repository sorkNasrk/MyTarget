VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
INSILICO MEDICINE AND TENACIA BIOTECHNOLOGY EXPAND 
AI-DRIVEN CNS COLLABORATION WITH ADDITIONAL DEAL VALUE 
UP TO USD94.75 MILLION
This announcement is made by InSilico Medicine Cayman TopCo (the “Company”, together 
with its subsidiaries, the “Group” or “Insilico Medicine”) on a voluntary basis to inform the 
shareholders and potential investors of the Company about the latest business updates of the 
Group.
The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) is pleased to announce that, building on the 
productive collaboration over the past year, Insilico Medicine and Tenacia Biotechnology (Hong 
kong) Co., Limited (“Tenacia Biotechnology”), a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company 
dedicated to developing innovative therapeutics for patients with underserved neurological 
disorders, have entered into an expanded AI-driven R&D collaboration (the “Collaboration”).
Through the Collaboration, the two parties aim to provide a broader and more precise set of 
therapeutic options for highly challenging nerve system-related conditions, contributing to the 
mitigation of late-stage development risk and improvement of potential clinical benefit.
Under the terms of the relevant agreement, the parties will leverage generative AI to jointly 
develop an additional innovative molecule candidate with defined properties for challenging 
neurological diseases and advance it to the preclinical candidate (PCC) stage to address 
differentiated clinical needs. The expanded collaboration carries additional potential deal value of 
up to USD94.75 million, with Insilico Medicine being eligible to receive near-term and milestone 
payments from Tenacia Biotechnology.

About Insilico Medicine
Insilico Medicine is a pioneering global biotechnology company dedicated to integrating artificial 
intelligence and automation technologies to accelerate drug discovery, drive innovation in the life 
sciences, and extend health longevity to people on the planet. The Company was listed on the Main 
Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on December 30, 2025, under the stock code 03696.HK.
By integrating AI and automation technologies and deep in-house drug discovery capabilities, 
Insilico Medicine is delivering innovative drug solutions for unmet needs including fibrosis, 
oncology, immunology, pain, and obesity and metabolic disorders. Additionally, Insilico Medicine 
extends the reach of Pharma.AI across diverse industries, such as advanced materials, agriculture, 
nutritional products and veterinary medicine. For more information, please visit www.insilico.com.
