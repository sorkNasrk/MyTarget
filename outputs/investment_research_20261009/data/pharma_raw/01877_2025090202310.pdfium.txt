JT118 is a “two-in-one” recombinant protein vaccine composed of a tandem fusion of monkeypox 
virus antigens A35 (an extracellular enveloped virus antigen) and M1 (an intracellular mature virus 
antigen), and is intended mainly for the prevention of monkeypox virus infection. Monkeypox is 
a zoonotic viral disease caused by infection with the monkeypox virus. The monkeypox virus is 
a member of the Orthopoxvirus of the Poxviridae. During the virus replication process, two types 
of virus particles with different morphologies are produced: extracellular enveloped virus and 
intracellular mature virus, which are both infectious. JT118 not only retains the epitope structure of 
two natural antigens, A35 and M1, but also greatly increases the molecular weight through multi￾antigen fusion, which is more conducive to immune stimulation. Preclinical studies demonstrate 
that administration of JT118 elicits high-titer binding antibodies , neutralizing antibodies against 
vaccinia virus/monkeypox virus , and robust cellular immune activation in both small and large 
animal models . The vaccine confers significant protection against vaccinia virus/monkeypox virus 
infection in these models, with a favorable safety profile.
In October 2023, the Company reached cooperation with Peking University* （北京大學）, the 
Institute of Microbiology, Chinese Academy of Sciences* (中國科學院微生物研究所), Shanxi 
Academy of Advanced Research and Innovation* (山西高等創新研究院) and Beihang University* 
（北京航空航天大學） to jointly develop a recombinant protein vaccine for monkeypox. As of 
the date of this announcement, no vaccine for preventing monkeypox virus infection has been 
approved for marketing in China.
