(hereinafter referred to as the “Company Law”), Securities Law of the People's 
Republic of China* (hereinafter referred to as the “Securities Law”), Certain Provisions 
on the Reduction of Shareholdings by Shareholders, Directors, Supervisors, and Senior 
Management of Listed Companies* (《上市公司股東、董監高減持股份的若干規
定》), the Implementation Measures of Shanghai Stock Exchange on the Reduction of 
Shareholdings by Shareholders, Directors, Supervisors and Senior Management of 
Listed Companies* (《上海證券交易所上市公司股東及董事、監事、高級管理人

[PDF PAGE 9]
員减持股份實施細則》), as well as the articles of association of Shanghai Junshi 
Biosciences Co., Ltd. * (hereinafter referred to as the “Articles of Association”). The 
details are as follows:
1. If a participant is a director or senior management of the Company, the shares 
transferred by him/her every year shall not exceed 25% of the total shares held by 
him/her in the Company during his/her term of office, and he/she shall not transfer the 
shares of the Company held by him/her within six months after his/her resignation;
2. If a participant is a director or senior management of the Company and his/her 
spouse, parents and children, who sells the shares of the Company held by him/her 
within six months after the purchase, or purchases the shares within six months after 
the sale, the income derived therefrom shall be attributed to the Company, and the board 
of directors of the Company shall collect his/her gains;
3. During the validity period of the incentive scheme, if the relevant provisions of 
the relevant laws, regulations and regulatory documents including the Company Law, 
the Securities Law, Certain Provisions on the Reduction of Shareholdings by 
Shareholders, Directors, Supervisors, and Senior Management of Listed Companies*, 
the Implementation Measures of Shanghai Stock Exchange on the Reduction of 
Shareholdings by Shareholders, Directors, Supervisors and Senior Management of 
Listed Companies*, and the Articles of Association on the transfer of shares held by the 
directors and senior management of the Company are changed, the transfer of the shares 
held by such participants shall comply with the modified provisions.

IV. Capital Verification and Share Registration
On 11 January 2023, RSM China (容誠會計師事務所(特殊普通合夥)) issued 
the Capital Verification Report (Rong Cheng Yan Zi No. [2023]230Z0007), which 
reviewed the Company’s newly increased registered capital and share capital as of 10 
January 2023. As of 10 January 2023, the Company has received investment amount 
totaling to RMB156,411,820.50 from 668 participants, including an increase in share 
capital of RMB2,818,231.00 and an increase in capital reserve of RMB153,593,589.50, 
all of which are contributed by monetary funds.
The newly attributed shares were registered with the Shanghai Branch of China 
Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation Limited on 2 February 2023.
V. The Impact of New Shares after the Attribution on the Latest Financial 
Report
According to the Company’s third quarterly report of 2022, the net profit 
attributable to shareholders of the listed company for the period from January to 
September 2022 is RMB -1,594,729,564.22, and the basic earnings per share for the 
period from January to September 2022 is RMB -1.75; based on the total share capital 
of 985,689,871 shares after the attribution, the basic earnings per share for the period 
from January to September 2022 will be correspondingly diluted with the net profit 
attributable to the shareholders of the listed company unchanged.
The number of attributed restricted shares is 2,818,231, representing 
approximately 0.29% of the total share capital of the Company before the attribution, 
which does not have a material impact on the latest financial position and operating 
results of the Company.
