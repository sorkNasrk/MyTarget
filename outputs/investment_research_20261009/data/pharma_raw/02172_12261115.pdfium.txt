VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
Artemis Dream® SELF-EXPANDING INTRACRANIAL RAPAMYCIN 
ELUTING STENT SYSTEM 
ENTERS THE INNOVATIVE GREEN PATH OF NMPA
This announcement is made by MicroPort NeuroScientific Corporation (the “Company”, 
together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) on a voluntary basis.
The board of directors (the “Board”) of the Company is pleased to announce that Artemis 
Dream® Self-Expanding Intracranial Rapamycin Eluting Stent System (“Artemis Dream®”), 
developed by MicroPort NeuroTech (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of the Group, has 
passed the special review application for innovative medical devices of National Medical 
Products Administration (the “NMPA”) and entered the special review procedure (the 
“Green Path”). To date, the Group has had seven products included in the NMPA’s special 
review program for innovative medical devices. The inclusion of this product marks further 
official recognition of the Group’s technological strategy in the field of intracranial arterial 
stenosis treatment.
As an innovative product developed to tackle the clinical challenges of intracranial arterial 
stenosis, Artemis Dream® is the first domestically-developed targeted drug-eluting self￾expanding intracranial arterial stent whose core design advantages are as follows:
• Balance radial support and long-term outcomes: It adopts a self-expanding stent 
structure to precisely tune mechanical support performance and metal coverage, 
lowering the risk of vascular injury and restenosis from a structural perspective.

• Realize targeted drug delivery: Equipped with the “single-sided grooved” targeted 
drug delivery technology, the stent delivers superior coating adhesion durability and 
achieves directional drug release exclusively toward the vessel wall. It inhibits intimal 
hyperplasia, accelerates endothelialization, and helps shorten the postoperative dual 
antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) duration.
The inclusion of Artemis Dream® in the “Green Path” for innovative review will effectively 
shorten the product registration review cycle, accelerate the product’s market launch to 
benefit patients, and further enhance the Group’s comprehensive product portfolio for the 
treatment of ischemic cerebrovascular diseases.
There can be no assurance that Artemis Dream® will eventually be successfully 
commercialized. Shareholders and potential investors of the Company should exercise 
caution when dealing in the shares of the Company.
