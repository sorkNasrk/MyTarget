VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
INCLUSION IN FTSE GLOBAL EQUITY INDEX SERIES
This announcement is made by InSilico Medicine Cayman TopCo (the “Company”, together 
with its subsidiaries, the “Group” or “Insilico Medicine”) on a voluntary basis to inform the 
shareholders and potential investors of the Company about the latest business updates of the 
Group.
The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) is pleased to announce that, based on the 
FTSE Russell’s quarterly review results of the FTSE Global Equity Index Series, Insilico Medicine 
will be included in the FTSE Global Equity Index Series, including the FTSE Global Small Cap 
Index, the FTSE Global All Cap Index, and the FTSE Global Total Cap Index (the “Inclusion”). 
The Inclusion will take effect on June 22, 2026 (Monday).
The FTSE Global Equity Index Series provides a robust global equity index framework with over 
19,000 large-, mid-, small- and micro-cap securities across 49 developed and emerging markets 
globally.
The Board believes that the Company’s inclusion in these three FTSE indexes marks widespread 
recognition of its solid fundamentals, innovative capabilities, and growth potential. The Inclusion 
is expected to expand the Company’s profile among global institutional investors and analysts, 
attract capital inflows from funds tracking the indexes to diversify its shareholder base, and support 
greater trading liquidity for its shares.
The Board appreciates the continued support from the shareholders and investors of the Company.
