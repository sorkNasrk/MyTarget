COMPLETION OF THE PLACING
The Board is pleased to announce that all the conditions set out in the Placing Agreement 
have been fulfilled and completion of the Placing took place on September 4, 2025.
Pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Placing Agreement, a total of 285,920,000 new 
Shares have been successfully placed by the Placing Agents to not less than six Placees at 
the Placing Price of HK$9.28 per Placing Share, representing approximately 6.64% of the 
enlarged issued share capital of the Company immediately upon completion of the Placing.
To the best of the Directors’ knowledge, information and belief, having made all reasonable 
enquiries, the Placees are professional, institutional, or other investors that are, together with 
their respective ultimate beneficial owners, third parties independent of the Company and its 
connected persons.
The total gross proceeds from the Placing are approximately HK$2,653.3 million, and the 
net proceeds (after deducting all applicable costs and expenses, including commission and 
levies) arising from the Placing amounted to approximately HK$2,629.8 million.
NET PROCEEDS FROM THE PLACING
The Company intends to use the net proceeds from the Placing primarily for the purposes 
set forth below:
(i) continuous product iterations and upgrades to enhance its research and development 
technological capabilities and solution capabilities, for example:
(a) the Group will continue to upgrade and iterate robotic laboratory technology 
to create its industry-unique flywheel system integrating high-throughput 
experiment, high-quality data and advanced AI models. In particular, in the first 
half of 2025, the Group has achieved important breakthroughs by successfully 
developing a new generation of modular robotic systems. By integrating advanced 
visual perception, AI and robotic technologies, the Group has developed a new￾generation modular robot features its patented “NeoDispenser,” a flexible gripper 
that successfully mimics human hand operations with exceptional dexterity, 
which addresses three major industry pain points: (1) precise handling of trace 
powders, as the NeoDispenser can accurately dispense solid reagents below 5 
mg; (2) overcoming traditional solutions’ poor adaptability to different powders; 
and (3) eliminating intermediate containers to reduce costly material waste;

[PDF PAGE 3]

(b) the Group will also continue to upgrade its AI platform capabilities. For 
example, in the first half of 2025, its AI antibody development platform, 
XtalFold, launched a new Ultra mode. Compared to the Normal mode, this Ultra 
mode improves accuracy by approximately 10 percentage points in antigen￾antibody complex structure prediction, significantly enhancing its performance 
in modeling complex biomolecules. The platform was also honored as one of 
the “Top 10 AI Innovations (Technologies/Products)” at the World Artificial 
Intelligence Conference in July 2025 and was officially launched at the 
conference. The Group will also continue to collaborate with strategic partners 
to upgrade its underlying AI models. For example, the Group has been working 
with its key strategic partner, Pfizer, to build a next-generation AI-driven 
molecular simulation platform, which focuses on constructing a next-generation 
classical mechanics-based models tailored to Pfizer’s proprietary chemical 
space and deploying Pfizer’s localized XFEP free energy perturbation platform, 
covering the entire workflow from parameter customization to FEP calculations. 
Furthermore, the Group is actively expanding such platform’s capabilities across 
diverse application, including small molecules, peptides, and antibodies, to 
comprehensively empower Pfizer’s drug discovery efforts; and
(c) the Group is also developing new modality platforms, such as the new Molecular 
Glue platform possessing greater potential for targeting difficult-to-drug or 
under-targeted targets; the PepiX™ peptide & protein research and development 
platform for early-stage peptide drug research and novel functional peptide 
ingredients in cosmetics, as well as the mRNA2vec technology platform. 
Apart from developing on its own, the Group may also consider rapid building 
of relevant upstream and downstream technologies through investment, 
acquisition, industry collaboration and/or academic collaboration, to enhance the 
competitiveness of its products and services and rapidly launch these services and 
products to the market;
(ii) product commercialisation and business development, strengthening business 
collaborations with external parties and partners which are engaged in similar and 
mutually beneficial industries as the Group, and expanding the scale and market share 
of the Company:
(a) as described in (i), the Group is developing new modality platforms and 
upgrading existing technologies, and it will consider rapidly building upstream 
and downstream technology platforms through investment, acquisitions, industry 
collaboration and/or academic collaboration. Furthermore, the Group will also 
expedite the launch of technology or products to the market and accelerate the 
scale-up of its services and products through collaborations;

(b) strengthen industry-university-research collaboration by establishing
