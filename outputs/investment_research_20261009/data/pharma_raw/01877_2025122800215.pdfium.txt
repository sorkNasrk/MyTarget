ACCEPTANCE OF REGISTRATION OF 
THE MEDIUM TERM NOTES BY THE NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF 
FINANCIAL MARKET INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS
This announcement is made by Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海君實生物醫藥科技
股份有限公司) (the “Company”) on a voluntary basis. Reference is also made to the overseas 
regulatory announcement of the Company dated 28 December 2025.
The Company has recently received the Notice of Acceptance for Registration (Zhong Shi Xie Zhu 
(中市協注) [2025] No. MTN1249) (the “Notice”) issued by the National Association of Financial 
Market Institutional Investors (the “NAFMII”). The NAFMII accepted the registration of the 
technology innovation bonds of the Company with the following specifications:
(1) The basic type registered by the Company is medium term notes, with the registered amount 
of the medium term notes of the Company being RMB20 billion. Such registered amount will 
be valid for 2 years commencing from the date of this Notice, and will be underwritten by 
Bank of Shanghai Co., Ltd.* (上海銀行股份有限公司).
(2) The Company may issue the technology innovation bonds in tranches during the validity 
period, subject to any prior filing requirement with the NAFMII if proceeding. The 
management of the issuance of such bonds of the Company must be conducted in accordance 
with the resolutions of the competent authorities and relevant management requirements. 
Upon completion, the issuance results shall be disclosed through channels recognised by 
NAFMII.
In accordance with the requirements of relevant laws and regulations and the authorization granted 
by the general meeting of the Company, the Company will issue technology innovation bonds 
according to capital needs and market conditions within the prescribed validity period, and make 
the disclosure in a timely manner in accordance with relevant requirements.
