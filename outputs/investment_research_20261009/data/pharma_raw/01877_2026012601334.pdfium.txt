COMPLETION OF ISSUANCE OF THE 2026 FIRST 
TRANCHE OF TECHNOLOGY INNOVATION BONDS
This announcement is made by Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海君實生物醫藥科技
股份有限公司) (the “Company”) on a voluntary basis. Reference is also made to the overseas 
regulatory announcement of the Company dated 26 January 2026.
Reference is made to the announcement dated 28 December 2025 of the Company in relation to the 
acceptance of registration of the medium term notes by the national association of financial market 
institutional investors (the “Announcement”). Unless otherwise defined, capitalized terms used 
herein shall have the same meanings as those defined in the Announcement.
The board of directors of the Company is pleased to announce that the Company has completed the 
issuance of the 2026 first tranche of technology innovation bonds recently, and received the funds 
on 26 January 2026. The 2026 first tranche of technology innovation bonds are issued with a total 
amount of RMB1 billion and a term of 3+2 years, and shall mature on 26 January 2031 and have 
an issuance interest rate of 2.70%.
Shareholders of the Company and potential investors are advised to exercise cautiously when 
dealing in the securities of the Company.
