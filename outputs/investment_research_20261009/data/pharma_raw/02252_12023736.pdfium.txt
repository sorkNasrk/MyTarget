VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
TOUMAI® LAPAROSCOPIC SURGICAL ROBOT COMMERCIAL 
ORDERS EXCEEDING 200 UNITS WORLDWIDE
This announcement is made by Shanghai MicroPort MedBot (Group) Co., Ltd. (the 
“Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) on a voluntary basis to provide 
the shareholders and potential investors of the Company the updates on the latest business 
development of the Group.
The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) is pleased to announce that, as of 
the date of this announcement, the Group’s core product, Toumai® Laparoscopic Surgical 
Robot (“Toumai”), has received over 200 commercial orders worldwide, covering nearly 
50 countries and regions, and achieved commercial installations of approximately 130 units. 
The further milestone leap forward in the commercial orders and installation base of Toumai 
symbolizes the sustained and widespread recognition of its clinical value around the world, 
and is a systematic certification of the safety, stability and reliability of Toumai. Surgeons 
from diverse countries, healthcare systems and specialist backgrounds are integrating 
Toumai into their daily surgical practices and long-term discipline development through 
authentic and prudent clinical decision-making.
Among the nearly 50 covered countries and regions, 12 have ordered more than 5 units. 
Emerging markets such as India, Brazil and Argentina have shown strong growth, of 
which the cumulative number of orders in India has reached 14 units, and in Brazil over 10 
units; the developed country markets have continued to make breakthroughs, with Spain, 
Australia and other countries becoming the fast growing points. The commercialization of 
Toumai has formed a new pattern of synergy and resonance in multiple regions around the 
world, penetrating through emerging markets in developing countries as well as mature 
healthcare systems in developed countries.

While continually accelerating its global expansion, Toumai has also been making 
breakthroughs in the depth of single-center clinical applications. According to internal 
statistics, dozens of medical institutions in various countries that have completed 
the commercial installation of Toumai have performed more than 100 surgeries per 
single-center, covering a wide range of departments including urology, general surgery, 
thoracic surgery, gynecology, pediatric surgery, and head and neck surgery.
As the world’s first remote surgical robot system approved for commercial application, 
Toumai remote surgical robot has been approved for listing in nearly 10 countries, including 
China, India and Brazil. Its application has covered areas with nearly half of the world’s 
population and has taken the lead in entering the stage of routine and large-scale clinical 
application. So far, Toumai has completed nearly 800 remote surgeries, covering more than 
20 countries, and the implementation success rate continued to maintain 100%.
