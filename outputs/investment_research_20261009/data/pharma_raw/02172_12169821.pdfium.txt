VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
NUMEN™ Helia COIL RECEIVES APPROVAL FROM FDA FOR 
MARKETING
This announcement is made by MicroPort NeuroScientific Corporation (the “Company”, 
together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) on a voluntary basis.
The board of directors (the “Board”) of the Company is pleased to announce that 
NUMEN™ Helia Coil Embolization System (“NUMEN™ Helia”), independently 
developed by MicroPort NeuroTech (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of the Group, has 
recently received approval from the United States Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”) 
for marketing.
This approval marks the third coil product of the Group to be approved for marketing 
in the United States, following the NUMEN™ Detachable Coil and the NUMEN™ 
Silk Coil Embolization System. It further enhances the Group’s product portfolio 
in the neuro-interventional embolization field in North America and reflects the 
Group’s continuous breakthroughs in the research and development of innovative 
neuro-interventional products and international registration.
NUMEN™ Helia is intended to endovascularly obstruct or occlude blood flow in vascular 
abnormalities of the neurovascular vessels. Compared with the currently marketed 
NUMEN™ series coils products, NUMEN™ Helia features as “longer, thinner, and softer”. 
It is compatible with 0.013-inch microcatheters and is suitable for delivery and embolization 
operations in small, tortuous, or access-constrained vessels, offering a more refined solution 
for the treatment of complex vascular lesions.

The key design advantages of NUMEN™ Helia include:
• Optimized adaptability to complex vasculature
Its unique Cube/Spiral structural configuration enhances wall anchoring performance, 
enabling smoother delivery and natural conformance in small and complex vascular 
environments.
• Support for efficient and dense packing
The product covers diameters ranging from 1mm to 3.5mm, with lengths up to 60cm, 
and offers a variety of extended-length configurations, assisting operators in achieving 
more efficient and precise packing operations in small vessels.
• Enhanced minimally invasive operation experience
The ultra-soft coil design meets the delivery requirements of small vessels and 
complex access routes, helping to improve embolization operations efficiency and 
optimize clinical operation experience.
This approval of NUMEN™ Helia by FDA further enriches the Group’s product matrix 
in the neuro-interventional market in the United States. Its design features will help meet 
a wider range of clinical needs for neurovascular embolization therapy, enhancing the 
Group’s product strength and competitive advantage in the neuro-interventional market 
in the United States. Looking ahead, the Group will continue to focus on the therapeutic 
needs of neurovascular diseases, accelerate the global registration and commercialization of 
innovative products, and continuously refine comprehensive neuro-interventional solutions, 
providing physicians and patients worldwide with higher-quality and more accessible 
treatment options.
