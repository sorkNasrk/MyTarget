VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
APOLLO Dream® Rapamycin Target Eluting Stent System Granted FDA 
Breakthrough Device Designation
This announcement is made by MicroPort NeuroScientific Corporation (the “Company”, 
together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) on a voluntary basis.
The board of directors (the “Board”) of the Company is pleased to announce that 
APOLLO Dream® Rapamycin Target Eluting Stent System (“APOLLO Dream® Stent 
System”), independently developed by the Group, has been granted the Breakthrough 
Device Designation (“Breakthrough Device Designation”) by the U.S. Food and Drug 
Administration (“FDA”) recently, being the world’s first balloon-expandable, rapid 
exchange drug-eluting stent in the neuro-interventional field to receive this designation.
The APOLLO Dream® Stent System is specifically designed for patients with symptomatic 
intracranial arterial stenosis who are refractory to best medical therapy. The system 
integrates targeted drug-eluting technology with an optimized stent mechanical structure, 
providing stable vascular scaffolding and restoring cerebral blood flow, while targeted 
delivery of rapamycin to the vessel wall inhibits vascular smooth muscle cell proliferation, 
thereby reducing the risk of in-stent restenosis.
Compared with conventional drug-eluting stents, APOLLO Dream® Stent System enables 
more precise control of drug-release dosage, significantly reducing the total systemic 
drug load while maintaining therapeutic efficacy. Its drug coating utilizes biodegradable 
materials, which gradually degrade after drug release is completed, potentially lowering the 
long-term risk of thrombosis.

The Breakthrough Device Designation is intended to be granted to innovative medical 
technologies that are developed for life-threatening or irreversibly debilitating diseases and 
that demonstrate the potential to provide significant clinical advantages. APOLLO Dream®
Stent System is the world’s first balloon-expandable, rapid exchange drug-eluting stent in 
the neuro-interventional field to receive the designation. This recognition highlights:
• The technological innovation and potential clinical value of the APOLLO Dream®
Stent System in the treatment of intracranial atherosclerotic disease (ICAD) have been 
recognized by an international authoritative regulatory body;
• It will help accelerate the clinical development and regulatory review process of the 
device;
• It is expected to fill the market gap of the treatment of intracranial atherosclerotic 
stenosis with drug stent overseas and to benefit patients worldwide; and
• It lays an important foundation for the Group to advance its globalization strategy and 
accelerate its entry into key international markets.
Looking ahead, the Group will continue to advance the global clinical studies and 
registration process of the APOLLO Dream® Stent System, further expand the Company’s 
international influence in the neurovascular interventional innovative therapy, and provide 
more advanced treatment options for patients worldwide.
There can be no assurance that the APOLLO Dream® Stent System will be successfully 
commercialized. Shareholders and potential investors of the Company should exercise 
caution when dealing in the shares of the Company.
