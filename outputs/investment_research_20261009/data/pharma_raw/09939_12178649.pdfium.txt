VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
KX-826 PHASE III CLINICAL TRIAL AND COMBINATION THERAPY 
STUDY DATA FOR AGA PRESENTED AT THE 2026 WORLD
CONGRESS FOR HAIR RESEARCH (WCHR 2026), AND WAS
SELECTED AS AN OUTSTANDING POSTER
This is a voluntary announcement made by Kintor Pharmaceutical Limited (the “Company”,
together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to update its shareholders and potential investors 
on the latest developments related to the Group.
The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company is pleased to announce 
that, its in-house developed and potential first-in-class KX-826 data from two important 
clinical studies was presented at the 14th World Congress for Hair Research (WCHR 2026) 
held in Seoul, South Korea from May 28 to 31, 2026: 
(i) phase III clinical trial data for the treatment of male adult androgenetic alopecia (“AGA”) in 
China. Professor Cheng Zhou (周城) from Peking University People’s Hospital, the lead
principal investigators of the phase III clinical trial, presented the final analysis results of KX￾826 1.0% tincture for the treatment of male adult AGA in China at main venue. The overall 
results demonstrated that KX-826 exhibited excellent efficacy and safety in patients with AGA. 
Furthermore, the results of the phase III clinical trial were selected as an Outstanding Poster at 
WCHR 2026.
(ii) clinical observational study data in combination with minoxidil for the treatment of male 
adult AGA in China. The clinical observational study was led and completed by Professor 
Leiwei Jiang (江蕾微) and colleagues, and provided a solid basis for future phase III clinical 
trial of KX-826 and minoxidil combination therapy.

Held biennially, it provides participants with a unique opportunity to delve into the outcomes
