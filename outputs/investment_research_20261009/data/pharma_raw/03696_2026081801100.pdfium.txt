VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
POSSIBLE PARTICIPATION IN THE ESTABLISHMENT OF AN 
INVESTMENT FUND
This announcement is made by InSilico Medicine Cayman TopCo (the “Company”, together with 
its subsidiaries, the “Group”) on a voluntary basis to inform its shareholders and potential investors 
of the latest business development of the Group.
The Company is considering participating in the proposed establishment of an investment fund, 
tentatively named Insilico Pudong Development Value Partners (Shanghai) Private Equity 
Investment Fund Partnership (Limited Partnership)* (英矽浦發惠理(上海)私募股權投資基金合夥
企業(有限合夥)). (the “Insilico Medicine Exponential Technology Fund” or “Fund”) through a 
series of transactions (the “Possible Transactions”).
As part of the implementation structure, the Company proposes to contribute RMB3 million 
through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Insilico Medicine (Shanghai) Investment Co., Ltd.* (英矽
智能(上海)投資有限公司) (“Insilico Shanghai Investment”) to establish a joint venture (the 
“Joint Venture”) with Shanghai Pudong Construction Co., Ltd. (上海浦東建設股份有限公司) 
(“Pudong Construction”, a company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange with stock code 
600284) and Shanghai Pudong Development (Group) Co., Ltd.* (上海浦東發展(集團)有限公司, 
the controlling shareholder of Pudong Construction). The capital contribution by Insilico Shanghai 
Investment is expected to represent 60% of the registered capital of the Joint Venture, being 
RMB5 million. Upon completion of its establishment, the Joint Venture is expected to become an 
indirect non-wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, and its financial results are expected to be 
consolidated into the accounts of the Group.
Following the establishment of the Joint Venture, the Company proposes to contribute a total 
of RMB302.5 million through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Insilico Medicine Ltd.* (英矽
智能科技(上海)有限公司) and the Joint Venture to establish the Fund with Value Partners 
Equity Investment Management (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.* (惠理股權投資管理(深圳)有限公司, 
a wholly-owned subsidiary of Value Partners Group Limited (a company listed on the Stock 
Exchange with stock code 806)), Pudong Construction and other prospective investors.

The Fund is proposed to be established as a limited partnership in the PRC under the proposed 
name of Insilico Pudong Development Value Partners (Shanghai) Private Equity Investment Fund 
Partnership (Limited Partnership)* (英矽浦發惠理(上海)私募股權投資基金合夥企業(有限合夥)). 
The Fund is expected to principally focus on investments in the biopharmaceutical sector and other 
related industries. The target fund size is expected to be RMB1.01 billion.
The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) believes that the Possible Transactions are 
aligned with the Group’s strategic direction, and would enable the Group to participate in growth 
opportunities arising from the continued development and innovation of the biopharmaceutical and 
life sciences sectors.
As at the date of this announcement, no definitive agreement has been entered in respect 
of the Possible Transactions. The implementation of the Possible Transactions, including 
any capital contribution and deployment, remains subject to the execution of definitive 
agreements and the phased capital contribution arrangements to be agreed therein. As such, 
the Possible Transactions may or may not proceed. If the Company enters into the definitive 
agreements relating to the Possible Transactions, it is expected to constitute a discloseable 
transaction of the Company under Chapter 14 of the Rules Governing the Listing of 
Securities on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the “Listing Rules”). The Company 
will make further announcement(s) in relation to the Possible Transactions as and when 
appropriate in accordance with the Listing Rules.
Shareholders and potential investors of the Company are advised to exercise caution when 
dealing in the shares of the Company.
