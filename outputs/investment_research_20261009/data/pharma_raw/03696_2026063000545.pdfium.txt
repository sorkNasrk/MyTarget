VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
VOLUNTARY EXTENSION OF LOCK-UP 
UNDERTAKINGS GIVEN BY CEOS
This announcement is made by InSilico Medicine Cayman TopCo (the “Company”, together with 
its subsidiaries, the “Group” or “Insilico Medicine”) on a voluntary basis.
Reference is made to the prospectus of the Company dated December 18, 2025 (the “Prospectus”). 
Unless otherwise defined, terms used herein shall have the same meaning as defined in the 
Prospectus.
The Company has recently received undertakings (the “Extension Undertakings”) from Dr. 
Aleksandrs Zavoronkovs, the chairman of the Board, an executive Director, CEO, CBO and 
the single largest shareholder of the Company, and Dr. Feng Ren, an executive Director, CEO 
and CSO of the Company (collectively, the “Relevant Shareholders”), regarding the voluntary 
extension of the lock-up periods of all of their equity interests in the Company as of the date of 
this announcement, which include:
1. 42,583,500 shares directly held by Dr. Aleksandrs Zavoronkovs; and
2. 8,740,820 shares interested by Dr. Feng Ren, including (i) 475,400 shares held by Dr. Feng 
Ren through his wholly-owned company; (ii) 5,065,420 underlying shares to be subscribed 
upon full exercise of options granted under the Pre-IPO Equity Incentive Plans; and (iii) 
3,200,000 underlying shares to be issued upon vesting of share awards granted under Pre-IPO 
Equity Incentive Plans;
(collectively, the “Equity Interests”).

Each of the Relevant Shareholders has entered into a lock-up undertaking pursuant to which he has 
agreed that, during the period commencing from the date of the Prospectus up to and including the 
date falling twelve (12) months after the Listing Date (the “Original Lock-up Period”), he will 
not, directly or indirectly, dispose of any shares of the Company held by him (for further details, 
please refer to the Prospectus). The Original Lock-up Period will expire on December 29, 2026.
Pursuant to the Extension Undertakings, as the Relevant Shareholders remain positive of the 
growth potential, core competitiveness and industry prospects of the Company from a long-term 
perspective, and to safeguard the stability of the share price of the Company and capital market 
confidence, as well as the legitimate rights and interests of the shareholders of the Company, 
particularly the minority shareholders, each of the Relevant Shareholders has undertaken to extend 
the Original Lock-up Period in respect of the Equity Interests for an additional six (6) months, up 
to and including June 29, 2027. During such extended lock-up period, the Relevant Shareholders 
will not, in any manner, directly or indirectly, dispose of or otherwise reduce their Equity Interests.
For the avoidance of doubt, the Extension Undertakings shall not restrict (i) any cashless exercise 
or cashless vesting under the Pre-IPO Equity Incentive Plans or the Post-IPO Equity Incentive 
Plans (if applicable), in case of which the total number of Equity Interests may be subject to 
deduction in accordance with applicable tax laws and regulations; and (ii) the disposal of any 
shares by proxy grantors who have granted proxy attached to their shares in favour of Dr. 
Aleksandrs Zavoronkovs pursuant to the Voting Proxy Arrangement as disclosed in the Prospectus.
The Company considers that not reducing the Equity Interests in the Company by the Relevant 
Shareholders will help to further promote the sustainable, steady and healthy development of the 
Company.
Shareholders and potential investors of the Company are advised to exercise caution when 
dealing in the Company’s shares.
