UPDATE ON CONNECTED TRANSACTION
TERMINATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT
Reference is made to the announcement of HighTide Therapeutics, Inc. (the “Company”) dated 
April 27, 2026 (the “Announcement”) in relation to the connected transaction regarding the Loan 
Agreement. Unless otherwise specified, capitalized terms used herein shall have the same meanings 
as those defined in the Announcement.
TERMINATION OF THE LOAN AGREEMENT
The Board hereby announces that on July 2, 2026, the Company (as the lender) and Dr. LIU 
Liping (“Dr. Liu”) (as the Borrower) have mutually agreed to terminate the Loan Agreement 
with immediate effect and the parties have unconditionally discharged each other from their 
obligations under the Loan Agreement. No amount under the facility has been drawn down prior to 
the termination of the Loan Agreement. The Directors consider that the termination will have no 
material adverse effect on the business and operations of the Group.
VIEWS OF THE STOCK EXCHANGE
As disclosed in the Announcement, as the highest applicable percentage ratio for the transaction 
contemplated under the Loan Agreement exceeds 0.1% but is less than 5%, the transaction is 
subject to the reporting and announcement requirements, but is exempt from the circular and 
independent shareholders’ approval requirements pursuant to Rule 14A.76(2) of the Listing Rules 
(the “Partial Exemption”).
Rule 14A.76(2) of the Listing Rules states that the Partial Exemption from the circular (including 
independent financial advice) and independent shareholders’ approval requirements applies to a 
connected transaction conducted on normal commercial terms or better.
When determining the interest rate of the facility, the Company used HIBOR (which represents a 
base lending rate between commercial banks and prime borrowers in Hong Kong) as a benchmark 
in determining the interest rate applicable to the facility, plus a margin reflecting an additional risk 
premium, and at the time, considered the reference to HIBOR to be consistent with precedent cases 
of other Hong Kong listed issuers.

Subsequent to the Announcement, the Stock Exchange has raised its concerns to the Company 
that the facility may not be on normal commercial terms or better, and accordingly, the Partial 
Exemption should not have been applied to the facility and the transaction contemplated 
thereunder should have been subject to the circular, independent financial advice and independent 
shareholders’ approval requirements under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules, for the reasons 
summarized below:
(a) the provision of the facility is outside the ordinary and usual course of business of the 
Company as a pre-revenue biotech company. The Stock Exchange considered that the specific 
risk profiles of Dr. Liu as a borrower and the Company itself as a lender were inherently 
higher than those of a prime borrower and a commercial bank respectively. Benchmarking 
with other market precedents would not be an appropriate approach to address the Stock 
Exchange’s concerns;
(b) the Stock Exchange also considered that the Borrower has yet to demonstrate that an 
unsecured personal loan on comparable terms would be available from independent 
commercial lender; and
(c) the Stock Exchange’s concern was further complicated by the fact that the facility carries 
minimal or no spread with reference to the Company’s cost of borrowings.
Accordingly, the Company should have complied with the following Listing Rules on a timely 
basis:
(a) Rule 14A.36 of the Listing Rules requires that a connected transaction must be conditional 
on shareholders’ approval at a general meeting held by the listed issuer. Any shareholder who 
has a material interest in the transaction must abstain from voting on the resolution;
(b) Rule 14A.39 of the Listing Rules requires an issuer to set up an independent board 
committee and appoint an independent financial advisor if the connected transaction requires 
shareholders’ approval; and
(c) Rule 14A.46 of the Listing Rules requires an issuer to send a circular to its shareholders if 
the connected transaction is to be approved by its shareholders in a general meeting.

RESPONSES FROM THE COMPANY
The Company attaches the utmost importance to compliance with the Listing Rules and all 
applicable laws and regulations. In light of the concern from the Stock Exchange, the parties have 
terminated the Loan Agreement, and no amount under the facility has been drawn down. Neither 
the interests of the Company nor those of the Shareholders have been prejudiced. The Company 
will comprehensively review and enhance its internal policies and procedures, so that the Directors, 
senior management and responsible personnel can make appropriate judgments on potential Listing 
Rules issues, and if necessary, escalate to the Board and/or seek professional advice on a timely 
basis.
