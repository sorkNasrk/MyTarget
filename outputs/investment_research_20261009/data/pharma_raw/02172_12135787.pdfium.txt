CLOSURE OF REGISTER OF MEMBERS

Reference is made to the 2025 annual report (the “Annual Report”) of MicroPort 
NeuroScientific Corporation (the “Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) 
dated 27 April 2026, in relation to, among other things, the proposed payment of a final 
dividend, which remains subject to the approval of the Shareholders at the general meeting 
of the Company to be held on Wednesday, 3 June 2026 (the “2025 AGM”). Unless

meanings as those defined in the Annual Report.
The Board further announces that for the purpose of determining the entitlement of the 
Shareholders to receive the proposed final dividend, the register of members of the Company 
will be closed from Monday, 6 July 2026 to Wednesday, 8 July 2026, both days inclusive, 
during which period no transfer of shares will be registered. The record date for the 
proposed final dividend is 8 July 2026. In order to qualify for the proposed final dividend, 
all transfers of shares, accompanied by the relevant share certificates, must be lodged with 
the Company’s branch share registrar in Hong Kong, Computershare Hong Kong Investor 
Services Limited, at Shops 1712–1716, 17th Floor, Hopewell Centre, 183 Queen’s Road 
East, Wanchai, Hong Kong, for registration not later than 4:30 p.m. on Friday, 3 July 2026 
(Hong Kong Time), being the last registration date.
