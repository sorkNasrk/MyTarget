IV. Accounting treatment and estimation impact on results 
(I) Fair value and determination method of the Restricted Shares 
According to relevant requirements of the Accounting Standards for Business 
Enterprises No. 11 - Share-based Payments, Accounting Standards for Business 
Enterprises No. 22 - Recognition and Measurement of Financial Instruments and 
Applicable Cases of Share-Based Payments – Grant of Restricted Shares, the Company 
selected the Black-Scholes model to calculate the fair value of Type II Restricted Shares, 
and estimated the Type II Restricted Shares granted with such model. The Grant Date 
of the Reserved Grant, i.e. 15 November 2021, was determined as the benchmark date. 
The specific parameters were selected as follows: 
1. Subject share price: RMB53.80 per share (the closing price at the Grant Date of 
the Reserved Grant of the Company); 
 

[PDF PAGE 10]
2. Validity periods are: 1 year and 2 years (the period from the Grant Date of the 
Reserved Grant to the first attribution date of each period), respectively; 
3. Historical volatility: 32.63% and 33.59% (adopted the annualized volatility of 
Shenwen Second-Class Pharmaceutical Biology Index (申萬生物製品(二級)指數) for 
last year and last two years, respectively); 
4. Risk-free interest rate: 1.50% and 2.10% (adopted the benchmark interest rates 
of financial institutions for 1-year and 2-year deposits determined by the People’s Bank 
of China, respectively). 
(II) Impacts of the implementation of the Restricted Shares on the operating 
results for each period 
The Company determines the fair value of the Restricted Shares at the Grant Date 
in accordance with the requirements of the accounting standards, and finally recognizes 
the share-based payment expenses under the Incentive Scheme. The total amount of 
such expenses, as the incentive cost of the Company’s Incentive Scheme, will be 
recognized over the course of the Incentive Scheme in attribution proportion in 
installments and will be charged to operating profit or loss.
