VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING — 
STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH 
PT SINAR MAS MULTIARTHA, TBK
This announcement is made by QuantumPharm Inc. (the “Company”, collectively with its 
subsidiaries, the “Group”) on a voluntary basis for the purpose of keeping the shareholders 
of the Company (the “Shareholders”) and potential investors informed of the latest business 
update of the Group.
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company is pleased to 
announce that on 30 October 2024, the Company entered into a non-legally binding 
memorandum of understanding (the “MOU”) with PT Sinar Mas Multiartha, Tbk. 
(“SMMA”) in relation to the proposed formation of a strategic partnership to collaborate 
in the fields of artificial intelligence and automation in Southeast Asia (the “Proposed 
Partnership”). It is contemplated under the MOU, among other things, that the Proposed 
Partnership will be carried out through the establishment of a joint venture company.

– 2 –
REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF ENTERING INTO THE MOU
The Company is an innovative research platform company powered by artificial intelligent 
(AI) and robotics and is driving intelligent and digital transformation in the life sciences and 
material sciences sectors. The Group combines quantum physics, AI, cloud computing, and 
large-scale robotics to provide R&D solutions, services, and products for pharmaceuticals, 
biotechnology, renewable energy, and advanced materials industries globally. The Group, 
a pioneer in AI and robotics for life science and new materials R&D, has collaborated with 
over 300 global enterprises and research institutions, leading the way with an extensive 
successful track record in AI for science commercialization.
SMMA is a leading financial services company in Indonesia, including banking, insurance, 
asset management, share administration, multi finance and information technology. SMMA 
is part of Sinar Mas Group which is one of Indonesia’s most influential and diversified 
conglomerates, and which maintains a commanding presence across sectors such as 
healthcare, mining, renewable energy, logistics, consumer goods, chemicals, and financial 
services. With its robust supply chain and deep market penetration across the Asia-Pacific, 
Sinar Mas Group is well-positioned to drive industry innovation through advanced AI 
solutions, reshaping the region’s economic landscape.
The Board considers that the Proposed Partnership allows the Group to leverage SMMA’s 
extensive industry resources and expertise in the expansion of the Group’s businesses 
in terms of both scale and geography. The Directors are of the view that the Proposed 
Partnership between the Group and SMMA, if materialised, represents an opportunity for 
the Group to broaden the revenue stream of the Group and is in the interests of the Company 
and the Shareholders as a whole.
