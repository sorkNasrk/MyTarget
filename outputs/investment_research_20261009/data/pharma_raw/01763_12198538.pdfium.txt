VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
RADIOACTIVE SOURCE R&D AND PRODUCTION BASE 
(PHASE II) SUCCESSFULLY GRANTED RADIATION 
SAFETY PERMIT, MARKING THE FULL COMPLETION AND 
COMMISSIONING OF ASIA’S LARGEST RADIOACTIVE 
SOURCE R&D AND PRODUCTION BASE
This announcement is made by China Isotope & Radiation Corporation (“China Isotope & 
Radiation” or the “Company”) on a voluntary basis to keep the shareholders and potential 
investors informed of the latest business development of the Company.
The board of directors of the Company is pleased to announce that, recently, the radioactive source 
R&D and production base project (Phase II) of Chengdu Gaotong Isotope Co., Ltd. (CNNC) (成
都中核高通同位素股份有限公司), a subsidiary of China Isotope & Radiation, was completed 
and successfully approved for the addition of licensed items to its radiation safety permit. The 
production lines for radioactive sources such as Californium-252, Caesium-137 and Americium-241 
have officially commenced production, fully meeting the diversified needs of the market.
In August 2025, Phase I of the radioactive source R&D and production base project received the 
radiation safety permit. The granting of the radiation safety permit for licensed items for Phase II 
marks the full completion and commencement of production of the largest radioactive source R&D 
and production base in Asia. The base has 14 radioactive source production lines, including for 
Carbon-14 sources and Krypton-85 sources, and is capable of producing high-quality radioactive 
source products of over 70 types of nuclides. The annual production capacity of radioactive sources 
will exceed 100,000 units, meeting the current demand for radioactive sources in fields such as 
industry, agriculture, medicine, national defense and aerospace in China.

The full commencement of production of this project marks the accelerated realization of technical 
upgrades and the transition from old to new production capacity in the field of sealed radioactive 
sources in China, consolidating China’s leading position in the research and development and 
industrial application of radioactive sources. Meanwhile, with the release of production capacity, 
relevant products will not only satisfy domestic demand but also continue to intensify international 
market expansion efforts, actively serving countries along the “Belt and Road”.
