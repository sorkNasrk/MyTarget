This announcement is published by MicroPort Scientific Corporation (the “Company”, together with

The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the “Stock Exchange”).
DELAY IN PUBLICATION OF THE 2026 INTERIM RESULTS
The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) wishes to inform the shareholders (the 
“Shareholders”) and potential investors of the Company that the Company will not be able to publish 
its interim results for the six months ended 30 June 2026 (the “2026 Interim Results”) on or before 31 
August 2026 in accordance with Rule 13.49(6) of the Listing Rules.
The delay in publication of the 2026 Interim Results is a prudent decision to allow the audit 
committee and the management of the Company additional time to thoroughly evaluate the relevant 
matters and their impact on financial reporting. To safeguard the integrity of financial reporting, 
the Board considered it appropriate to ensure that the Company’s interim financial statements and 
related disclosures are properly substantiated and that the auditor’s review is completed before public 
dissemination.
Reference is made to the announcement of the Company dated 20 August 2026 (the “Announcement”). 
Terms defined in the Announcement will have the same meaning in this announcement unless the 
context requires otherwise.

More time is required for (a) further negotiation on the key terms of the Proposed Lease, including the 
unit rent, the lease term and the area to be leased, and on the arrangements for the security deposit, 
including the amount of any deposit payable under the Proposed Lease and the timing and manner of 
any refund of the security deposit paid under the Existing Lease Agreements (if applicable); (b) a review 
of the recoverability of such security deposit and the reasonableness of the renovation costs in respect of 
the Property and (c) a review of the relationship between the Group and the landlord of the Property and 
its associates.
Pursuant to Rule 13.49(6) of the Listing Rules, the Company is required to publish the 2026 Interim 
Results on a date not later than two months after that period of six months, i.e. not later than 31 August 
2026. In light of these circumstances, there will be a delay in the Company’s publication of the 2026 
Interim Results.
POSTPONEMENT OF BOARD MEETING
Reference is made to the announcement of the Company dated 19 August 2026 in relation to the 
meeting of the Board to be held on Monday, 31 August 2026 for the purpose of, among other things, 
considering and approving the 2026 Interim Results. As the 2026 Interim Results will not be available 
for publication by 31 August 2026, the meeting of the Board for the purpose of, among other things, 
considering and approving the 2026 Interim Results, will be postponed.
SUSPENSION OF TRADING
Pursuant to Rule 13.50 of the Listing Rules, the Stock Exchange will normally require suspension of 
trading in an issuer’s securities if an issuer fails to publish periodic financial information in accordance 
with the Listing Rules, and the suspension will normally remain in force until the issuer publishes an 
announcement containing the requisite financial information.
At the request of the Company, trading in the shares of the Company on the Stock Exchange (stock 
code: 853) and its debt securities (debt stock code: 40168) will be suspended with effect from 9:00 a.m. 
on 1 September 2026, pending the publication of the 2026 Interim Results.
