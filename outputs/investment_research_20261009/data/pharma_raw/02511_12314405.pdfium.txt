GRANTS OF OPTIONS AND AWARDS
This announcement is made by the Company pursuant to Rules 17.06A, 17.06B and 17.06C of the 
Listing Rules.
The Board hereby announces that on September 1, 2026, the Company granted (i) 2,040,450 
Options to 4 Grantees, and (ii) 306,749 Awards to 1 Grantee pursuant to the 2025 Share Incentive 
Plan (together, the “Grant”), representing approximately 0.30% and 0.05% of the total Shares in 
issue and outstanding (excluding the treasury Shares) as of the Grant Date, respectively.
The details of the Grant are as follows:
Grant Date: September 1, 2026
Number of the 
 Options/Awards 
 granted:
(i) 2,040,450 Options, among which, 644,171 Options are granted to 
three Employee Participants and 1,396,279 Options are granted to

primarily responsible for overseeing and giving advice on the

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE GRANT
The purposes of the Grant are to (i) provide the Grantees with an opportunity to acquire proprietary 
interests in the Company; (ii) retain key personnel and high performers of the Group; (iii) provide 
the Grantees with additional incentives to achieve individual and corporate performance targets; 
and (iv) align the interests of the Grantees with those of the Shareholders by motivating them to 
enhance the value of the Company through equity ownership.
In particular, the Directors believe that, as a Service Provider Participant, Dr. Surmont’s extensive 
experience in the healthcare and pharmaceutical industries will be invaluable to the Group in 
advancing the commercialization of its core pipeline for the treatment of CKM diseases. The 
Directors further consider that the grant of Options to Dr. Surmont will assist the Company in 
retaining him as its chief medical officer and will incentivize him to contribute to the long-term 
growth and development of the Group, which aligns with the purpose of the 2025 Share Incentive 
Plan.
LISTING RULES IMPLICATIONS
To the best of the Directors’ knowledge, information and belief having made all reasonable 
enquiries, none of the Grantees is (i) a Director, a chief executive, a substantial shareholder of the 
Company, or an associate of any of them; (ii) a participant with Options and Awards granted and to 
be granted exceeding the 1% individual limit under Rule 17.03D of the Listing Rules; or (iii) (other 
than Dr. Surmont) a related entity participant or service provider with Options and Awards granted 
and to be granted in any 12-month period exceeding 0.1% of the total issued Shares (excluding 
treasury Shares). The Grant will not be subject to approval by the Shareholders.
NUMBER OF SHARES AVAILABLE FOR FUTURE GRANT
