INTRODUCTION
Reference is made to the announcement of the Company dated May 28, 2025 in relation to 
the issuance of the Filing Notice by the CSRC in respect of the Conversion and Listing (the 
“Announcement”). Unless otherwise stated, terms used herein shall have the same meanings as 
those defined in the Announcement.
LISTING APPROVAL GRANTED BY THE STOCK EXCHANGE
The Company has applied to the Listing Committee of the Stock Exchange for the approval (the 
“Listing Approval”) of the listing of and the permission to deal in 54,268,364 H shares (the 
“Converted H Shares”), representing the maximum number of unlisted shares to be converted 
under the Conversion and Listing (the “Unlisted Shares”). The Company is pleased to announce 
that the Listing Approval was granted by the Stock Exchange on June 19, 2025.
SHAREHOLDING STRUCTURE
The Conversion and Listing will involve a total of 4 participating holders of 54,268,364 Unlisted 
Shares who will, after the Conversion and Listing, hold approximately 3.17% of the total issued 
shares of the Company.
