SUPPLEMENTAL ANNOUNCEMENT
DISCLOSEABLE TRANSACTION IN RELATION TO
THE ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT
Reference is made to the announcement of Everest Medicines Limited (the ‘‘Company’’)
dated 23 March 2026 (the ‘‘Announcement’’). Unless otherwise defined, capitalized terms
used in this announcement shall have the same meanings as those defined in the
Announcement.
The Board would like to provide the Shareholders and potential investors of the Company
with additional information in connection to the Asset Purchase Agreement and the
transactions contemplated thereunder as follows.
(i) In respect of the Assigned Contracts: (a) the excluded liabilities under the License
Agreement refer to the payment, performance and other obligations arising from and in
connection with the clinical operation, CMC (chemistry, manufacturing and controls)
activities, supply chain activities and application of NMPA PSVT approval which were
engaged in or incurred by the Seller or its affiliates prior to the Closing; and (b) the
ancillary agreements refer to a license and collaboration agreement, a supply agreement,
an amendment to the supply agreement, a safety data exchange agreement and a quality
agreement, each in respect of the Acquired Assets entered into between the Seller and
the Licensor (being Milestone Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ: MIST), a third party
independent of the Company and its connected persons and their respective associates).
The ancillary agreements are currently in force and are operational and compliance￾related agreements that are customary for licensing arrangements in the pharmaceutical
industry. The assignment of the ancillary agreements enables the Group to assume the
Seller’s rights, interests, claims, duties, obligations and liabilities under the existing
contractual framework with the Licensor in respect of the Products, and to ensure
continuity of supply, safety, quality and regulatory responsibilities between the Licensor
and the licensee and enable the parties to continue to invest in the commercialization of
the Products. More specifically, (a) the supply agreement sets out the commercial and
operational terms on which the relevant Product, substance, materials or related supplies
are manufactured and supplied by or on behalf of the Licensor for use by the licensee;
(b) the safety data exchange agreement sets out the allocation of responsibilities

between the Licensor and the licensee for the exchange of safety information and
compliance with applicable pharmacovigilance obligations; and (c) the quality
agreement sets out the allocation of responsibilities between the Licensor and the
licensee in relation to the testing, release, storage, handling and distribution of the
relevant Product or materials.
(ii) The obligations of the Buyer at Closing are subject to the fulfillment, on or before the
Closing as the case may be, among others, to the consent of the Licensor on the
assignment of the Assigned Contracts which, as set out in the Announcement, shall be
given in a written side letter, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to the
Buyer, duly executed and delivered by the Licensor, Seller and Buyer. As at the date of
this announcement, such condition has been satisfied.
(iii) The ultimate beneficial owner of the Seller is Roderick Wong, MD, the managing
partner and chief investment officer of RTW Investments, LP. He is also the sole
director of RTW Holding Limited, which is a controlling shareholder of the Seller. He
is a third party independent of the Company and its connected persons.
(iv) As set out in the Announcement, the consideration was determined taking into account
(i) the status of the development of the Product; and (ii) the future prospects of the
development and commercialization of the Product in the Territory, based on
addressable patient population, unmet medical needs as well as discount taking into
account the probability of success. In particular, the Board considered that the Product
has already been approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), becoming
the first and only self-administered nasal spray in more than 30 years capable of
converting PSVT to sinus rhythm in adults. In China, the NDA was accepted by the
National Medical Products Administration on 17 January 2025 and is expected to
receive approval in the third quarter of 2026. PSVT is a large and significantly
underserved market in China, with an estimated 3 to 6 million patients. The Product
provides an innovative on-demand treatment option that empowers patients to manage
episodes anytime and anywhere. It is currently the only therapy designed for at-home
self-administration to enable the acute termination of PSVT, addressing a significant
unmet medical need among patients in China. The consideration was adjusted by a risk
discount associated with the probability of achieving clinical developments and
regulatory approvals of the Product and the potential amounts payable under the
Assigned Contracts. The parties took a commercial and holistic approach in arriving at
the consideration, which reflects the value of acquiring a fully operational in-licensed
asset and the overall economic exposure and benefits assumed by the Company from the
Asset Purchase Agreement.
