DISCLOSEABLE TRANSACTIONS
SUBSCRIPTIONS OF WEALTH MANAGEMENT PRODUCTS
BACKGROUND
The Board hereby announces that, on April 29, 2026, the Company and Insilico Hong Kong (a 
wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company) placed orders to subscribe for the following Wealth 
Management Products:
(i) the Company placed an order to subscribe for the CMB International USD Money Market 
Fund managed by CMB International Asset Management Limited in the principal amount 
of USD50 million;
(ii) Insilico Hong Kong placed an order to subscribe for the USD Standard Money Market 
VNAV Fund managed by JPMorgan Asset Management (Europe) S.a.r.l in the principal 
amount of USD50 million; and
(iii) Insilico Hong Kong placed an order to subscribe for the USD / Offshore RMB Dual￾Currency Deposit issued by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., Hong Kong Branch in the 
principal amount of USD5 million.
Prior to the Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products, on April 14, 2026, Insilico Hong 
Kong placed an order to subscribe for the aforementioned USD Standard Money Market VNAV 
Fund managed by JPMorgan Asset Management (Europe) S.a.r.l in the principal amount of 
USD2 million, the outstanding balance of which, immediately before the Subscriptions of Wealth 
Management Products, was USD2 million.
The Company used internal resources (without using any proceeds from the Global Offering) 
to fund the Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products. The Company’s bank balances and 
cash and financial assets at fair value through profit and loss increased from approximately 
USD447 million as at December 31, 2025, to approximately USD596 million as at the date of 
this announcement. Such increase was mainly attributable to cash generated from the Group’s 
revenue, including pipeline development upfront payments, milestone payments and software 
subscription fees.

BASIS OF DETERMINATION FOR THE CONSIDERATION
The Board confirms that the considerations of the Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products 
were determined on the basis of commercial terms negotiated at arm’s length between the Group 
and the counterparties, having considered (i) the then available surplus cash of the Group for 
treasury management purposes; (ii) the expected investment return; and (iii) the prevailing market 
interest rates and practices.
REASONS AND BENEFITS OF THE SUBSCRIPTIONS OF WEALTH MANAGEMENT 
PRODUCTS
Prior to the Global Offering, the Company had invested in a money market fund managed by China 
Asset Management (Hong Kong) Limited, namely the ChinaAMC Select USD Money Market Fund 
(Class A, USD), with the principal amount of USD30 million, which was fully redeemed on April 
21, 2026.

[PDF PAGE 6]
6
The funds used for the Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products were derived from the 
Company’s internal resources (without using any proceeds from the Global Offering), including 
proceeds from the aforesaid redemption and other operating income derived from the Company’s 
ordinary course of business. Taking into account the Group’s anticipated operational needs, 
the Board considered that, at the relevant time, such funds were not immediately required for 
operational use and could be managed in a prudent manner pending their utilisation. The Board 
has carefully assessed and evaluated the risks and expected returns of the Subscriptions of Wealth 
Management Products, together with the Group’s future capital requirements, and is of the view 
that the Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products will not adversely affect the Group’s 
normal working capital position, day-to-day operations or the development of its principal 
businesses.
The Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products were made as part of the Group’s prudent 
treasury and cash management activities, with a view to enhancing returns on surplus cash while 
maintaining a high level of liquidity and capital preservation. Two of the Wealth Management 
Products invest primarily in USD-denominated short-term deposits and high-quality money 
market instruments with low risk, while the Dual Currency Deposit is linked to the USD/Offshore 
RMB exchange rate and is designed to provide enhanced deposit returns with flexibility to 
meet the Group’s anticipated RMB-denominated operating needs. The Group expects that the 
Wealth Management Products will generate returns that are more favorable than those offered 
by traditional fixed-term bank deposits, while at the same time providing flexibility in treasury 
management and supporting the Group’s operational funding requirements.
The Directors (including independent non-executive Directors) are of the view that the terms 
of the Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products are on normal commercial terms, fair and 
reasonable, and that the Subscriptions of Wealth Management Products are in the interests of the 
Company and its shareholders as a whole.
