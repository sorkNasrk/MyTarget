VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT –
REGULAR APPROVAL OF DEUREMIDEVIR HYDROBROMIDE TABLETS 
BY THE NATIONAL MEDICAL PRODUCTS ADMINISTRATION
This announcement is made by Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海君實生物醫藥科技股
份有限公司) (the “Company”) on a voluntary basis.
The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company is pleased to announce 
that Shanghai Vinnerna Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海旺實生物醫藥科技有限公司), a 
subsidiary controlled by the Company, has received the Notice of Approval for Supplemental 
Drug Application* (《藥品補充申請批准通知書》) issued by the National Medical Products 
Administration (the “NMPA”). The NMPA has approved for the conditional approval for 
the indication for Deuremidevir Hydrobromide Tablets (氫溴酸氘瑞米德韋片) (trade name: 
MINDEWEI*(民得維®), product code: VV116/JT001) as the treatment of adult patients with mild 
to moderate coronavirus disease 2019 (“COVID-19”) to be converted to regular approval. Relevant 
details are set out below:
ABOUT DEUREMIDEVIR HYDROBROMIDE TABLETS
Drug name: Deuremidevir Hydrobromide Tablets
Application matter: Application for conversion from conditional approval to regular approval for 
marketing
Acceptance No.: CYHB2401370
Notice No.: 2025B00098
Marketing Authorization Holder: Shanghai Vinnerna Biosciences Co., Ltd.*
Review conclusions: According to the Drug Administration Law of the People’s Republic of 
China* (《中華人民共和國藥品管理法》) and relevant requirements, upon review, the application 
for the drug satisfied the relevant requirements for drug registration and the following supplemental 
application matters are approved: 1. It is agreed that the research information submitted by the 
applicant under the condition for the marketing of the drug has satisfied relevant requirements; 2. 
It is agreed that the approval for the drug shall be converted from conditional approval to regular 
approval for marketing.

On 28 January 2023, based on a multi-center, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled phase 
III clinical study (JT001-015 Study, NCT05582629) conducted among mild to moderate COVID-19 
patients with or without high risk for progression to severe COVID-19, MINDEWEI received the 
conditional approval for marketing by the NMPA. 
The regular approval for MINDEWEI is mainly based on a number of clinical and non-clinical 
studies, including the JT001-015 Study and a real-world study enrolling nearly 8,000 patients. 
Study results have shown that, among patients with mild and moderate COVID-19, MINDEWEI 
significantly facilitates symptoms alleviation and resolution, shortens course of disease, accelerates 
the virus to negative, and reduces the incidence of severe COVID-19 cases or all-cause death, 
especially for elderly and high-risk patients. Among patients with mild and moderate hepatic 
or renal dysfunction, MINDEWEI exhibited satisfactory safety and tolerability, and patients 
do not need to adjust, or only need to slightly adjust, their dosage when taking the medication. 
Meanwhile, the pharmacological study showed that MINDEWEI basically does not have drug-drug 
interactions, greatly reducing medication contraindications, effectively ensuring the combined use 
of medications by patients, and providing a safer and more effective choice for groups with special 
needs.
MINDEWEI is an oral nucleoside analog drug that inhibits the replication of SARS-CoV-2. 
Related studies have shown that MINDEWEI exhibited significant antiviral effects against both 
the original SARS-CoV-2 strain and mutant strains, including Omicron XBB.1.16, EG.5 and JN.1, 
and exhibited no genetic toxicity. MINDEWEI was jointly developed by Shanghai Institute of 
Materia Medica, Chinese Academy of Sciences* (中國科學院上海藥物研究所), Wuhan Institute 
of Virology, Chinese Academy of Sciences* (中國科學院武漢病毒研究所), Xinjiang Technical 
Institute of Physics and Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences* (中國科學院新疆理化技
術研究所), Central Asian Center of Drug Discovery and Development of Chinese Academy of 
Sciences* (中國科學院中亞藥物研發中心)/China-Uzbekistan Medicine Technical Park (the Belt 
and Road Joint Laboratory of the Ministry of Science and Technology)* (中烏醫藥科技城(科技
部“一帶一路”聯合實驗室)), Lingang Laboratory* (臨港實驗室), Suzhou Vigonvita Biomedical 
Co., Ltd.* (蘇州旺山旺水生物醫藥有限公司) and the Company. MINDEWEI was included in the 
scope of provisional medical insurance reimbursement in January 2023, and has been officially 
included in the National Reimbursement Drug List since January 2024.
