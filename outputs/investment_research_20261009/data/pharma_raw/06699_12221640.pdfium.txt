PROPOSED TERMINATION OF THE POST-IPO SHARE OPTION SCHEME
Reference is made to the announcement of the Company dated March 21, 2024 and August 25, 
2025 and the circular of the Company dated April 23, 2024 relating to the Post-IPO Share Option 
Scheme.
The Post-IPO Share Option Scheme was conditionally approved and adopted by the Shareholders 
on May 20, 2021, and was amended on June 29, 2023, June 28, 2024 and August 25, 2025. 
The purpose of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme is to attract, retain and motivate employees, 
Directors and such other participants, and to provide a means of compensating them through the 
grant of options pursuant to the terms of such scheme for their contribution to the growth and 
profits of the Group, and to allow such employees, Directors and other persons to participate in the 
growth and profitability of the Group. For further details of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme, 
please refer to the circular of the Company dated April 23, 2024 and the announcement of the 
Company dated August 25, 2025, respectively.
According to the terms of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme, the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme 
is valid and effective for a period of ten (10) years from the date of adoption. Nevertheless, the 
Company may terminate the operation of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme at any time by 
resolution of the Board or resolution of the Shareholders in general meeting and in such event no 
further Option will be offered but the provisions of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme shall remain 
in full force and effect to the extent necessary to give effect to the exercise of the Options (to the 
extent not already exercised) granted prior to the termination or otherwise as may be required in 
accordance with the provisions of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme. Options (to the extent not 
already exercised) granted prior to such termination shall continue to be valid and exercisable in 
accordance with the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme.

Proposed Amendments to the Post-IPO RSU Scheme
The Post-IPO RSU Scheme was conditionally approved and adopted by the Shareholders on May 
20, 2021, and was amended on June 29, 2023, June 28, 2024 and August 25, 2025. The purpose 
of the Post-IPO RSU Scheme is to recognize the contributions by grantees and to give incentives 
thereto in order to retain them for the continual operation and development of the Group, and 
to attract suitable personnel for further development of the Group. For further details of the 
Post-IPO RSU Scheme, please refer to the circular of the Company dated April 23, 2024 and the 
announcement of the Company dated August 25, 2025, respectively.
In light of the near full utilization of the Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit and the proposed termination 
of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme, and in order to provide effective long-term incentives to 
Directors, management and employees of the Group, the Board is pleased to announce that it has 
resolved to increase the Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit from 2% to 8% of the issued share capital 
(excluding treasury shares) as at the Amendment Date. The Board will prudently consider a range 
of factors, including market conditions, the Group’s incentive needs and operational performance, 
in determining the amount of potential grant under the refreshed Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit, and 
it is expected that the aggregate grants to be made over the next three years will not exceed 4.5% 
of the issued share capital (excluding treasury shares) as at the Amendment Date.
Save as disclosed above, there is no other substantive change to the rules of the Post-IPO RSU 
Scheme. Further details of the proposed amendments to the Post-IPO RSU Scheme will be set out 
in a circular to be despatched to the Shareholders in due course.

Proposed Refresh of the Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit
As at the date of this announcement, only 612,173 Shares were available for future grant of Shares 
under the Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit representing approximately 0.36% of the total number of 
Shares in issue (excluding treasury shares) as at the Latest Refreshment Date, and approximately 
0.36% of the Shares of the Company in issue as at the date of this announcement. In light of 
the near full utilization of the Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit and the proposed termination of the 
Post-IPO Share Option Scheme, the Company proposes to convene the EGM at which ordinary 
resolution(s) will be proposed to the Independent Shareholders to consider and, if thought fit, 
approve the proposed refreshment of the Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit so as to allow the Company 
to grant new share awards under the Post-IPO RSU Scheme up to 8% of the issued share capital of 
the Company (excluding treasury Shares) as at the date of the EGM. The total number of Shares 
which may be issued in respect of all share awards to be granted under the Post-IPO RSU Scheme 
and any other schemes of the Company as “refreshed” is thus 8% must not exceed 10% of the 
relevant class of shares of the Company in issue (excluding treasury shares) as at the date of the 
EGM.
The Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors) consider that (i) the 
proposed terms relating to the scope of eligible participants, the vesting period and performance 
arrangement, the purchase price determination and clawback mechanism as set forth in the terms 
of the Fourth Amended Post-IPO RSU Scheme and (ii) the proposed refresh of the Post-IPO RSU 
Scheme Limit are in line with the purpose of the Fourth Amended Post-IPO RSU Scheme because 
both proposals incentivize employees of the Company who are critical for the business of the 
Company, to perform better and to maintain long-term relationship with the Company, aligning 
their interests with the success of the Company.
Adoption conditions for the Fourth Amended Post-IPO RSU Scheme and the refresh of the 
Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit
The adoption of the proposed amendments to the Post-IPO RSU Scheme is conditional upon the 
fulfillment of the following conditions:
(a) the passing of ordinary resolutions by the Shareholders at the EGM approving the proposed 
amendments to the Post-IPO RSU Scheme and the refresh of the Post-IPO RSU Scheme 
Limit; and
(b) the Listing Committee of the Stock Exchange granting or confirming the approval for the 
listing of, and permission to deal in, all the new Shares which may be allotted and issued 
under the Scheme Mandate Limit.

EGM
An EGM will be convened for the Shareholders to consider, and if thought fit, approve, inter alia, 
(i) proposed termination of the Post-IPO Share Option Scheme; and (ii) proposed amendments to 
the Post-IPO RSU Scheme and refresh of the Post-IPO RSU Scheme Limit.
Pursuant to Rule 17.03C(1) of the Listing Rules, any refreshment of the scheme mandate limit 
within three years from the date of shareholders’ approval for the last refreshment must be 
approved by independent shareholders of the Company. Considering that the scheme limit of 
the Post-IPO RSU Scheme and Post-IPO Share Option Scheme was refreshed on June 28, 2024, 
CareCapital Orthotech Limited, the controlling shareholder of the Company will abstain from 
voting in favor of the resolution approving the proposed amendments to the Post-IPO RSU 
Scheme at the general meeting. To the best knowledge, information and belief of the Directors, 
no other Shareholders are required to abstain from voting on the relevant resolution at the EGM. 
CareCapital Orthotech Limited did not provide any intention to vote against the relevant resolution 
at the EGM. When the Company subsequently becomes aware of that CareCapital Orthotech 
Limited may change its minds as to whether to abstain or vote against the resolution before the 
date of the EGM, the Company will immediately despatch a circular to the Shareholders or publish 
an announcement in accordance with Rule 2.07C of the Listing Rules notifying the Shareholders of 
the change and, if known, the reason for such change.
Further details of the aforementioned proposals, together with a notice of the EGM, will be 
published on the websites of the Stock Exchange (www.hkexnews.hk) and of the Company 
(www.angelalign.com) in accordance with the requirements of the Listing Rules in due course.
As of the date of this announcement, the adoption of the Fourth Amended Post-IPO RSU 
Scheme remains subject to the approval of the Shareholders. Shareholders and potential 
investors are advised to exercise caution when dealing in the Shares.
