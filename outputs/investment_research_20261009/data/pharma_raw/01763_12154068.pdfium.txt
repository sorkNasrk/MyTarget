VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
CONTRACT ON SECONDARY STANDARDIZED NEUTRON 
DOSIMETRY LABORATORY PROJECT TECHNICAL SERVICE 
ENTERED INTO BY CNGT, A SUBSIDIARY OF CIRC, 
AND CHINA NATIONAL NUCLEAR
WITH PONDPOL
This announcement is voluntarily made by China Isotope & Radiation Corporation (“CIRC” or 
the “Company”) to keep its shareholders and potential investors informed of the latest business 
developments of the Company.
The board of directors of the Company is pleased to announce that recently, Chengdu Gaotong 
Isotope Co., Ltd. (CNNC) (“CNGT”, a subsidiary of CIRC) and China National Nuclear 
Corporation Overseas Ltd. (中國中原對外工程有限公司) (“China National Nuclear”) have 
entered into the Contract on Secondary Standardized Neutron Dosimetry Laboratory Project 
Technical Service (the “Project”) with Pondpol Analytical Co., Ltd. (“Pondpol”). China National 
Nuclear is a wholly-owned subsidiary of China National Nuclear Corporation, the controlling 
shareholder of the Company, and Pondpol is an independent third party of the Company. Under 
the Project, CNGT is primarily responsible for providing the required products and related services 
to Pondpol, while China National Nuclear is mainly responsible for project management and 
supervision, and will not participate in the provision of related products and services. Both parties 
respectively receive consideration and assume liabilities for the services they provide, and there 
is no arrangement for joint performance or joint liability. Accordingly, the transactions under the 
Project do not constitute transactions under Chapter 14 or Chapter 14A of the Rules Governing the 
Listing of Securities on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited.
This Project undertakes important tasks including the calibration of neutron radiation dose 
measuring instruments and related scientific research experiments, representing a key infrastructure 
project for Thailand to bolster its application capabilities of nuclear technology and improve its 
national metrology system.
