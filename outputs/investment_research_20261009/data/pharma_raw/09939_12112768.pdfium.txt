VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
BUSINESS UPDATE
This is a voluntary announcement made by Kintor Pharmaceutical Limited (the “Company”, 
together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) to update its shareholders and potential 
investors on the latest developments related to the Group.
The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company is pleased to 
announce that the Company’s B2C and B2B sales business has grown rapidly and steadily 
since the beginning of 2026. According to the Company’s preliminary statistics, as of the 
date of this announcement, the cumulative realized sales revenue for the year has exceeded 
the sales revenue for the whole year of 2025.
As of the date of this announcement, the Company’s sales revenue for the year has reached 
approximately RMB34 million, exceeding the total sales revenue of RMB32.68 million for 
the whole last year, and is expected to complete the annual target of exceeding RMB150 
million, mainly comprising B2C domestic e-commerce sales, overseas e-commerce sales 
and B2B raw material sales.
In the B2C domestic e-commerce segment, the sales revenue in the first quarter of this year 
increased by 25 times compared to the same period last year. In particular, since the launch 
of the Company’s in-house live-streaming sales business 5 months ago, the sales revenue 
has continued to grow, with an average daily sales of nearly RMB100,000 this month, 
setting a record for a single-day gross merchandise value (GMV) of RMB400,000.
In the B2C overseas e-commerce segment, the sales revenue in the first quarter of this year 
increased by 150% compared to the same period last year.

In the B2B raw material sales segment, the sales revenue in the first quarter of this year was 
RMB3.44 million, compared with zero in the same period last year, which also far exceeded 
the sales revenue for the whole last year.
The Company plans to use the cash flow generated from the cosmetic products and raw 
materials business to fund the development and commercialization of the Company’s core 
products KX-826 and GT20029 and other pipeline products (the “Listing Business”). 
Meanwhile, the Company will continue to adhere to the established policy of its listing 
business.
Shareholders and potential investors of the Company are advised to exercise caution 
when dealing in the shares of the Company.
