The Board is pleased to announce that on August 29, 2025 (before trading hours), the 
Company entered into the Placing Agreement with the Placing Agents, pursuant to which 
the Company agreed to issue, under the General Mandate, and the Placing Agents agreed, 
severally and not jointly or jointly and severally, as agents of the Company, to procure on 
a best efforts basis the Placees to subscribe for a total of up to 285,920,000 Placing Shares 
at the Placing Price of HK$9.28 per Placing Share subject to the terms and conditions of 
the Placing Agreement.
The number of Placing Shares represents approximately 7.12% of the existing issued 
share capital of the Company as at the date of this announcement and approximately 
6.64% of the enlarged issued share capital of the Company immediately following 
Completion (assuming all the Placing Shares are subscribed for in full and there is no 
change in the issued share capital of the Company between the date of this announcement 
and Completion).
The Placing Price of HK$9.28 per Placing Share represents (i) a discount of approximately 
7.75% to the closing price of HK$10.06 per Share as quoted on the Stock Exchange on 
August 28, 2025, being the date on which the Placing Price was fixed; (ii) a discount of 
approximately 8.10% to the average closing price of HK$10.098 per Share as quoted on 
the Stock Exchange for the five consecutive trading days immediately prior to August 
28, 2025, being the date on which the Placing Price was fixed; and (iii) a premium of 
approximately 23.40% over the average closing price of HK$7.52 per Share as quoted on 
the Stock Exchange for the thirty consecutive trading days immediately prior to August 
28, 2025, being the date on which the Placing Price was fixed.
The Placing Shares are to be issued under the General Mandate. As such, the allotment 
and issue of the Placing Shares is not subject to additional Shareholders’ approval.
Assuming all the 285,920,000 Placing Shares are subscribed for in full, upon Completion, 
the gross proceeds to be raised from the Placing will be approximately HK$2,653.3 
million and the net proceeds (after deducting all applicable costs and expenses, including 
commission and levies) arising from the Placing are estimated to be approximately 
HK$2,629.8 million. On this basis, the net price per Placing Share will be approximately 
HK$9.20.
Application will be made to the Stock Exchange for the approval for the listing of, and 
permission to deal in, the Placing Shares.
As completion of the Placing is subject to the satisfaction or, if applicable, waiver 
of the Conditions, the Placing may or may not proceed. Shareholders and potential 
investors should exercise caution when dealing in the securities of the Company.

REASONS FOR THE PLACING AND USE OF PROCEEDS
The Company is an investment holding company. The Group is principally engaged in the 
provision of intelligent robotics solutions and drug discovery solutions.
In the January Placing and the February Placing, the Company raised total net proceeds 
of approximately HK$3,205 million for (i) continuous product iterations and upgrades to 
enhance its research and development technological capabilities and solution capabilities, 
and data generation and model building; (ii) product commercialisation and business 
development, strengthening business collaborations with external parties and partners which 
are engaged in similar and mutually beneficial industries as the Group, and expanding the 
scale and market share of the Company; and (iii) acquisitions and investing in potential 
opportunities, talent attraction and introduction, working capital replenishment and general 
corporate purposes.
In June 2025, British drugmaker AstraZeneca entered into a strategic collaboration with a 
Chinese pharmaceutical company to advance the discovery and development of novel oral 
drugs, utilizing an AI-driven, dual-engine efficient drug discovery platform. Separately, 
in late June, Xtalpi secured a landmark partnership worth nearly US$6 billion. These 
collaborations demonstrate AI’s increasingly critical role in drug discovery. Additionally, in 
early August this year, a publicly listed Chinese pharmaceutical company announced plans 
to test a tokenization project in Hong Kong. The initiative will use future revenue rights 
from innovative drug development as the base asset, prompting life science companies to 
explore Real-World Assets (RWA) tokenization.
In addition, the Group’s revenue for the year ended December 31, 2024, as generated from 
the provision of (i) drug discovery solutions and (ii) intelligent robotics solutions, had reach 
the revenue threshold of a Commercial Company (as defined under Chapter 18C of the 
Listing Rules), being HK$250,000,000. Such revenue had been due to the Group’s business 
developments and market opportunities during the year ended 31 December 2024.
Assuming all the 285,920,000 Placing Shares are subscribed for in full, upon Completion, 
the gross proceeds to be raised from the Placing will be approximately HK$2,653.3 
million and the net proceeds (after deducting all applicable costs and expenses, including 
commission and levies) arising from the Placing are estimated to be approximately 
HK$2,629.8 million. On this basis, the net price per Placing Share will be approximately 
HK$9.20.

In view of the strong performance of the Company and the optimistic market outlook and 
prospects, the Company believes that it is an opportune timing to raise additional capital to 
be applied towards the following purposes:
(i) continuous product iterations and upgrades to enhance its research and development 
technological capabilities and solution capabilities;
(ii) product commercialisation and business development, strengthening business 
collaborations with external parties and partners which are engaged in similar and 
mutually beneficial industries as the Group, and expanding the scale and market share 
of the Company; and
(iii) acquisitions and investing in potential opportunities, talent attraction and introduction, 
working capital replenishment and general corporate purposes.
With the net proceeds raised from the Placing, for each of the purposes set out above, 
the Company may, after considering the best interests of the Group’s business and the 
Shareholders, either increase in the size of its investment or commitment in the projects 
as previously contemplated and/or envisaged as at the time of the January Placing or the 
February Placing, and/or may invest in or commit to further projects and/or initiatives in 
connection with the relevant purpose. Such further projects and/or initiatives may include:
1. acquisition and/or investment into intelligent robotics solution, and drug discovery 
solution;
2. increase in data generation and model building;
3. exploring strategic opportunities in the fields of Web 3.0 and/or Real-World Assets; 
and
4. start-up investment and incubation ecosystem building.
The Group’s AI + robotics digital drug development platform is a natural fit and 
synergistic partner with its Real-World Assets (RWA) and Real-Data Assets (RDA) 
trading platforms. By integrating resources and technologies, a new financial ecosystem 
in the biopharmaceutical sector is built, promoting industry innovation and sustainable 
development.

[PDF PAGE 11]
– 11 –
The Company plans to use a portion of the net proceeds from the Placing to explore 
innovative financial tools such as Real-World Assets (RWA) and Real-Data Assets (RDA) 
to empower innovative drug development. This aims to significantly improve capital 
utilization efficiency during the development of innovative drug pipeline.
Over the past decade, the Group has leveraged AI and robotics to enhance drug development 
efficiency, while Real-World Assets (RWA) and Real-Data Assets (RDA) can enhance 
transaction efficiency in the pharmaceutical industry. Both research and development 
efficiency and transaction efficiency are obviously equally important, and thus the Company 
plans to develop the relevant underlying infrastructure systems.
The Company currently expects that the net proceeds from the Placing will be primarily 
allocated towards categories (i) and (iii) of the abovementioned purposes and the remaining 
net proceeds towards category (ii) of the abovementioned purposes. Among the above four 
categories of further projects and/or initiatives which may be pursued by the Group, the 
Company currently expects to put more emphasis on categories (1), (2) and (4) so more 
funds may be invested in projects or initiatives in such categories. The Company currently 
expects to utilize such net proceeds for the above intended uses in the coming five to ten 
years. Such allocation and expected time frame are based on the Directors’ best estimation 
barring unforeseen circumstances, and would be subject to change based on the future 
development of the Group, the market conditions and availability of business (including 
technical) development and investment opportunities.
To the extent that the net proceeds from the Placing are not immediately used for the 
purposes described above, the Company may hold such proceeds in short-term deposits or 
purchase short-term wealth management products so long as it is deemed to be in the best 
interests of the Company.
The Directors consider that apart from being an opportunity for the Company to raise further 
capital, the Placing can also bring about further broadening of its Shareholder base. The 
Directors are of the view that the Placing would further strengthen the financial position of 
the Group and provide further working capital to the Group.
