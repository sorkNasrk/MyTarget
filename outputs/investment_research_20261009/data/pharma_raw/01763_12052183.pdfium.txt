VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
CIRC AND ITS SUBSIDIARY ZHONGHE HEADWAY ENTERED INTO 
STRATEGIC COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH THE PEOPLE’S 
GOVERNMENT OF MAOMING MUNICIPALITY
This announcement is voluntarily made by China Isotope & Radiation Corporation (“CIRC” or 
the “Company”) to keep its shareholders and potential investors informed of the latest business 
developments of the Company.
The board of directors of the Company is pleased to announce that, on 13 March, CIRC and its 
subsidiary, Zhonghe Headway, entered into a strategic cooperation agreement with the People’s 
Government of Maoming Municipality, Guangdong Province.
Pursuant to the strategic cooperation agreement, the three parties have entered into a comprehensive 
strategic partnership to jointly advance the agglomeration and implementation of the nuclear 
technology application industry in the western Guangdong region, and to transform Maoming 
into an important stable isotope R&D and production base and a nuclear medicine application 
demonstration zone in the Southern China region. Leveraging their respective advantages, the three 
parties will focus on tackling key technologies for stable isotopes, while enhancing the resilience, 
safety, and security capabilities of China’s nuclear technology application industry chain.
