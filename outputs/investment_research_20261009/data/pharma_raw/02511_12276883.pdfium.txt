COMPLETION OF PLACING OF NEW SHARES UNDER GENERAL MANDATE
Sole Overall Coordinator and Sole Placing Agent
Reference is made to the announcement of HighTide Therapeutics, Inc. (the “Company”) dated 
July 31, 2026 in respect of the Placing (the “Announcement”). Unless otherwise specified, 
capitalized terms used herein have the same meanings as those defined in the Announcement.
COMPLETION OF PLACING
The Board is pleased to announce that all conditions of the Placing have been fulfilled and the 
completion of the Placing took place on August 7, 2026 in accordance with the terms of the 
Placing Agreement. A total of 98,710,000 Placing Shares have been successfully placed by the 
Sole Placing Agent to not less than six independent placees at the Placing Price of HK$2.28 per 
Placing Share pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Placing Agreement.
To the best of the Directors’ knowledge, information and belief having made all reasonable 
enquiries, (i) the placees and their respective ultimate beneficial owner(s) are Independent Third 
Parties; and (ii) none of the placees has become a substantial shareholder (as defined under the 
Listing Rules) of the Company immediately upon completion of the Placing.
The aggregate gross proceeds from the Placing are approximately HK$225.1 million and the 
aggregate net proceeds from the Placing to be received by the Company (after deduction of the 
commissions and expenses relating to the Placing) amounted to approximately HK$222.4 million.

The Company intends to utilize the net proceeds from the Placing as follows:
(i) approximately 30% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$66.72 million, 
for the commercialization of HTD1801, the Group’s core pipeline product, in its current 
indication of type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM);
(ii) approximately 45% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$100.09 million, 
for the expansion of HTD1801’s development into new indications, including but not limited 
to chronic kidney disease (CKD);
(iii) approximately 15% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$33.36 million, 
for the research and development of the Group’s other drug candidates; and
(iv) approximately 10% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$22.24 million, 
will be used for working capital and other general corporate purposes.
The Company expects the net proceeds from the Placing to be fully utilized by the end of 2028.
