VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
ENTERING INTO EQUITY TRANSFER AGREEMENT TO FURTHER 
EXPAND THE NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING BUSINESS LAYOUT
This announcement is made on a voluntary basis by China Isotope & Radiation Corporation 
(“CIRC” or the “Company”) to keep shareholders and potential investors informed of the latest 
business developments of the Company.
The board of directors of the Company is pleased to announce that on 19 March, Chengdu Gaotong 
Isotope Co., Ltd. (CNNC) (成都中核高通同位素股份有限公司) (“CNGT”), a subsidiary of 
CIRC, entered into an equity transfer agreement to acquire 51% equity interest of Hunan Huifeng 
Engineering Detection Co., Ltd. (湖南滙豐工程檢測有限公司) (“Huifeng Detection”). Upon 
completion of the transaction, Huifeng Detection will become a controlling subsidiary of the 
Company and will be consolidated into the consolidated statements.
Huifeng Detection is mainly engaged in special equipment inspection and testing services and 
is one of the institutions with relatively comprehensive qualifications and a higher level in the 
national special equipment non-destructive testing industry, possessing 9 complete qualifications 
under the inspection and testing institution approval certificate of special equipment of the People’s 
Republic of China: four types of CG basic testing including “radiographic testing (RT), ultrasonic 
testing (UT), penetrant testing (PT) and magnetic particle testing (MT)”, eddy current testing 
(ECT), acoustic emission (AE) testing, time of flight diffraction (TOFD), phased array ultrasonic 
testing (PAUT), magnetic flux leakage (MFL) (including magnetic flux leakage of tank bottom 
plates, internal and external magnetic flux leakage of pipelines); periodic inspection of public 
pipelines (DD2) and periodic inspection of industrial pipelines (DD3); safety valve calibration; 
as well as the CMA qualification certificate for inspection and testing institutions, the laboratory 
accreditation certificate from the China National Accreditation Service for Conformity Assessment 
(CNAS) and a comprehensive operation management system. It has a broad customer base in the 
fields of petrochemicals, metallurgy, aerospace, rail transit and electric power, with its business 
network covering most regions of China.

Anchoring on the positioning of “a world-class system integration supplier of radioactive sources 
and application technology”, CNGT focuses on four directions including isotopes, radioactive 
sources, equipment containing radioactive sources and application services for equipment 
containing radioactive sources, and is committed to building a full-chain industrial ecosystem 
of “radioactive source – equipment containing radioactive sources – back-end service”. CNGT 
occupies a dominant position in the domestic non-destructive testing radioactive source market and 
holds the vast majority of the market share. This transaction represents another expansion of the 
non-destructive testing business layout by CNGT since the acquisition of Xi’an Zhanshi Testing 
& Engineering Co., Ltd. (西安展實檢測工程有限公司) in 2023, and will assist it in further 
expanding its market share in segmented areas, enhancing its comprehensive competitive strength, 
and steadily elevating its market position and core influence in the industry.
