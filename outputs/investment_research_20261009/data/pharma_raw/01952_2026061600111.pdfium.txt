VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
AGREEMENT WITH DIMERIX TO DEVELOP AND
COMMERCIALIZE DMX-200 IN GREATER CHINA,
SOUTH KOREA AND SOUTHEAST ASIA
This announcement is made by Everest Medicines Limited (the ‘‘Company’’) on a voluntary
basis.
The board (the ‘‘Board’’) of directors (the ‘‘Directors’’) of the Company is pleased to
announce that on 16 June 2026, the Company entered into an agreement (the ‘‘Agreement’’)
with Dimerix Limited (‘‘Dimerix’’), a company listed on Australian Stock Exchange under
the symbol ‘‘DXB’’, pursuant to which Dimerix irrevocably granted to the Company an
exclusive license to develop and commercialize DMX-200, a small molecule inhibitor of the
chemokine receptor 2 (CCR2) under development in a pivotal, Phase 3 study, ACTION3, for
the treatment of focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (‘‘FSGS’’), in Greater China, South
Korea and certain Southeast Asian countries. DMX-200 has received orphan drug
designations from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the European
Medicines Agency (EMA).
Under the exclusive license, the Company’s payment obligations include: (i) an upfront
payment of US$10 million; and (ii) potential development and regulatory milestone
payments of up to US$30 million; (iii) potential sales milestone payments of up to US$300
million; and (iv) potential tiered royalties between 10% to 15% based on future annual net
sales.
This transaction is expected to further strengthen the Company’s nephrology product
portfolio and pipeline synergy, reinforce the Company’s strategic position in kidney and
autoimmune diseases.

ABOUT DMX-200
DMX-200 is a small molecule inhibitor of the chemokine receptor 2 (CCR2) under
development in a pivotal, Phase 3 study, ACTION3, for the treatment of FSGS. DMX-200
has received orphan drug designations from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
and the European Medicines Agency (EMA).
ABOUT DIMERIX
Dimerix (ASX: DXB) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company working to improve the
lives of patients with inflammatory diseases, including kidney diseases. Dimerix is currently
focused on developing its proprietary Phase 3 product candidate DMX-200, for Focal
Segmental Glomerulosclerosis (FSGS) kidney disease. DMX-200 was identified using
Dimerix’ proprietary assay, Receptor Heteromer Investigation Technology (Receptor-HIT),
which is a scalable and globally applicable technology platform, enabling the understanding
of receptor interactions to rapidly screen and identify new drug opportunities. For more
information, please visit the Dimerix’s website at www.dimerix.com.
