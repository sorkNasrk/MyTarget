VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
UPDATE ON THE RENEWAL STATUS OF THE LEASE OF THE PROPERTY
This announcement is made by MicroPort Scientific Corporation (the “Company”, together with its 
subsidiaries, the “Group”) on a voluntary basis.
References are made to the announcements of the Company dated 13 May 2021 and 11 June 2021 (the 
“Announcements”) in relation to a discloseable transaction of the Company concerning the leasing of 
the Property (as defined below). Unless otherwise defined, capitalised terms used in this announcement 
have the same meanings as those defined in the Announcements.
THE EXISTING LEASE AGREEMENTS
As set out in the Announcements, on 13 May 2021, the relevant subsidiaries of the Company and Shanghai 
Weichuang Investment Management Co., Ltd.* (上海微創投資管理有限公司) (now renamed as Shanghai 
BlueOrange Capital Corp.* (上海回青橙投資管理有限公司) (“BlueOrange Capital”)) entered into ten 
lease agreements (the “Original Lease Agreements”) pursuant to which such subsidiaries agreed to lease 
from BlueOrange Capital certain premises within the purpose-built industrial facility (the “Property”) 
located at No. 1661, Zhangdong Road, the China (Shanghai) Pilot Free Trade Zone, the PRC (中國（上海）
自由貿易試驗區張東路1661號) with an aggregate area of approximately 126,370 sq.m. for use as office 
and manufacturing purposes. Subsequently, the relevant parties entered into supplemental agreements in 
response to the actual delivery arrangements of the premises within the Property, confirming that the lease 
term would commence on 1 August 2021 (the Original Lease Agreements and the relevant supplemental 
agreements are hereinafter collectively referred to as the “Existing Lease Agreements”).
The principal terms of the Existing Lease Agreements are summarised below:
(a) Term: a term of five years for the period from 1 August 2021 to 31 July 2026;

UPDATE ON THE RENEWAL STATUS OF THE LEASE OF THE PROPERTY
The board of directors (the “Board”) of the Company wishes to inform the Shareholders and potential 
investors that as at the date of this announcement, three subsidiaries of the Company, namely, Shanghai 
MicroPort CardioFlow Medtech Co., Ltd.* (上海微創心通醫療科技有限公司), MicroPort Soaring 
CRM (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.* (創領心律管理醫療器械（上海）有限公司) and Shanghai Yuanxin Medtech 
Co., Ltd.* (上海遠心醫療科技有限公司), have respectively entered into the lease agreements with 
BlueOrange Capital pursuant to which such subsidiaries leased certain premises with an aggregate area 
of approximately 13,872.64 sq.m. within the Property, each with a lease term expiring on 31 July 2031.
As at the date of this announcement, eight other subsidiaries of the Company are in discussions with 
BlueOrange Capital regarding the lease/renewal arrangements for certain other premises with an 
aggregate area of approximately 74,686.84 sq.m. within the Property (the “Proposed Lease”). Having 
reviewed their respective operational requirements and space utilisation, such subsidiaries of the 
Company are in discussions with BlueOrange Capital on the commercial terms of the Proposed Lease 
and no agreement has been reached as at the date of this announcement. In particular, the following key 
terms have not been finalised:
(a) the unit rent per sq.m., the lease term and, accordingly, the aggregate rent payable over the term of 
the leases; and
(b) the arrangements for the security deposit, including the amount of any deposit payable under the 
leases and the timing and manner of refund of the security deposit paid under the Existing Lease 
Agreements (if applicable).
As at the date of this announcement, the Group has maintained security deposits of approximately 
RMB295.99 million in total with BlueOrange Capital for the leasing of the Property. The relevant 
subsidiaries of the Company continue to occupy the Property while the commercial terms of the 
Proposed Lease are being negotiated. The Group is still in the process of assessing the potential impact 
of the above matters on the Group. The Company will comply with the applicable requirements under 
the Listing Rules if the Group and BlueOrange Capital enter into definitive agreement(s) in respect of 
the Proposed Lease, and will make further announcement(s) as and when appropriate in accordance with 
the Listing Rules.
Note: For the purpose of the Announcements, Renminbi has been converted into US dollars at an exchange rate of USD1.00 
to RMB6.4612.

INFORMATION ON BLUEORANGE CAPITAL
BlueOrange Capital is a limited liability company established in the PRC and is principally engaged 
in investment management, investment consulting, production and sales of precision machinery and 
pharmaceutical intermediates (excluding pharmaceutical products), research and development of high￾tech products and related technical services, import and export of goods and technology, residential 
property leasing, non-residential property leasing, and property management. It is an indirect subsidiary 
of Real & Realistic Foundation Limited (“Real & Realistic”). To the best knowledge of the Company, 
Real & Realistic is a company limited by guarantee with no share capital incorporated in Hong Kong.

beneficial owners and ultimate controllers. The current directors of Real & Realistic are Mr. Chen Wei
