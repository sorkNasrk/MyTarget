Reference is made to the circular of China Isotope & Radiation Corporation (the “Company”) 
dated 29 May 2026 (the “Circular”) in relation to, among other things, the convening of the 2025 
annual general meeting (the “AGM”). Capitalised terms used herein shall have the same meanings 
as defined in the Circular.
The Board is pleased to announce the poll results of the AGM held at 10:00 a.m. on Thursday, 
18 June 2026 at Room 403, No. 66 Changwa Middle Street, Haidian District, Beijing, China. The 
AGM was legally and validly convened in compliance with the requirements of the Company Law 
and the Articles of Association, and was chaired by Chairman Xiao Yafei. All the resolutions set 
out in the Circular were voted by way of poll as required under the Rules Governing the Listing of 
Securities on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the “Listing Rules”).
ATTENDANCE OF THE AGM
As at the date of the AGM, the total number of Shares entitling the holders to attend and vote for 
or against the resolutions was 319,874,900 Shares, which was divided into 79,968,800 H Shares 
and 239,906,100 Domestic Shares. The Shareholders in attendance either in person or by proxy at 
the AGM held a total of 254,645,968 Shares, representing approximately 79.61% of the Company’s 
total issued Shares with voting rights.
Pursuant to the Listing Rules, no Shareholders were required to abstain from voting on the 
resolutions considered at the AGM. The Company was also not aware of any person who has 
indicated an intention to vote against any resolution proposed to be put forward at the AGM. Nor 
was there any Shareholder entitled to attend the AGM but was required under Rule 13.40 of the 
Listing Rules to abstain from voting in favour of any of the resolutions.

APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER OF SPECIAL COMMITTEES OF THE BOARD
The Board is pleased to announce that on 18 June 2026, the Board resolved to appoint Mr. Zhang 
Guoliang as a member of Legal Affairs Committee of the Board and a member of Science and 
Technology Innovation Committee of the Board, with his term ending on the expiry of the fourth 
session of the Board.
As at the date of this announcement, there are no changes to the biographical details of Mr. Zhang 
Guoliang disclosed in the Circular.
DISTRIBUTION OF FINAL DIVIDEND
The proposal for the payment of a final dividend of RMB0.2264 per Share (inclusive of tax) 
totaling RMB72,419,677.36 (inclusive of tax) for the year ended 31 December 2025 to the 
Shareholders was approved at the AGM. It is expected that the Final Dividend will be paid in RMB 
to holders of Domestic Shares and in HKD to holders of H Shares before Monday, 17 August 2026. 
Dividend payable in HKD will be converted from RMB based on the average median price of the 
exchange rate for HKD to RMB as quoted by the People’s Bank of China for the five working 
days immediately preceding the date of the AGM (inclusive) (being HKD1.00 to RMB0.869296), 
amounting to a cash dividend of HKD0.2604 per Share (inclusive of tax).
