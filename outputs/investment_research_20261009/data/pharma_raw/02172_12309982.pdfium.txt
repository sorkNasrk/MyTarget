INSIDE INFORMATION

This announcement is made by MicroPort NeuroScientific Corporation (the “Company”, 
together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) pursuant to Rules 13.09, 13.48(1) and 13.49(6) 
of the Rules Governing the Listing of Securities (the “Listing Rules”) on The Stock 
Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the “Stock Exchange”) and the Inside Information 
Provisions under Part XIVA of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Chapter 571 of the 
Laws of Hong Kong).
DELAY IN PUBLICATION OF 2026 INTERIM RESULTS AND POSSIBLE 
DELAY IN PUBLICATION OF 2026 INTERIM REPORT
Pursuant to Rules 13.49(6) and 13.48(1) of the Listing Rules, the Company is required to 
publish the interim results of the Group for the six months ended 30 June 2026 (the “2026 
Interim Results”) not later than two months after the end of the relevant period, i.e. on or 
before 31 August 2026, and to send its interim report for the six months ended 30 June 2026 
(the “2026 Interim Report”) to its shareholders (the “Shareholders”) not later than three 
months after the end of the relevant period, i.e. on or before 30 September 2026.
Reference is made to the announcement of the Company dated 26 August 2026 (the 
“Announcement”). The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company 
wishes to further announce that the publication of the 2026 Interim Results and the 2026 
Interim Report are expected to be delayed, as the Board considers that the Company 
requires additional time to (a) ascertain the background of the lessor and its associates and 
its relationship with the Company’s shareholder; (b) assess the reasonableness of the terms

of lease agreement, including but not limited to the rent and security deposit provisions; and 
(c) assess the reasonableness of the additional renovation costs incurred in connection with 
certain leased areas under the lease agreement and the accounting treatment thereof.
The Company will make further announcement(s) to provide updates on the above matters 
as soon as possible and when appropriate in accordance with the Listing Rules.
POSTPONEMENT OF BOARD MEETING
Reference is made to the Announcement in relation to the postponement of the Board 
meeting (the “Board Meeting”) for the purpose of, among other things, considering and 
approving the 2026 Interim Results and the publication thereof, considering the payment 
of an interim dividend (if any), and transacting any other business. In view of the aforesaid 
delay in the publication of the 2026 Interim Results, the Board Meeting will be further 
postponed. The Company will publish further announcement(s) to inform the Shareholders 
of the date of the Board Meeting as and when appropriate.
SUSPENSION OF TRADING
At the request of the Company, trading in the shares of the Company on the Stock Exchange 
will be suspended with effect from 9:00 a.m. on Tuesday, 1 September 2026, pending the 
release of the 2026 Interim Results, which constitutes inside information.
The Company will publish further announcement(s) to keep the Shareholders and potential 
investors informed of any material developments regarding the publication of the 2026 
Interim Results and the resumption of trading in the shares of the Company as and when 
appropriate. Shareholders and potential investors of the Company are advised to exercise 
caution when dealing in the securities of the Company.
