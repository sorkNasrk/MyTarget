VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT –
JS213 RECEIVED THE NOTICE OF APPROVAL FOR CLINICAL TRIAL
This announcement is made by Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海君實生物醫藥科技
股份有限公司) (the “Company”) on a voluntary basis. Reference is also made to the overseas 
regulatory announcement of the Company dated 5 February 2025.
The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company is pleased to announce 
that the Company has received the Notice of Approval for Clinical Trial* (《藥物臨床試驗批准
通知書》) issued by the National Medical Products Administration. The investigational new drug 
application for AWT020 sterile powder for injection (project code: “JS213”) has been approved. 
Relevant information is as follows:
ABOUT JS213
Drug name: AWT020 sterile powder for injection
Application matter: Clinical Trial of Domestic Production Registration of Pharmaceutical Product
Acceptance No.: CXSL2400793
Applicant: Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.*
Review conclusion: According to the Drug Administration Law of the People’s Republic of 
China* (《中華人民共和國藥品管理法》) and relevant regulations, upon review, the application 
for clinical trial of AWT020 sterile powder for injection accepted on 22 November 2024 meets 
the relevant requirements for drug registration, and it has been approved to conduct clinical trials 
as a monotherapy and in combination with other anti-tumor therapies in patients with advanced 
malignant tumors.

JS213 is a PD-1 and interleukin-2 (“IL-2”) bifunctional antibody fusion protein, which is mainly 
used for the treatment of advanced malignant tumors. In view of the co-expression of PD-1 and 
IL-2 in the tumor microenvironment, the fusion protein can selectively activate the IL-2 signaling 
pathway by binding to the IL-2 receptor while blocking the PD-1 pathway, thereby strengthening 
the anti-tumor immune responses. The combination therapy with PD-1 and IL-2 has shown 
potential efficacy in a variety of tumor types. Compared with combination therapy, JS213 as 
a single agent targeting both PD-1 and IL-2 pathways, may be more effective in coordinating 
the activation and suppression of the immune system and thus enhancing anti-tumor activity. 
Preclinical studies have demonstrated that JS213 has a significant anti-tumor effect in multiple 
animal models. In addition, JS213 has a favorable, acceptable safety and tolerability profile. As of 
the date of this announcement, there is no bifunctional antibody fusion protein product with similar 
targets approved for marketing domestically and overseas. JS213 has entered into phase I clinical 
trial stage overseas.
