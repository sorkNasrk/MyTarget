VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
CNGT Lu-177 DMF SUCCESSFULLY OBTAINED U.S. FDA 
FILING CLEARANCE
This announcement is made by China Isotope & Radiation Corporation (“China Isotope & 
Radiation” or the “Company”) on a voluntary basis to keep the shareholders and potential 
investors informed of the latest business development of the Company.
The board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) is pleased to announce that, recently, 
Chengdu Gaotong Isotope Co., Ltd. (CNNC) (“CNGT”), a subsidiary of China Isotope & 
Radiation, received a confirmation letter from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”). 
The Lu-177 APIs DMF previously submitted by the Company successfully passed the review. This 
milestone signifies that the Lu-177 APIs DMF of CNGT has been recognised by the FDA with 
its DMF number activated, qualifying it for citation by downstream pharmaceutical formulation 
manufacturers. This achievement marks a crucial step in CNGT’s internationalisation strategy in 
core raw material sector of nuclear medicines.
The approval and activation of the Lu-177 DMF means that downstream formulation 
manufacturers may directly cite the relevant DMF number when submitting applications to the 
FDA for Lu-177-based radiopharmaceuticals, without the need to separately provide supporting 
documentation relating to APIs. This effectively streamlines the drug registration process, shortens 
regulatory review timelines and reduces application costs. For downstream partners, this establishes 
a more convenient and efficient compliant source of Lu-177 APIs, facilitating the acceleration of 
clinical research and commercialisation progress of Lu-177-based radiopharmaceutical products.
