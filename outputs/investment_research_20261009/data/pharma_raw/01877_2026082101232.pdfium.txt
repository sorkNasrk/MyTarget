COMPLETION OF ISSUANCE OF THE 2026 THIRD 
TRANCHE OF TECHNOLOGY INNOVATION BONDS
This announcement is made by Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海君實生物醫藥科技
股份有限公司) (the “Company”) on a voluntary basis. Reference is also made to the overseas 
regulatory announcement of the Company dated 21 August 2026.
Reference is made to the announcement dated 28 December 2025 of the Company in relation to 
the acceptance of registration of the medium term notes by the National Association of Financial 
Market Institutional Investors (the “Announcement”). Unless otherwise defined, capitalized terms 
used herein shall have the same meanings as those defined in the Announcement.
The board of directors of the Company is pleased to announce that the Company has recently 
completed the issuance of the 2026 third tranche of technology innovation bonds, and received the 
funds on 21 August 2026. The 2026 third tranche of technology innovation bonds is issued with 
a total amount of RMB0.7 billion and a term of 3 years, and shall mature on 21 August 2029 and 
have an issuance interest rate of 2.55%.
The relevant documents in respect of the issuance of the 2026 third tranche of technology innovation 
bonds have been published on websites recognised by the National Association of Financial Market 
Institutional Investors, including the websites of ChinaMoney at https://www.chinamoney.com.cn, the 
Shanghai Clearing House at https://www.shclearing.com.cn and the Beijing Financial Assets Exchange 
at https://www.cfae.cn.
Shareholders of the Company and potential investors are advised to exercise cautiously when 
dealing in the securities of the Company.
