COMPLETION OF THE ISSUE OF THE BONDS
The Board is pleased to announce that all of the conditions precedent to the issue of the 
Bonds under the Subscription Agreement have been satisfied and the issue of the Bonds in an 
aggregate principal amount of HK$2,866,000,000 was completed on 28 January 2026.
The listing of and permission to deal in the Bonds on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange is 
expected to become effective on 29 January 2026. The Company has obtained the approval 
for the listing of, and permission to deal in, the Conversion Shares on the Hong Kong Stock 
Exchange.
