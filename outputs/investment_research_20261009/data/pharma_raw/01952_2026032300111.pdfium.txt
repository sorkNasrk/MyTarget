DISCLOSEABLE TRANSACTION IN RELATION TO
THE ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT
The Board is pleased to announce that on 23 March 2026 (before trading hours), the Buyer,
a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, and the Seller entered into the Asset Purchase
Agreement, pursuant to which, (i) the Buyer agreed to acquire, and the Seller agreed to sell,
the Acquired Assets at a consideration of up to US$50 million (equivalent to approximately
RMB344,895,000); and (ii) the Seller agreed assign and transfer to the Buyer, and the Buyer
agreed to accept the assignment of, the Seller’s rights, interests, claims, duties, obligations
and liabilities (other than certain excluded liabilities) under the License Agreement and
certain ancillary agreements related to such assignment (the ‘‘Assigned Contracts’’).
Principal terms of the Asset Purchase Agreement are set out below.

Acquired Assets
Pursuant to the Asset Purchase Agreement, the Seller shall (or shall cause its applicable
affiliates to) sell, transfer, convey, assign and deliver irrevocably to the Buyer, and the
Buyer shall purchase and accept from Seller (or such affiliates), the following Acquired
Assets, free and clear of any encumbrances (other than certain permitted encumbrances):
(a) certain intellectual property (i) owned by the Seller; and (ii) exclusively related to the
Product in the Field in the Territory; and
(b) certain inventory of raw materials and work in progress and finished inventory of the
Products, and any and all rights of Seller to use such inventory as of the Closing Date.
Consideration
The consideration for the transfer of the Acquired Assets and the assignment of the
Assigned Contracts is up to US$50 million (equivalent to approximately RMB344,895,000),
consisting of (i) an upfront payment of US$30 million (equivalent to approximately
RMB206,937,000); and (ii) development milestone payments of up to US$20 million
(equivalent to approximately RMB137,958,000) in aggregate. The Company currently
intends to settle the aforementioned payments in cash through internal resources.
Upfront Payment
The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an upfront payment of US$30 million (equivalent to
approximately RMB206,937,000) on the Closing Date, provided that the Seller has delivered
to the Buyer a pro-forma invoice indicating the amount of the Upfront Payment at least 10
business days prior to the Closing Date.
Milestone Payments
The Buyer shall pay to the Seller development milestone payments upon the first
achievement of any of the development milestones set out below: (i) US$15 million
(equivalent to approximately RMB103,468,500) relates to the NDA approval of the Product
by the NMPA for the treatment of paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia (‘‘PSVT’’); (ii)
US$2 million (equivalent to approximately RMB13,795,800) upon the inclusion of the
Product in the National Reimbursement Drug List for the first time at a specific price per
unit; and (iii) US$3 million (equivalent to approximately RMB20,693,700) relates to the
acceptance by the NMPA of a marketing authorization application for a version of the
localized Product manufactured in the Chinese Mainland.
Each milestone payment is payable by the Seller to the Buyer within 10 business days after
the Buyer’s receipt of a duly issued pro-forma invoice from Seller and the achievement of
the applicable development milestone event.

Basis of the consideration under the Asset Purchase Agreement
The consideration and payment terms were determined and arrived at after arm’s length
negotiations between the parties, taking into account (i) the status of the development of the
Product; and (ii) the future prospects of the development and commercialization of the
Product in the Territory, based on addressable patient population, unmet medical needs as
well as discount taking into account the probability of success.
Assignment
Pursuant to the Asset Purchase Agreement, the Seller also assign and transfer to the Buyer,
and the Buyer shall accept the assignment of, the Seller’s rights, interests, claims, duties,
obligations and liabilities (other than certain excluded liabilities) under the Assigned
Contracts.
Subject to consents on the assignment of the Assigned Contracts, the Buyer shall assume
and perform all the payment obligations under each of the Assigned Contracts which have
incurred, arisen, became due or payable on and after the Closing Date, including:
(i) development milestone payments of up to US$8 million (equivalent to approximately
RMB55,183,200) in aggregate upon the first achievement of any of the following
development milestones, including the NDA approval by NMPA in the Chinese
Mainland for the Product for the treatment of PSVT, the NDA approval by the U.S.
Food and Drug Administration for the Product in any indication other than PSVT and
the NDA approval by NMPA in the Chinese Mainland for the Product in any indication
other than PSVT;
(ii) sales milestone payments of up to US$92 million (equivalent to approximately
RMB634,606,800) in aggregate, based on the achievement of different sales milestone
figures for the annual net sales of the Product in the Territory, from first exceeding
US$25 million to first exceeding US$400 million; and
(iii) royalties calculated based on the applicable tiered royalty rate for each specified portion
of annual net sales of the Product in the Territory which ranges between low-to-high
teens percentage.

Closing
Pursuant to the terms and subject to the satisfaction of conditions to the Closing, the Closing
shall take place at the Seller’s office no later than the earlier date of (i) 31 March 2026, or
(ii) 30 days following the execution date of the Asset Purchase Agreement. The Closing

Termination
At any time prior to the Closing, the Asset Purchase Agreement may be terminated by and
upon delivery of a 30-day prior written notice explaining the reason for such termination at
the following circumstances:
(i) by mutual written consent of both the Seller and the Buyer;
(ii) by the Buyer, if it is not in material breach of any provision of the Asset Purchase
Agreement, and if the Seller shall have breached in any material respect any provision
of the Asset Purchase Agreement, which breach would render unsatisfied any condition
and such breach is incapable of being cured;
(iii) by the Seller, if it is not in material breach of any provision of the Asset Purchase
Agreement, and if Buyer shall have breached in any material respect any provision of
the Asset Purchase Agreement, which breach would render unsatisfied any condition
and such breach is incapable of being cured;
(iv) by the Buyer, if the Seller fails to fulfill any of its obligations for the Closing on or
before the earlier of (i) 31 March 2026, or (ii) 30 days following the execution date of
the Asset Purchase Agreement; or
(v) by the Seller, if the Buyer fails to fulfill any of its obligations for the Closing on or
before the earlier of (i) 31 March 2026, or (ii) 30 days following the execution date of
the Asset Purchase Agreement.
For clarity, neither party has the right to terminate the Asset Purchase Agreement for
convenience prior to the Closing.
After the Closing, the Asset Purchase Agreement may be terminated by and upon delivery of
a 30-day prior written notice by the Seller, if the Buyer fails to make the full payment of
any one of development milestone payments within 30 days following the achievement of
such development milestone event and receipt of pro-forma invoice from the Seller, and
such failure for the payment is not cured by the Buyer with ten business days following the
Buyer’s receipt of the Seller’s written notice of a payment reminder.
The Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors) consider that the terms of
the Asset Purchase Agreement are on normal commercial terms, fair and reasonable, and in

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT
The Directors are of the view that the transaction arranged under the Asset Purchase
Agreement represents the best opportunity to optimize value for Shareholders and patients
worldwide. The transaction is expected to allow the Group to focus on growing other
pipeline assets and to pursue additional opportunities to further expand its existing pipeline
of drug products. In addition, the transaction contemplated under the Asset Purchase
Agreement is aligned with the overall and long-term goals of the Group that will best serve
the interests of the Company and its shareholders in the long run.
INFORMATION ABOUT THE PARTIES
The Buyer and the Company
The Buyer is a company incorporated in Singapore principally engaged in international
activities and a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. The Company is a
biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing transformative
pharmaceutical products that address critical unmet medical needs for patients in Asian
markets. The management team of the Company has deep expertise and an extensive track
record of high-quality clinical development, regulatory affairs, CMC, business development
and operations both in China and with leading global pharmaceutical companies. The
Company has built a portfolio of ten potentially global first-in-class or best-in-class
molecules, many of which are in late stage clinical development. The Company’s therapeutic
areas of interest include oncology, autoimmune disorders, cardio-renal diseases and
infectious diseases.
The Seller
The Seller is a company incorporated in Hong Kong with limited liability and is principally
engaged in research and development of innovation therapies and a wholly-owned subsidiary
of Corxel Pharmaceuticals Limited. Corxel Pharmaceuticals Limited is a clinical-stage
biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing innovative therapies for patients with
cardiometabolic conditions around the world. Corxel Pharmaceuticals Limited is led by an
experienced management team that has a strong track record of identifying, in-licensing and
developing attractive clinical product candidates directed at validated targets with proven
mechanisms of action (MoAs). Corxel Pharmaceuticals Limited’s diverse portfolio of
clinical-stage product candidates has the potential to redefine treatment standards and
address key limitations of current therapies for multiple cardiometabolic indications. Corxel
Pharmaceuticals Limited is developing selective small molecule compounds across the
cardiometabolic spectrum with the lead product candidate CX11, an oral GLP-1 RA for
obesity and overweight conditions and T2DM, JX10, a thrombolytic and anti-inflammatory
agent for acute ischemic stroke and CX12 (amylin) for obesity treatment. Corxel
Pharmaceuticals Limited also has additional small molecule programs in development
targeting validated obesity targets.

INFORMATION OF THE PRODUCT
The Product refers to any pharmaceutical product that uses a device to deliver the etripamil
(as the sole active ingredient) by nasal spray. Etripamil is a fast-acting non-dihydropyridine
L-type calcium channel blocker (CCB) developed by Milestone Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ:
MIST) for the treatment of PSVT and other cardiac arrhythmias. In an unsupervised medical
situation (e.g., at home), patients can self-administer etripamil through nasal spray to rapidly
terminate an acute PSVT episode. PSVT is a condition characterized by abnormalities in the
heart’s electrical system that can lead to unexpected, symptomatic tachycardia. One unmet
medical need is the early cessation of PSVT. In China, there are currently no self￾administered, fast-acting, non-injectable medical treatments approved for acute PSVT.
