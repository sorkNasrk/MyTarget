This announcement is made by Shanghai MicroPort MedBot (Group) Co., Ltd. (the 
“Company”, together with its subsidiaries, the “Group”) pursuant to Rules 13.09(2)(a) and 
13.49(6) of the Rules Governing the Listing of Securities on The Stock Exchange of Hong 
Kong Limited (the “Listing Rules”) and the Inside Information Provisions (as defined in the 
Listing Rules) of Part XIVA of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571 of the Laws 
of Hong Kong).
DELAY IN PUBLICATION OF 2026 INTERIM RESULTS
Pursuant to Rule 13.49(6) of the Listing Rules, the Company is required to publish the 
interim results of the Group for the six months ended 30 June 2026 (the “2026 Interim 
Results”) on or before 31 August 2026.
The Board would like to inform the shareholders and potential investors of the Company 
that, with respect to a lease arrangement of the Group during the reporting period, the 
Company needs more time to further confirm the relationship between the counterparty 
and the Group. As of the date of this announcement, the Company is unable to publish a 
preliminary announcement of 2026 interim results pursuant to Rule 13.49(6) of the Listing 
Rules as the above matters are still to be clarified. The expected date of publication of the 
2026 interim results will depend on the progress and outcome of the confirmation of the 
above matters. The Company will publish separate announcements in due course.

POSTPONEMENT OF BOARD MEETINGS
Reference is made to the announcements of the Company dated 17 August 2026 and 
27 August 2026 in relation to the dates and postponement of the Board meetings of the 
Company to consider and approve, among other things, the 2026 interim results and 
its publication, as well as considering the payment of an interim dividend, if any, and 
transacting other matters. Based on the above reasons, the Board hereby announces that the 
Board meeting will be further postponed until further notice.
SUSPENSION OF TRADING
At the request of the Company, trading in the shares of the Company on the Stock Exchange 
will be suspended from 9:00 a.m. on Tuesday, 1 September 2026 and will be suspended 
until further announcement.
The Company would like to emphasise that the Group’s operations remain stable and have 
not been affected in any way.
Shareholders and potential investors of the Company are advised to exercise caution 
when dealing in the shares of the Company.
