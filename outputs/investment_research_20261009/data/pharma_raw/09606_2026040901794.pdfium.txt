INSIDE INFORMATION
BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION FOR DB-1303/BNT323 FOR 
UNRESECTABLE OR METASTATIC HER2-POSITIVE BREAST CANCER 
IN ADULTS ACCEPTED BY THE NATIONAL MEDICAL PRODUCTS 
ADMINISTRATION
This announcement is made by Duality Biotherapeutics, Inc. (the “Company”) pursuant to Rule 
13.09 of the Rules Governing the Listing of Securities on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong 
Limited (the “Listing Rules”) and the Inside Information Provisions under Part XIVA of the 
Securities and Futures Ordinance (Chapter 571 of the Laws of Hong Kong).
Reference is made to the announcement of the Company dated September 5, 2025, regarding the 
latest update on the development progress of the Company’s Core Product DB-1303/BNT323. The 
board of directors (the “Board”) is pleased to announce that, based on the positive results obtained 
from the Phase III pivotal clinical trial (study code: DB-1303-O-3001) and the feedback from the 
pre-submission communication with the Center for Drug Evaluation (CDE) of the National Medical 
Products Administration (the “NMPA”) regarding the Biologics License Application, the submitted 
Biologics License Application (the “BLA”) has been accepted by the NMPA.
The DB-1303-O-3001 trial is a randomized, controlled, open-label, multi-center Phase III clinical 
trial designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of DB-1303 compared with T-DM1 in patients 
with HER2-positive unresectable or metastatic breast cancer who have previously received 
trastuzumab and a taxane. As per the review by the Independent Data Monitoring Committee 
(IDMC), the Phase III clinical trial achieved the primary endpoint progression-free survival (PFS). 
In the pre-specified interim analysis, DB-1303 demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically 
meaningful improvement in the primary endpoint compared with the control arm, with a favorable 
safety and tolerability profile.

ABOUT DB-1303/BNT323
DB-1303 (partner BioNTech SE development code: BNT323; International Nonproprietary 
Name (INN)/United States Adopted Name (USAN): Trastuzumab pamirtecan; proposed Chinese 
generic name: 帕康曲妥珠單抗) is a clinical-stage HER2 ADC candidate built on the Company’s 
proprietary DITAC (Duality Immune Toxin Antibody Conjugates) technology platform.
Trastuzumab pamirtecan is currently being studied across multiple solid tumors and exploring 
innovative combination therapy regimens, including two global pivotal clinical trials: the 
DYNASTY-Breast02 study (NCT06018337) for first-line treatment of HER2-low, hormone 
receptor-positive (HR+) metastatic breast cancer, and the BNT323-01 study (NCT06340568) for 
second-line treatment of endometrial cancer; as well as a Phase I/II novel combination clinical trial 
BNT323-03 (NCT06827236), designed to evaluate trastuzumab pamirtecan in combination with 
pumitamab for the treatment of patients with hormone receptor-positive or -negative, HER2-low, 
ultralow or negative advanced/metastatic breast cancer.
DB-1303/BNT323 has obtained Fast Track and Breakthrough Therapy Designations from the FDA 
and Breakthrough Therapy Designation from the NMPA for the treatment of HER2-expressing 
advanced endometrial cancer in patients who progressed on or after treatment with immune 
checkpoint inhibitors. Moreover, DB-1303/BNT323’s treatment responses have been observed in a 
range of tumors, including breast cancer, endometrial cancer, ovarian cancer, colorectal cancer and 
esophageal cancer, and are supported by global clinical data from patients across the U.S., China, 
Europe, Australia and other countries.
