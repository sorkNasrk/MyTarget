NET PROCEEDS FROM THE PLACING
The Company intends to use the net proceeds from the Placing primarily for the purposes 
set forth below:
(i) for continuous product iterations and upgrades to enhance its research and development 
technological capabilities and solution capabilities, such as:
(a) empowering new materials R&D with artificial intelligence (AI) and automation. 
For example, in August 2024, the Group entered into a strategic R&D 
collaboration agreement with GCL Group Co., Ltd. The cooperation includes the 
joint development of various AI, quantum physics and robotic automation system 
in the field of new energy materials, and joint R&D to update new energy and 
new materials and to improve industrial upgrade;
(b) computational advancements which will enable earlier and more widespread 
use of antibody-antigen structural insights across drug discovery process and 
which have the potential to transform antibody discovery methods, broadening 
therapeutic applications and accelerating the development of innovative 
molecules. For example, in October 2024 and January 2025, respectively, the 
Group entered into a license agreement on XtalFold™, a biologics AI platform 
developed by Ailux Biologics, a division of the Group, with Janssen Biotech, a 
Johnson & Johnson company, and UCB, a global biopharmaceutical company; 
and
(c) design and development of petroleum catalysts and improvement of the yield and 
quality of petroleum products through catalysts which can reduce the emission of 
environmental pollutants. For example, in October 2024, the Group successfully 
delivered a catalyst synthesis workstation to Sinopec Shanghai Research Institute 
of Petrochemical Technology for the high-throughput synthesis of a catalyst. The 
equipment is currently deployed at the customer’s site and put into use;
(ii) for facilitating the commercialization of the Company, strengthening external 
collaborations, and expanding the scale and market share of the Company, such as:
(a) external collaborations in the use of cutting-edge technologies such as AI, 
quantum physics and robotic laboratories, and active exploration of innovative 
applications of these technologies in the fields of biomedicine and materials 
science, for example, in December 2024, the Group entered into a strategic 
cooperation memorandum with Microsoft China, to use AI and robotics to drive 
change in life sciences, new materials, education and other fields;

(b) development of platforms, powered by AI, automation and engineering, which 
will provide an intelligent and automated system for the rapid construction of 
Traditional Chinese Medicnie (TCM) chemical and active ingredient screening 
libraries, enabling fast and efficient analysis of data results, and real-time 
monitoring, tracking and tracing, fault alerts, and diagnostic analysis of system 
operations. For example, recently, the Group is in the final stage of delivery of 
TCM automation systems and total solutions to Chinese Medicine Guangdong 
Laboratory. Such automation system was visited by top government leaders; and
(c) collaboration with medical and/or academic institutions to develop AI-based 
new drug discovery. For example, in December 2024, the Group entered into a 
collaboration with National Cancer Centre Singapore (NCCS) and Duke-NUS 
Medical School (Duke-NUS) where the Group will use a peptide research and 
design platform driven by AI and automated experiment to discover and design 
drugs for new targets of clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC) selected by 
NCCS, in order to provide better treatment options for this common type of renal 
cancer, and share subsequent commercialization results with partners;
(iii) for investing in potential opportunities, talent attraction and introduction, working 
capital replenishment and general corporate purposes, with details as follows:
(a) in terms of investing in potential opportunities, since completion of the Global 
Offering, due to its increased profile as a listed issuer, the Group has been 
expanding its global footprint at a pace faster than expected, primarily in the 
United States, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia, Europe and the Middle East. The 
Company has been approached by various potential business partners and 
collaborators in the exploration of potential business collaboration and investment 
opportunities, some of which had not been anticipated at the time of the Global 
Offering. For example, as announced by the Company, on October 30, 2024, the 
Company entered into a non-legally binding memorandum of understanding with 
PT Sinar Mas Multiartha, Tbk in relation to the proposed formation of a strategic 
partnership to collaborate in the fields of artificial intelligence and automation 
in Southeast Asia. As at the date of this announcement, discussions on various 
investment and partnership opportunities are ongoing between the Group and the 
relevant counterparties. The Group is currently in the process of evaluating the 
opportunities. With the necessary funds and preparation in place, the Group will 
be well-positioned to grasp the right opportunities as and when they arise, which 
would be in the best interest of the Company and its shareholders;
(b) in terms of talent attraction and introduction, to maintain the Company’s position 
and competitiveness as a leading AI drug discovery company, it is critical for the 
Group to have sufficient cash resources from time to time for talent attraction 
so as to strengthen the R&D team of the Group and attract the best talents and 
experts in the field; and

While the Company currently intends to continue to utilize the IPO Proceeds in accordance 
with the purposes and timeline as disclosed by the Company in the Prospectus, the Company 
believes that the additional funding raised from the Placing will benefit the Group’s 
development as a whole and could serve to supplement the intended uses allocated for the 
IPO Proceeds. The Group has continued to rapidly develop its business operations at a 
pace faster than expected, and has achieved significant milestones in various aspects of its 
business operations. For example, with its solid accumulation in the fields of AI, quantum 
physics and robotics technology, the Group stood out in the competitive bidding and was 
qualified to participate in the construction of the “Four-in-One Platform” for the creation of 
new Chinese medicines in Chinese Medicine Guangdong Laboratory.
In addition, as alluded to in the section headed “NET PROCEEDS FROM THE 
PLACING” above, the Group has come across multiple new business opportunities since 
completion of the Global Offering in June last year. These include (i) antibody discovery, 
(ii) automation total solution, (iii) automated chemical synthesis, (iv) material science, and 
(v) collaboration with technology giants, among others. Such opportunities had not been 
anticipated as at the time of the Global Offering. With such new business opportunities, 
the Company considers that the proceeds of the Placing and the Global Offering will 
be complementary to each other and the additional funding raised from the Placing will 
facilitate the Group’s achievement of commercialization and reinforcement of its position as 
a leader in drug discovery solutions and intelligent automation solutions.
Since the completion of the Global Offering, global market conditions and geopolitical 
environment have changed dramatically, and the global macro and business environment 
for the Group will continue to be volatile and unpredictable. Recently, various monetary 
and/or economic policies have been announced resulting in global fluctuations in interest 
rate adjustment expectation, which in turn affect the valuation of companies in particular 
emerging technology and biotechnology companies. The Company has noted the recent 
surge in fundraising activities taken by its peers. As a technology-driven company in a 
highly competitive global business environment which is highly sensitive to policy changes, 
it is critical for the Group to stay ahead of market changes, be prepared for opportunities as 
and when they arise any time and keep up with actions taken by peers in the same industry. 
While the global economy continues to face challenges and risks, the Group believes in 
the coexistence of challenges and opportunities. In view of the recent developments, the 
prevailing market conditions and the macro-environment, and having considered the needs 
of the Company and the business outlook, the Board considers that it would be appropriate
