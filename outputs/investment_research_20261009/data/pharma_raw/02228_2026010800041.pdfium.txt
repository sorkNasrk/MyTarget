Adjustment upon
Change of Control:
If a Change of Control shall have occurred, the Company shall 
give notice of that fact to the Bondholders (“Change of Control 
Notice”) in accordance with the Terms and Conditions within 14 
days after it becomes aware of such Change of Control. Following 
the giving of a Change of Control Notice, upon any exercise of 
Conversion Rights such that the relevant Conversion Date falls 
within the period of 30 days following the later of (i) the relevant 
Change of Control and (ii) the date on which the Change of 
Control Notice is given to Bondholders (such period, the “Change 
of Control Conversion Period”), the Conversion Price shall be 
adjusted in accordance with the following formula:
NCP = OCP/(1 + (CP x c/t))
Where:
“NCP” means the Conversion Price after such adjustment;
“OCP” means the Conversion Price before such adjustment. For 
the avoidance of doubt, OCP for the purposes of this paragraph 
shall be the Conversion Price applicable on the relevant 
Conversion Date in respect of any conversion to which this 
paragraph is applicable;
“CP”, or conversion premium, means 25 per cent. expressed as a 
fraction;
“c” means the number of days from and including the first day 
of the Change of Control Conversion Period to but excluding the 
Maturity Date; and
“t” means the number of days from and including the Issue Date 
to but excluding the Maturity Date,
provided that the Conversion Price shall not be reduced below 
the level permitted by the Listing Rules or applicable laws and 
regulations from time to time (if any).

REASONS FOR THE ISSUE OF THE BONDS AND USE OF PROCEEDS
The Company is an investment holding company. The Group is principally engaged in the 
provision of intelligent robotics solutions and drug discovery solutions.
The Company has been actively pursuing its vision to become the global leader in quantum 
physics-based, AI-powered drug and material science research and development.
Several recent business development highlights as announced by the Company in the 
past few months include: (1) definitive agreement with DoveTree Medicines LLC and its 
affiliates with potential regulatory and commercial milestone payments of up to US$5.99 
billion to the Group, where the Group will use its end-to-end AI drug discovery platform 
through integrated “AI + robotics” technology to discover and develop small molecule and 
antibody drug candidates for multiple targets selected by DoveTree primarily in the fields 
of oncology, immunologic and inflammatory diseases, neurological disorders and metabolic 
dysregulation; (2) strategic platform-based collaboration with Eli Lilly and Company with 
development, regulatory and commercial milestone payments of up to US$345 million, to 
accelerate the discovery and development of bispecific antibodies for the treatment of various 
diseases; and (3) formally entering the consumer healthcare sector with enormous potential, 
via the successful development and validation of two innovative topical efficacy ingredients 
for hair loss prevention and hair growth, the small-molecule Remeanagen™ (XTP-118) and 
the peptide AquaKine™ (XTP-016), leveraging the Group’s industry leading AI molecular 
discovery platform. The combined formula of such “Gemini” molecules has been proven in 
rigorous human trials to significantly outperform existing similar products in terms of hair 
growth and hair loss prevention, safety, and speed of onset of action. Both molecules have 
successfully passed the INCI (International Nomenclature Cosmetic Ingredient) registration 
for new cosmetic ingredients in the U.S., and their combined formulated product, Groland, 
has also been approved for cosmetic product filing by the U.S. FDA (Food and Drug 
Administration).
The Group’s continual efforts have been reflected in its financial results, with revenue for the 
six months ended 30 June 2025 of RMB517.1 million representing a year-on-year growth of 
403.8%, and having achieved profitability for the first time on a half-year basis with adjusted 
net profit of RMB141.6 million for the six months ended 30 June 2025. This milestone is 
a testament to the Group’s collective effort and innovation, signalling its entry into a new 
phase of large-scale growth and strengthening the foundation for its long-term strategy, and 
ultimately, the right direction.
