ENTERING INTO THE SHAREHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT
On 28 July 2026, the Company entered into the Shareholders’ Agreement with CNNC Zhejiang 
Science and Technology Innovation, in respect of the establishment of the joint venture company, 
CNNC Fuzhi. Pursuant to the Shareholders’ Agreement, the registered capital of CNNC Fuzhi 
is RMB50 million, among which, RMB45 million will be contributed by the Company by way 
of a combination of non-monetary assets and cash, comprising RMB26.60 million in cash and 
RMB18.40 million in the form of the net assets of the Nuclear Medicine Development Center; 
and RMB5 million will be contributed by CNNC Zhejiang Science and Technology Innovation 
in cash. Upon the establishment of CNNC Fuzhi, the Company and CNNC Zhejiang Science and 
Technology Innovation will hold 90% and 10% equity interests in CNNC Fuzhi, respectively, 
and CNNC Fuzhi will become a non-wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company.
IMPLICATIONS OF THE LISTING RULES
As at the date of this announcement, CNNC directly and indirectly holds approximately 73.83% 
of the Company’s total issued share capital through CIAE, NPIC, CNNC Fund and 404 Company, 
and is the controlling Shareholder and connected person of the Company. CNNC Capital is a 
wholly-owned subsidiary of CNNC, CNNC Science and Technology Innovation is a wholly￾owned subsidiary of CNNC Capital, and CNNC Zhejiang Science and Technology Innovation is 
a non-wholly-owned subsidiary of CNNC Science and Technology Innovation. Therefore, CNNC 
Zhejiang Science and Technology Innovation is an associate of CNNC and a connected person 
of the Company under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules. The entering into the Shareholders’
Agreement and the transaction contemplated thereunder thus constitute a connected transaction 
of the Company.
As the highest applicable percentage ratio calculated under the Listing Rules in respect of 
the transaction under the Shareholders’ Agreement exceeds 0.1% but is less than 5%, such 
transaction is only subject to the reporting and announcement requirements but exempt from 
independent Shareholders’ approval requirement under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules.

Valuation of the Net Assets of the Nuclear Medicine Development Center
The summary of the Asset Valuation Report issued by the Independent Valuer is set out below:
Valuation Purpose
China Isotope & Radiation Corporation plans to make an outbound capital contribution, for which 
the market value of the net assets of the China Isotope & Radiation Corporation Nuclear Medicine 
Development Center as at the Benchmark Date needs to be assessed, so as to provide a reference 
basis for the aforesaid economic activity.
Valuation Object and Scope
The valuation object is the net asset value of the China Isotope & Radiation Corporation Nuclear 
Medicine Development Center; the valuation scope covers all assets and related liabilities reported 
as of 30 September 2025, specifically including: current assets of RMB83,500, non-current assets 
of RMB5,727,400, and current liabilities of RMB1,541,200.
Valuation Benchmark Date
The Benchmark Date for asset appraisal is 30 September 2025.
Valuation Methods
Selection of the Income Approach Result
The basic methods of enterprise value valuation mainly include the income approach, market 
approach and asset-based approach.
The appraised entity operates on a going concern basis. The selection of economic and technical 
parameters used in the asset-based approach is supported by sufficient data as a foundation and 
basis, and the financial data have been audited. There are no off-book assets that would have a 
material impact on the value of the valuation object and that are difficult to identify and assess; 
therefore, the asset-based approach can be applied.
The income approach evaluates assets from the perspective of expected profitability, which 
can fully reflect the overall value of an enterprise. The appraised entity is an enterprise with a 
certain level of profitability. Considering the relatively stable historical annual performance of 
the appraised entity, its future earnings can be reasonably projected and measured in monetary 
terms, the income period can be reasonably determined, and the risks associated with obtaining the 
expected future earnings can be measured. Therefore, the income approach has been selected for 
this valuation.
A prerequisite for applying the market approach is that comparable reference entities have an 
open market with active trading, as well as the necessary information regarding such transactions 
is accessible. Currently, among domestic listed companies in the same industry as the appraised 
entity, few are comparable in terms of business structure, operating model, enterprise scale, 
asset allocation, and future growth potential. In addition, there have been few comparable equity 
transactions in the property rights trading market involving entities similar to the appraised entity 
recently. Information such as the relevant transaction background and the operating and financial 
data of the transaction cases cannot be obtained through public channels, and thus the basic 
conditions for applying the market approach are not met.

[PDF PAGE 6]
6
The asset-based approach considers the re-acquisition channels of assets and reflects the 
replacement value of an enterprise’s existing assets. The income approach evaluates profitability 
from the perspective of the enterprise’s future earning capacity. The asset-based approach only 
reasonably determines the market value of the assets as at the Benchmark Date from a static 
perspective. In applying the income approach, consideration is given not only to the value of all 
tangible assets, intangible assets, and liabilities already reflected on the enterprise’s balance sheet, 
but also to the enterprise’s capital capacity, the integrated resource capability of all its assets, its 
comprehensive profitability, and its production capacity, which are not shown on the balance sheet 
of the appraised entity.
Based on the purpose of this valuation, in conjunction with the valuation object and the applicable 
type of value, and following comparative analysis, it is considered that the conclusion derived from 
the income approach can more comprehensively and reasonably reflect the net asset value of the 
appraised entity as at the Benchmark Date. In summary, the result of the income approach has been 
selected as the conclusion for this valuation.
Valuation Input Data
Valuation Model and Key Parameters:
The income approach adopted in this valuation is the discounted cash flow method, using 
free cash flow to the firm as the cash flow metric. The net asset value is derived indirectly 
through the valuation of the overall enterprise value.
The calculation model is as follows:
Net asset value = overall enterprise value – interest-bearing debt value
(1) Overall enterprise value
Overall enterprise value refers to the sum of net asset value and the value of interest￾bearing debt. According to the allocation and use of the assets of the appraised entity, 
the overall enterprise value is calculated according to the following formula:
Overall enterprise value = value of operating assets + value of surplus assets + value of 
non-operating assets – value of non-operating liabilities

concern after the Valuation Benchmark Date, and the income period is set as

ii. Value of surplus assets
Surplus assets refer to assets that exceed the needs of the production and operation 
of the enterprise as at the Valuation Benchmark Date and are not covered in the 
forecast of free cash flow to the firm after the Valuation Benchmark Date. The 
surplus assets of the appraised entity have been valued using the cost approach in 
this valuation.
iii. Value of non-operating assets and liabilities
Non-operating assets and liabilities refer to assets and liabilities that are unrelated 
to the operations of the appraised entity and are not covered in the forecast of free 
cash flow to the firm after the Valuation Benchmark Date. The cost approach has 
been adopted in this valuation.
(2) Value of interest-bearing debt
Interest-bearing debt refers to debts of the appraised entity that bear interest as at the 
Valuation Benchmark Date. The appraised entity had no interest-bearing debt as at the 
Valuation Benchmark Date.
Principal Assumptions Underlying the Valuation
Basic Assumptions
1. In the open market assumption, it is assumed that for assets to be traded or intended to be 
traded in the market, the parties to the asset transaction shall have equal status, and also have 
opportunity and time to gain sufficient market information, so as to make rational judgement 
on functions, purpose and trading prices of assets;
2. In the transaction assumption, it is assumed that all assets to be valued are in the process of 
transaction, and the Independent Valuer makes estimation in a simulated market according 
to the transaction conditions of assets to be valued. The transaction assumption is one of the 
most basic prerequisites that asset valuation can be carried out;
3. In the going concern assumption, it is assumed that the appraised entity, based on the existing 
assets and resources, will not stop business for various reasons in the foreseeable future, but 
will continue to operate legally.

1. It is assumed that there are no significant changes in the current relevant laws, regulations,
