Date : April 27, 2026

Maximum Facility 
 Amount

Drawdown Date: : In respect of each drawdown, drawdown date means the date on

Interest Rate : 3.50 per cent. (3.50%) per annum. Interest shall accrue on the

Type of the Loan : Unsecured loan

Early Repayment : The Borrower may, upon giving prior notice, elect to prepay the

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF ENTERING INTO THE LOAN AGREEMENT
The terms of the Loan Agreement, including the maximum facility amount and interest rate, were 
determined after arm’s length negotiations between the Company and the Borrower. In arriving at 
these terms, the parties took into account the prevailing market interest rates, terms and practices. 
The Company intends to fund the Loan using its internal resources.
The Company entered into the Loan Agreement after considering the following factors: (i) the 
Group’s latest financial position and the availability of internal resources; (ii) the Loan will not 
adversely affect the Group’s working capital or its day-to-day operations; and (iii) the interest 
income generated by the Loan is expected to exceed the Group’s funding costs and the interest 
income from time deposits of a comparable term.
Further, the Company (with the coordination of the finance department and human resources 
department) conducted a comprehensive assessment of the Borrower’s repayment capacity, taking 
into account: (i) the Borrower’s current and expected remuneration, alongside the potential 
realizable value of share awards scheduled to vest during the Loan term; (ii) the prevailing market 
value of securities held by the Borrower; (iii) the Borrower’s long-term service with the Group 
as well as her sound integrity record, historical performance and commitment to the Group; and 
(iv) the Loan was intended for legitimate tax compliance purpose. Based on the results of the 
aforementioned assessment, the Board (excluding the Borrower, who has abstained from voting 
on the relevant resolution) is satisfied that the Borrower possesses sufficient repayment capacity 
and a bona fide intention to perform her obligations under the Loan Agreement, particularly given 
that her interests are closely aligned with the long-term success of the Company. Accordingly, the 
Board concluded that the overall risk associated with the Loan is relatively low, and expected the 
Borrower to fulfill the repayment of the principal and all accrued interest in strict accordance with 
the terms of the Loan Agreement.
In view of the above, the Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors) consider 
that the terms of the Loan Agreement are fair and reasonable, and although the transaction 
contemplated under the Loan Agreement is not in the ordinary and usual course of business of 
the Group, it is on normal commercial terms or better and in the interests of the Company and its 
shareholders taken as a whole.
As Dr. LIU Liping is the borrower under the Loan Agreement, she is considered to have a material 
interest in the Loan Agreement and the transaction contemplated thereunder. Accordingly, she has 
abstained from voting on the relevant Board resolution(s) approving the Loan Agreement and the 
transaction contemplated thereunder. Save as disclosed above, none of the Directors has a material 
interest in the Loan Agreement or the transaction contemplated thereunder, and none was required 
to abstain from voting on the relevant Board resolutions.
