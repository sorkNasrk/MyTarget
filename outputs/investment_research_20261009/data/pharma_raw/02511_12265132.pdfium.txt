THE PLACING
On July 31, 2026 (after trading hours), the Company and the Sole Placing Agent entered into the 
Placing Agreement pursuant to which the Company agrees to issue the Placing Shares, and the 
Sole Placing Agent agrees, as agent of the Company, to procure on a best effort basis not less 
than six independent placees to subscribe for up to an aggregate of 99,000,000 Placing Shares at 
the Placing Price of HK$2.28 per Placing Share subject to the terms and conditions set out in the 
Placing Agreement.
Assuming that there is no change in the issued share capital of the Company from the date of 
this announcement up to the date of completion of the Placing, the 99,000,000 Placing Shares 
represent: (i) approximately 17.33% of the existing issued share capital of the Company; and 
(ii) approximately 14.77% of the total issued share capital of the Company as enlarged by the 
allotment and issue of the Placing Shares.
On the assumption that all Placing Shares are issued, the aggregate gross proceeds from the 
Placing are expected to be approximately HK$225.72 million and the aggregate net proceeds 
(after deduction of the commissions and expenses relating to the Placing) from the Placing are 
expected to be approximately HK$223.07 million. For details of the proposed uses of the net 
proceeds from the Placing, see “Reasons for and Benefits of the Placing and Use of Proceeds”
below.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE PLACING AND USE OF PROCEEDS
The Company is a company incorporated in the Cayman Islands with limited liability, the Shares 
of which are listed on the Stock Exchange. The Group is an innovative biopharmaceutical 
company specializing in the research and development of transformative therapeutic solutions for 
cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic diseases.
On the assumption that all Placing Shares are issued, the aggregate gross proceeds from the 
Placing are expected to be approximately HK$225.72 million and the aggregate net proceeds (after 
deduction of the commissions and expenses relating to the Placing) from the Placing are expected 
to be approximately HK$223.07 million.
The Company intends to utilize the net proceeds from the Placing as follows:
(i) approximately 30% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$66.92 million, 
for the commercialization of HTD1801, the Group’s core pipeline product, in its current 
indication of type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM);
(ii) approximately 45% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$100.38 million, 
for the expansion of HTD1801’s development into new indications, including but not limited 
to chronic kidney disease (CKD);
(iii) approximately 15% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$33.46 million, 
for the research and development of the Group’s other drug candidates; and
(iv) approximately 10% of the net proceeds of the Placing, or approximately HK$22.31 million, 
will be used for working capital and other general corporate purposes.
The Company expects the net proceeds from the Placing to be fully utilized by the end of 2028.

HTD1801 is the Company’s lead compound, an in-house developed, first-in-class, gut-liver anti￾inflammatory metabolic modulator. The Directors believe that the Placing will be conducive to 
strengthening the Group’s liquidity and financial position, thereby enabling the Group to continue 
to finance the clinical development of HTD1801 and achieve commercialization.
In view of the above and having considered the recent prevailing market conditions, the prevailing 
market price of the Shares and other relevant factors (including the broadening of Shareholder 
and capital base of the Company), the Board considers that the Placing would be appropriate 
to replenish the Company’s cash resources for the above intended purposes, which would be 
important for the promotion of the Group’s long-term success.
The Directors consider that the Placing Agreement is entered into upon normal commercial terms 
following arm’s length negotiation between the Company and the Sole Placing Agent and the 
terms of the Placing Agreement are fair and reasonable and in the interests of the Company and the 
Shareholders as a whole.
