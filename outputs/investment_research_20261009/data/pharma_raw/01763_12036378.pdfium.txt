VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
SELF-DEVELOPED “RED BLOOD CELL LIFESPAN MEASUREMENT 
BREATH ANALYZER” OBTAINED CLASS II MEDICAL DEVICE 
REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE
This announcement is voluntarily made by China Isotope & Radiation Corporation (“CIRC” or 
the “Company”) to keep its shareholders and potential investors informed of the latest business 
developments of the Company.
The board of directors of the Company is pleased to announce that recently, the “Red Blood Cell 
Lifespan Measurement Breath Analyzer” independently developed by Anhui Young-Hearty Medical 
Appliance & Equipment Co., Ltd. (安徽養和醫療器械設備有限公司), a subsidiary of Zhonghe 
Headway (中核海得威) under CIRC, has obtained the Class II Medical Device Registration 
Certificate, approving the device for market launch. This marks a significant step forward for CIRC 
in enriching its breath diagnostic product pipeline, building on its deep cultivation of Helicobacter 
pylori breath testing technology. It signifies CIRC’s formal entry into the new breath diagnostic 
field of “red blood cell lifespan measurement”, which is expected to promote the application of 
breath testing technology in the early screening and clinical diagnosis of hematological diseases.
Clinically, red blood cell lifespan measurement is primarily applied in the following areas:
1. Diagnosis of anaemia and haemolysis: Excessively rapid destruction of red blood cells may 
lead to haemolytic anaemia, with patients presenting symptoms such as fatigue, pallor and 
jaundice. Measurement of red blood cell lifespan helps identify the underlying causes.
2. Differential diagnosis of jaundice: Jaundice (yellowing of the skin and eyes, etc.) occurring in 
newborns or adults may result from excessive red blood cell destruction or liver dysfunction. 
Red blood cell lifespan testing assists physicians in making quick judgments.
3. Assessment of therapeutic efficacy: For patients diagnosed with haematological disorders, 
periodic monitoring of red blood cell lifespan enables evaluation of treatment effectiveness 
and detection of disease recurrence.

The “Red blood cell Lifespan Measurement Breath Analyzer” is based on the principle of 
endogenous carbon monoxide (CO) metabolism detection. By analyzing the concentration of trace 
carbon monoxide in exhaled breath, the device scientifically calculates the red blood cell lifespan 
and enables non-invasive quantitative assessment. A test report can be generated in approximately 
250 seconds per examination, significantly enhancing testing efficiency and accuracy, making it 
suitable for use in clinical departments such as hematology.
The device achieves integrated integration of testing processes, data management, and equipment 
maintenance, which not only improves operational stability but also makes operation more 
convenient. It effectively meets clinical needs, facilitates ease of use for medical professionals, and 
supports the safeguarding of respiratory health through innovative medical technology.
