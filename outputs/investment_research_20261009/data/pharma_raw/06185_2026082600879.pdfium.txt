VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
UPDATE ON mRNA TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM DEVELOPMENT
This announcement is made by CanSino Biologics Inc. (the “Company”) on a voluntary basis.
The Company has noted recent market developments regarding mRNA technology. mRNA is 
one of the key technology platforms of the Company. The Company is currently engaged in the 
development of mRNA preventive vaccines and therapeutic biologics as well as the development 
of related delivery systems. In respect of therapeutic areas, the Company is advancing its research 
and development of mRNA vaccines for the treatment of indications including glioblastoma, 
rhabdomyosarcoma and cervical cancer together with the development of In Vivo CAR-related 
therapies. All of the foregoing are currently at an early stage.
Recently, CanSino (Shanghai) Biological Research Co., Ltd. (“CanSino Shanghai”), a subsidiary 
of the Company, has entered into a strategic cooperation framework agreement with deepGeneAI 
(Hangzhou) Biotechnology Co., Ltd. (“deepGeneAI”) for the joint development of personalized 
mRNA therapeutic cancer vaccines (the “Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement”). The 
Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement provides a framework for CanSino Shanghai and 
deepGeneAI to explore collaboration by leveraging their respective strengths in mRNA vaccine 
technologies and tumor neoantigen discovery and design.
The board of directors considers that the development of the Company’s mRNA technology 
platform and the entering into of the Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement are in line 
with the Company’s long-term strategy and in the interests of the Company and its shareholders 
as a whole.
