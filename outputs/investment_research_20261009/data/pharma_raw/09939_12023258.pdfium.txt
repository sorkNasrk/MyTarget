SUPPLEMENTAL ANNOUNCEMENT
IN RELATION TO THE VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT
Reference is made to the voluntary announcement (the “Announcement”) dated 21 January 
2026. Unless the context otherwise requires, capitalized terms used herein shall have the 
same meaning as those defined in the Announcement.
The Board would like to provide further information that anti-hair loss foam product (the 
“Product”) is one of the products under the Company’s cosmetic brand KOSHINÉ with 
KX-826 as the main ingredient. The Company wishes to clarify that, whilst the Sales 
Agency Agreement represents a favorable opportunity to further promote the Company’s 
cosmetics commercialization, such cosmetics sales business is currently of a small scale and 
ancillary in nature when compared with the Group’s listing business (namely, development 
and commercialization of the core products KX-826 and GT20029 and other pipeline 
products of the Company) (the “Listing Business”). For the avoidance of doubt, the sales 
target of RMB100 million as disclosed in the Announcement is intended to be a long-term 
objective measured on a cumulative basis, rather than an indication of sales expected to be 
achieved in the near term.
In respect of the human resources, as at the date of this announcement, out of the 
Company’s current 121 employees, 69 employees are designated in drug R&D, drug supply 
and local GMP production and 24 employees are currently dedicated in cosmetics sales, 
which are also designated for the marketing and sales preparation for the drug candidates 
of the Company. In respect of the funding, the R&D expenses are expected to be more than 
50% of total expenses of the Company for the year ended 31 December 2025 based on its 
management account which are primarily attributable to the funding of (i) the Phase II and 
Phase III clinical study of KX-826 on androgenetic alopecia (male) in China; and (ii) the 
Phase II clinical study of GT20029 on Acne Vulgaris in China.

The Board confirms that (i) the Company will use cashflow from its cosmetic business 
(including sales of the Product) to fund its medical/biological application and development; 
and (ii) the Company will remain consistent with its Listing Business.
The Directors have reviewed the Group’s cash flow projection covering a period of not less 
than twelve months from 31 December 2025. Having considered, among others, that (i) the 
Company is actively negotiated with several banks for the bank facilities which is expected 
to be granted and withdrawn by first quarter of 2026; and (ii) the Company is actively 
seeking equity financing and has been in negotiation with certain potential investors for 
subscribing to the Company’s new shares, the Directors are of the view that the Company 
has sufficient financial resources and liquidity to fund its operations, R&D and the 
commercialization preparations of its core products.
