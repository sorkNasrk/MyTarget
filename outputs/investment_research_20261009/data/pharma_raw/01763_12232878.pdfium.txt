ENTERING INTO THE SHAREHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT
On 3 July 2026, the Company entered into the Shareholders’ Agreement with CNNC Yuanke 
in respect of the establishment of the joint venture company, CNNC Fuyuan. Pursuant to 
the Shareholders’ Agreement, the registered capital of CNNC Fuyuan is RMB10 million, of 
which, RMB6 million will be contributed by the Company in cash, and RMB4 million will be 
contributed by CNNC Yuanke in cash. Upon the establishment of CNNC Fuyuan, the Company 
and CNNC Yuanke will hold 60% and 40% equity interests in CNNC Fuyuan, respectively, and 
CNNC Fuyuan will become a non-wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company.
IMPLICATIONS OF THE LISTING RULES
As at the date of this announcement, CNNC directly and indirectly holds approximately 73.83% 
of the Company’s total issued share capital through CIAE, NPIC, CNNC Fund and CNNC 
404, and is the controlling Shareholder and a connected person of the Company. CIAE is a 
public institute controlled and managed by CNNC, and CNNC Yuanke is a wholly-owned 
subsidiary of CIAE. Therefore, CNNC Yuanke is an associate of CNNC and a connected person 
of the Company under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules. Accordingly, the entering into the 
Shareholders’ Agreement and the transaction contemplated thereunder constitute a connected 
transaction of the Company.
As the highest applicable percentage ratio calculated under the Listing Rules in respect of 
the transaction under the Shareholders’ Agreement exceeds 0.1% but is less than 5%, such 
transaction is subject to the reporting and announcement requirements but exempt from 
Shareholders’ approval requirement under Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules.

REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF ENTERING INTO THE SHAREHOLDERS’
AGREEMENT TO ESTABLISH CNNC FUYUAN
The Company is a specialized company of CNNC in the nuclear technology application industry, 
and our entering into the Shareholders’ Agreement to establish CNNC Fuyuan, an accelerator 
equipment company, is an active expansion into the upstream industrial chain, representing a 
crucial step for the Company to coordinate the source of investment and improve its industrial 
chain control capabilities, thus achieving key milestones in strengthening and complementing the 
industrial chain of the nuclear technology application industry.
Building upon radiation technology, CNNC Fuyuan will continuously enrich its product pipeline to 
create a product matrix that fully covers industrial and medical applications, spans the full range of 
high-, mid-, and low-end segments, and supports multiple energy levels and multiple scenarios. By 
continuously implementing the above products in real-world or demonstration scenarios, it aims to 
become a leading provider of comprehensive solutions in the field of radiation application, thereby 
contributing new strength to the Company.

GENERAL INFORMATION
The Company
Focusing on the two major industrial directions of nuclear healthcare and irradiation application, 
the Company is committed to providing integrated solutions for nuclear medicine and radiotherapy, 
as well as radiation technology promotion and application, forming a “6+N” industrial layout 
with six major businesses as the core, including nuclides, nuclear medicines, nuclear medical 
equipment, integrated solution for nuclear medicine, radioactive source and application, and 
irradiation application.
CNNC Yuanke
CNNC Yuanke, a wholly-owned subsidiary of CIAE, is a platform company that engages in 
the transformation of scientific and technological achievements and external equity investment. 
Leveraging CIAE’s robust comprehensive scientific and technological strength and well-developed 
nuclear scientific research devices and facilities, CNNC Yuanke is committed to providing 
all-round and one-stop services for CNNC and its member units, as well as domestic and 
foreign enterprises and public institutions, and universities, such as transformation of scientific 
and technological achievements, incubation of innovative enterprises, investment consulting 
and management, operation and management of state-owned assets, sharing of scientific and 
technological resources and facilities in nuclear-related fields, and technology transfer and export. 
The ultimate beneficial owner of CNNC Yuanke is the State-owned Assets Supervision and 
Administration Commission of the State Council.
