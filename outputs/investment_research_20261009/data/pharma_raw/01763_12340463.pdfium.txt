POSTPONEMENT OF THE EGM AND DELAY IN DESPATCH OF THE CIRCULAR
As disclosed in the Interim Results Announcement, the EGM was scheduled to be held on 
Tuesday, 29 September 2026 for the purpose of considering and, if thought fit, approving the 2026 
Interim Dividend, the Proposed Transaction and other resolutions. Additionally, as disclosed in the 
Transaction Announcement, a circular setting out, among other things, details of the Partnership 
Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereunder, the letter from the independent board 
committee and the opinions of the Independent Financial Adviser would be despatched to the 
Shareholders within 15 business days after the date of the Transaction Announcement. The 
Company hereby announces that, as the Company requires additional time to finalise certain 
information to be included in the circular and to coordinate the arrangements for the meeting, the 
EGM will be postponed to 10:00 a.m. on Wednesday, 21 October 2026, and the circular containing, 
among other things, (i) further information relating to the 2026 Interim Dividend and the Proposed 
Transaction; (ii) the opinions of the independent financial adviser to the independent board 
committee and the independent Shareholders in respect of the Proposed Transaction; (iii) the letter 
from the independent board committee setting out its opinions and recommendations in respect 
of the Proposed Transaction; and (iv) the notice of the EGM, together with other information 
required under the Listing Rules, is expected to be despatched to the Shareholders on or before 
29 September 2026. As set out in the Interim Results Announcement, the book closure period for 
ascertaining the entitlement of the Shareholders of the Company to attend and vote at the EGM (and 
in the present case, the postponed EGM) remains unchanged.
CHANGE OF PERIODS OF CLOSURE OF REGISTER OF MEMBERS, RECORD DATE 
AND PAYMENT DATE FOR THE INTERIM DIVIDEND
As disclosed in the Interim Results Announcement, the Board has resolved to recommend to 
Shareholders the declaration of an interim dividend for the six months ended 30 June 2026. The 
2026 Interim Dividend was originally payable to the Shareholders whose names appeared on the 
register of members of the Company on Monday, 12 October 2026, and was originally scheduled 
to be paid before 27 November 2026. For the purpose of ascertaining the Shareholders’ entitlement 
to receive the 2026 Interim Dividend, the register of members of the Company was originally 
scheduled to be closed from Wednesday, 7 October 2026 to Monday, 12 October 2026 (both days 
inclusive). The 2026 Interim Dividend is subject to the review and approval of the Shareholders at 
the EGM.
The Company hereby announces that the 2026 Interim Dividend will be rescheduled to be paid 
before Friday, 18 December 2026 due to the postponement of the EGM. The register of members 
of the Company will be closed from Tuesday, 27 October 2026 to Friday, 30 October 2026 (both 
days inclusive). The Shareholders whose names appear on the register of members of the Company 
on Friday, 30 October 2026 will be entitled to receive the 2026 Interim Dividend. For the purpose 
of being qualified as the Shareholders to receive the 2026 Interim Dividend, the Shareholders must 
lodge all transfers documents with the Company’s H Share registrar, Computershare Hong Kong 
Investor Services Limited at Shops 1712-1716, 17/F, Hopewell Centre, 183 Queen’s Road East, 
Wan Chai, Hong Kong (for holders of H Shares) or the Company’s registered office at 66 Changwa 
Middle Street, Haidian District, China (for holders of Domestic Shares) for registration no later 
than 4:30 p.m. on Monday, 26 October 2026.
