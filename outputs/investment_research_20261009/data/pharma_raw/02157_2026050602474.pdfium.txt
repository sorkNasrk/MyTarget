DIS-APPLICATION OF RULES 18A.09 TO 18A.11 OF 
THE LISTING RULES AND REMOVAL OF STOCK MARKER
Lepu Biopharma Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) is pleased to announce that following an application 
to The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the “Stock Exchange”) pursuant to Rule 18A.12 
of the Rules Governing the Listing of Securities on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited 
(the “Listing Rules”), the Stock Exchange has granted approval for the dis-application of Rules 
18A.09 to 18A.11 of the Listing Rules (the “Relevant Rules”) to the Company.
As a result of the dis-application of the Relevant Rules, the “B” marker will no longer be affixed 
to the Company’s English and Chinese short name from May 11, 2026. The Company’s shares will 
be traded on the Stock Exchange under the new stock short name of “LEPU BIO” in English and 
“樂普生物” in Chinese. The stock code of the Company remains unchanged as “2157”.
REASONS FOR THE DIS-APPLICATION OF THE RELEVANT RULES
The Company is a biotech company listed under Chapter 18A of the Listing Rules, with its H 
shares (the “H Shares”) listed and traded on the Main Board of the Stock Exchange since February 
23, 2022, and subject to the application of, among others, Rules 18A.09 to 18A.11 of the Listing 
Rules.
The Company now satisfies the market capitalization/revenue test under Rule 8.05(3) of the Listing 
Rules. In particular, the Company has a trading record of at least three financial years, management 
continuity for at least the three preceding financial years and ownership continuity and control 
for at least the most recent audited financial year. The Company’s market capitalization as of 
May 6, 2026 was approximately HKD9.27 billion, which is in excess of HKD4 billion as required 
under Rule 8.05(3) of the Listing Rules. As stated in the annual results announcement of the 
Company for the year ended 31 December 2025, the Company’s total revenue for the year ended 
31 December 2025, being the most recent audited financial year, was approximately RMB934.9 
million (equivalent to approximately HKD1.07 billion), among which, revenue from sales of our 
commercialized products amounted to RMB501.0 million (equivalent to approximately HKD0.57 
billion). Therefore, the Company has been generating a revenue of more than HKD500 million 
for the most recent audited financial year as required under Rule 8.05(3) of the Listing Rules. 
Accordingly, the Company qualifies for listing under Rule 8.05(3) of the Listing Rules and the 
Stock Exchange has therefore granted its approval for the application for dis-application of the 
Relevant Rules to the Company.

EFFECTS OF THE DIS-APPLICATION OF THE RELEVANT RULES
Following the dis-application of the Relevant Rules, the “B” marker will cease to be affixed to 
the Company’s English and Chinese stock short name. There will be otherwise no effect on the 
existing share certificates in respect of the Company’s H Shares which will continue to be good 
evidence of legal title and be valid for trading, settlement and registration purposes and will not 
involve any transfer or exchange of the existing share certificates for holders of the Company’s 
H Shares. No other changes will be made to the English or Chinese stock short name of the 
Company, the existing share certificates, the board lot size, the trading currency of the Company’s 
H Shares and the share registrar and transfer offices of the Company following the dis-application 
of the Relevant Rules. For the purpose of this announcement and for illustrative purpose only, 
conversions of RMB to HKD are based on the exchange rate of RMB0.87504 = HKD1.00 as of 
May 6, 2026. No representation is made that any amounts in HKD or RMB can be or could have 
been converted at the relevant dates at the above rate, at any other rates, or at all.
