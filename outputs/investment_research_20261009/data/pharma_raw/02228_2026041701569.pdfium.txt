Notice is hereby given that the annual general meeting (the “Annual General Meeting”) of

ORDINARY RESOLUTIONS
1. To receive the audited consolidated financial statements and the reports of the directors
and auditor of the Company for the year ended 31 December 2025.
2. (a) To re-elect Dr. Ma Jian as an executive director of the Company;
(b) To re-elect Dr. Lai Lipeng as an executive director of the Company;
(c) To re-elect Mr. Law Cheuk Kin Stephen as an independent non-executive director
of the Company; and
(d) To authorise the board of directors of the Company to fix the remuneration of the
directors of the Company.
3. To re-appoint PricewaterhouseCoopers as auditor of the Company and to authorize the
board of directors of the Company to fix their remuneration.
