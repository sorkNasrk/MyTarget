VOLUNTARY ANNOUNCEMENT –
THE APPROVAL OF NEW DRUG APPLICATION FOR ONGERICIMAB 
INJECTION
This announcement is made by Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海君實生物醫藥科技
股份有限公司) (the “Company”) on a voluntary basis. Reference is also made to the overseas 
regulatory announcement of the Company dated 13 October 2024.
The board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of the Company is pleased to announce 
that, as informed by the website of the National Medical Products Administration (the “NMPA”), 
the NMPA approved the new drug application (the “NDA”) for ongericimab injection (recombinant 
human anti-PCSK9 monoclonal antibody injection* (重組人源化抗 PCSK9 單克隆抗體注射液, 
trade name being JUNSHIDA (君適達®)), submitted by the Company for the treatment of adult 
patients with primary hypercholesterolemia (non-familial) and mixed dyslipidemia, making it the 
Company’s fifth commercialized product. The relevant information is now announced as follows:
ABOUT ONGERICIMAB INJECTION
Drug name: Recombinant human anti-PCSK9 monoclonal antibody injection
Application matter: Registration of Domestic Production of Pharmaceutical Product
Acceptance Nos.: CXSS2300023, CXSS2300025
Applicant: Shanghai Junshi Biosciences Co., Ltd.* (上海君實生物醫藥科技股份有限公司)
Specification: 150 mg (1 ml) in a single dose (pre-filled syringe), 150 mg (1 ml) in a single dose 
(autoinjector)
Indication: On the basis of dietary control, in combination with statins or statins and ezetimibe 
for the treatment of adult patients with primary hypercholesterolemia (non-familial) and mixed 
dyslipidemia, who fail to achieve low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) goals after receiving 
moderate or higher doses of statins treatment.

According to the Chinese Guidelines for Lipid Management (2023), cardiovascular disease is 
the leading cause of death among urban and rural residents in China, with a predominant focus 
on atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (“ASCVD”). The rise of low-density lipoprotein 
cholesterol (“LDL-C”) levels is a dangerous factor in causing an ASCVD. Reducing LDL-C 
levels can significantly lower the incidence of ASCVD and the risk of death. Although the current 
statin-based lipid-lowering treatment can dramatically lower LDL-C levels and ASCVD risk, 
the LDL-C lipid-lowering compliance rate remains worrying amongst people with high risk of 
ASCVD. In particular, the LDL-C compliance rate is significantly lower for patients at ultra-high/
extremely high risk of ASCVD. As a result, there remains a relatively large unmet medical need 
for lipid-lowering treatments.
The approval is mainly based on two multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 
Phase III clinical studies (JS002-003 (NCT04781114) and JS002-006 (NCT05532800).
In particular, the JS002-003 study, for which Academician Han Yaling from the General Hospital 
of the Northern Theater Command of the People’s Liberation Army of China*(中國人民解放軍
北部戰區總醫院) served as the principal investigator, aimed to assess the efficacy and safety of 
subcutaneous ongericimab injection for the treatment of patients with primary hypercholesterolemia 
and mixed dyslipidemia in China, enrolling a total of 806 patients. The study results were first 
announced at the American Heart Association (AHA) Scientific Sessions 2023 and fully published 
in the Journal of the American Heart Association (JAHA, IF: 6.106), the AHA’s official journal, 
in May 2024. The JS002-003 study results demonstrated that compared to placebo, ongericimab 
administered as a subcutaneous injection at a dose of 150 mg once every 2 weeks (Q2W) or 300 
mg once every 4 weeks (Q4W) could significantly reduce LDL-C levels by more than 60% and 
invariably maintain stable decreases during the 52 weeks of treatment. The other blood lipid 
parameters, including non-high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (non-HDL-C), apolipoprotein B 
(ApoB) and total cholesterol (TC) are also significantly improved. Ongericimab demonstrated a 
favorable safety profile with no new safety signals identified.
The JS002-006 study, for which Professor Zhao Shuiping from the Second Xiangya Hospital of 
Central South University*(中南大學湘雅二醫院) served as the principal investigator, aimed to 
assess the effectiveness and safety of subcutaneous ongericimab injections using two drug delivery 
devices, a pre-filled syringe (“PFS”) and an autoinjector (“AI”), for the treatment of patients 
with primary hypercholesterolemia and mixed hyperlipidemia, enrolling a total of 255 patients. 
The study results were fully published in the Nutrition Metabolism And Cardiovascular Diseases 
(NMCD), an international academic journal in the field of endocrinology and metabolism, in May 
2024. The results showed that the administration of ongericimab using PFS and AI demonstrated 
significant lipid-lowering effects. Compared to placebo, patients receiving the ongericimab 
treatment at a dose of 150mg Q2W for 12 weeks saw a significant reduction in LDL-C levels of 
over 70% (72.7% in the PFS group and 71.1% in the AI group), with a favorable safety profile.
In April 2023, the NDAs for ongericimab were accepted by the NMPA for two indications 
(including the currently approved indication). Due to our strategic business realignment, the 
Company initiated the application for the withdrawal of the NDA for the indication of homozygous 
familial hypercholesterolemia in adults or adolescents aged 12 or above. In April 2024, the 
two supplemental NDAs for ongericimab were accepted by the NMPA, for the treatment of (1) 
heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia; and (2) primary hypercholesterolemia and mixed 
dyslipidemia in which statins are not tolerated or contraindicated (as a single agent).
