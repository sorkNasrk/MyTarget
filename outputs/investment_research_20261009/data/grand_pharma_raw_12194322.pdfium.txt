CONTINUING CONNECTED TRANSACTIONS
The Board announces that on 8 June 2026, the Group entered into the Huadong Medicine Supply 
Agreement, pursuant to which Grand Pharm (China) or its related companies shall supply 
pharmaceutical preparations, raw materials and related services to Huadong Medicine or its 
related companies.
To the best of the Directors’ knowledge having made all reasonable enquiries, as at the date of 
this announcement:
(a) China Grand is interested in 1,896,044,240 Shares, representing approximately 53.42% 
of the total issued share capital of the Company and is thus a controlling shareholder of 
the Company; and
(b) China Grand controls the exercise of 30% or more of the voting power at the general 
meeting of Huadong Medicine, and thus Huadong Medicine is an associate of China 
Grand.
Accordingly, Huadong Medicine is a connected person of the Company under Chapter 14A of 
the Listing Rules. Hence, the transactions contemplated under the Huadong Medicine Supply 
Agreement constitute continuing connected transactions on the part of the Company.

BACKGROUND
From 1 January 2026 up to the date of this announcement, the Group has supplied pharmaceutical 
preparations, raw materials and related services to Huadong Medicine and its related companies 
with an aggregate amount of approximately RMB10,000,000.
With a view to continuing the above arrangements with Huadong Medicine which are in alignment 
with the Group’s business development, the Group entered into the Huadong Medicine Supply 
Agreement.
The details of the Huadong Medicine Supply Agreement are set out below.

INFORMATION ABOUT THE GROUP AND HUADONG MEDICINE
The Group (including Grand Pharm (China)) is principally engaged in the research and 
development, manufacture and sales of pharmaceutical preparations and medical devices, bio￾technology products and health products, specialized raw materials and other products in the PRC.
Huadong Medicine and its subsidiaries are principally engaged in the manufacture and sale of 
pharmaceutical products.
REASONS AND BENEFITS FOR ENTERING INTO THE CONTINUING CONNECTED 
TRANSACTIONS
The Group will sell and/or supply to Huadong Medicine various pharmaceutical preparations, such 
as cerebro-cardiovascular medicines and antibiotics, certain pharmaceutical intermediates (which 
are chemicals and raw materials used for the production of its products), and related services.
As Huadong Medicine has established its own sales networks and retail outlets in many cities 
covering over 10 provinces in the PRC, the Directors (including the independent non-executive 
Directors) are of the view that the entering into of the Huadong Medicine Supply Agreement will 
provide the Group with stable sales channels and a solid demand base, allowing the Group to fully 
utilize the existing sales networks of Huadong Medicine to continuously promote the brand of the 
Group’s products. The Group has been supplying pharmaceutical preparations to Huadong 
Medicine or its related companies since 2012, and the entering into of the Huadong Medicine 
Supply Agreement can also ensure long-term and stable business development and help maintain 
market recognition continuity.
In view of the above, the Directors (including the independent non-executive Directors) consider 
that the entering into of the Huadong Medicine Supply Agreement is in the ordinary and usual 
course of business of the Group, and that the terms and conditions of the Huadong Medicine 
Supply Agreement are determined on an arm's length basis among the relevant parties, are fair and 
reasonable and in the interests of the Company and the Shareholders as a whole.
INTERNAL CONTROL MEASURES
To ensure that the terms of the continuing connected transactions under the Huadong Medicine 
Supply Agreement are fair and reasonable and that the transactions are conducted on normal or 
better commercial terms, the Group has adopted the following internal control measures:
1. To determine the market prices of raw materials, the sales department will extract 
information from the Group’s internal sales records to determine the quarterly unit price 
range of products of comparable quality, quantity, and specifications supplied by the Group 
to independent third parties prior to the proposed sale to Huadong Medicine. If no such 
sales records exist, the sales department will use the three most recent sales records of 
products of comparable quality, quantity, and specifications sold to independent third 
parties as a reference price range for sales to Huadong Medicine. The Group will ensure 
that the unit price charged to Huadong Medicine will not be lower than the upper limit of 
the aforementioned price range. The final unit price charged to Huadong Medicine is 
subject to approval and confirmation by the management of the sales department;

2. The financial manager of the Group will (a) monitor the transaction amounts on a monthly

3. The auditors and independent non-executive Directors of the Company will conduct an 
annual review of the continuing connected transactions under the Huadong Medicine 
Supply Agreement and provide annual confirmations to ensure that the transactions are 
conducted in accordance with the terms of the agreement, normal commercial terms, and 
relevant pricing policies, pursuant to Rules 14A.55 and 14A.56 of the Listing Rules; and
4. The Board will continue to review the Company's internal control system and its 
effectiveness on an annual basis.
